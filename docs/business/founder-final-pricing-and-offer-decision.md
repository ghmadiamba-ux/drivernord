## The Confirmed Pilot Model

### What is being offered to pilot companies

| Element | Decision |
|---------|----------|
| Offer name | Pilotleverans (not called "Package 1" or "gratis" externally) |
| Cost to pilot company | **Utan startkostnad** (zero upfront cost) |
| Language | Never "gratis", never "0 kr", never "gratis pilotperiod" |
| What is delivered | A quality-assured shortlist (kortlist) of matched driver candidates |
| Maximum pilot companies | 5 (scarcity framing: "max 5 utvalda pilotföretag") |
| What DriverNord asks in return | 1. Snabb återkoppling (quick feedback — a few sentences, a call) |
| | 2. Rätten att nämna samarbetet anonymt i DriverNords etablering |
| | 3. No logo, no named case study, no verbatim quote without separate approval |
| Driver data sharing | Only with driver consent. Personal contact information (name, phone, email) delivered in shortlist after consent is collected per candidate |
| Payment trigger | None during pilot phase |
| Duration of pilot framing | Until 5 pilots are completed or 5 companies have responded |

## Post-Pilot Commercial Terms

### Kortlistpaket — primary commercial product

| Element | Value |
|---------|-------|
| Product name | Kortlistpaket |
| Price | **15,000 SEK** |
| What is included | Qualified shortlist of 3–5 matched drivers per active need |
| Payment trigger | Delivery of shortlist (not hire) |
| Payment timing | Invoice on delivery; payment within 7 business days |
| Pilot-to-commercial transition | Explained after pilot: "Nu övergår vi till ordinarie villkor — 15,000 SEK per kortlistleverans" |

### Why payment on shortlist delivery, not on hire

- Hiring decisions are outside DriverNord's control
- Post-pilot: employer has seen quality; fee is predictable
- Keeps DriverNord's cash flow predictable
- Avoids misaligned incentives (chasing employers for hire confirmations)
- Allows the employer to budget without uncertainty

### Do not reveal the 15,000 SEK price in first contact

Price before relationship = resistance. The first email has one job: get a reply. The 15,000 SEK price is mentioned:
1. When the employer asks directly ("vad kostar det efter piloten?")
2. In the post-pilot debrief, framed with the cost-comparison anchor:
   - Traditional recruitment agency: 63,000–113,000 SEK per hire
   - Bemanning: 40–60 SEK/hour ongoing
   - DriverNord Kortlistpaket: 15,000 SEK per shortlist, regardless of hire outcome
3. Never before the employer has seen a delivered shortlist

## What DriverNord Is Not

These distinctions must be preserved in all external communication:

| DriverNord IS | DriverNord IS NOT |
|---------------|------------------|
| A matching service (matchningstjänst) | A staffing agency (bemanningsföretag) |
| A shortlist delivery service | A job board (jobbtavla) |
| A flat-fee service (per shortlist) | An hourly billing model |
| Paid on delivery | Paid on hire (not a success-fee model at this stage) |
| Consent-based (drivers chose to register) | Headhunting or cold sourcing of drivers |

## Quality Assurance Language

At V1 (current phase), credential verification is self-reported. Use correct language:

| Do say | Do NOT say |
|--------|-----------|
| "Kvalitetsgranskat" (quality-reviewed) | "Verifierat" without qualification |
| "Självdeklarerat CE-körkort" | "Verifierat CE-körkort" (we don't verify against Transportstyrelsen) |
| "Granskad profil" | "Godkänd av myndighet" |
| "Föraren har själv anmält sig och godkänt kontakt" | "Vi garanterar körkortets giltighet" |

A DPA (Data Processing Agreement) must be signed with each company before any shortlist with personal driver data is delivered.

## FOMO Framing (use in emails)

"Vi arbetar just nu med max 5 utvalda pilotföretag under vår etableringsfas. Till dessa levererar vi en första Pilotleverans utan startkostnad."

**Why this works:**
- "Max 5" creates scarcity without being false (it is actually 5)
- "Utvalda" signals that the company was chosen, not mass-contacted
- "Under vår etableringsfas" is honest — DriverNord is establishing itself
- "Utan startkostnad" is the offer, stated clearly as a condition of timing

Once the 5 pilots are completed: ordinary commercial terms apply. This is the correct framing — not "special promotion" but "phase transition."
