# Driver Personas and Pain Points

**Date:** 2026-05-16
**Purpose:** Define the real people DriverNord is trying to reach. Every piece of content must speak to at least one of these personas. This document is loaded into the Creative Agent's context for every generation task.

---

## How to Use This Document

Before generating any content, the agent must:
1. Select the primary persona being targeted
2. Identify which pain point the content addresses
3. Verify that the hook speaks to that pain point in language this persona would actually use
4. Confirm that the content does not accidentally alienate the other personas

## Persona 1 — Erfarenheten CE (Primary)

**Name for reference:** The Experienced CE Driver

### Profile

A CE-licensed driver (truck + trailer, full combination) with at least 3 years of professional driving experience. Based in or near Stockholm or Mälardalen. Valid YKB (professional competence certificate). Probably has a driver card. Has worked for 1–3 different transport companies. May currently be employed but open to change, or may be between jobs.

**Age range:** 30–52
**Gender:** Predominantly male, not exclusively
**Language:** Swedish, possibly with a migration background — copy must be in simple, clear Swedish
**Digital behavior:** Uses Facebook regularly. Moderate Instagram use. Not on LinkedIn professionally. Member of one or more truck driver Facebook groups.

### Work Situation

- Currently employed full-time, but considering a change (low pay, bad routes, management problems)
- Or: Just finished a contract/assignment and looking for the next one
- Or: Driving as a sole trader (enskild firma) and wants better client access
- Or: Recently YKB-renewed and actively looking

### Pain Points (in order of intensity)

1. **"Alla ringer inte tillbaka."** Transport companies are hard to reach. You send applications to 5 companies, hear back from 1. The process is opaque and slow.
2. **"Jag vill inte registrera mig på fem bemanningsbolag."** Has had bad experiences with staffing agencies — feels like a commodity, not a professional. Hates being "sold" to companies without knowing which ones.
3. **"Lönerna varierar för mycket och det är svårt att veta vad man är värd."** Salary transparency is low. Hard to know if you're being underpaid.
4. **"Jag vill köra det jag är bra på."** Experienced CE drivers have preferences — international, regional, bulk, tanker, hazmat. Generic job boards don't filter for this.
5. **"Jag vill inte börja om med ett nytt ställe och lura mig."** Switching jobs is risky. New employer may be worse than current one.

### Motivations (what pulls them toward registering)

- Being found by companies that match what they actually want to do
- Not having to search actively — "let the right offer come to me"
- Control over when their profile is shared: consent before contact
- Speed — registration should take minutes, not a full CV process
- Trust signals: clear about what DriverNord is (not a staffing agency)

### What Resonates in Copy

- Direct, no-nonsense tone. No corporate language.
- Concrete specificity: "CE med YKB" not "qualified drivers"
- Practical benefit framing: "Slipp söka på 10 ställen" (Skip searching 10 places)
- Control and autonomy messaging: "Du väljer om du vill gå vidare"
- Short sentences. Active verbs.
- Swedish without anglicisms where possible

### What Does NOT Work

- Phrases like "spännande möjligheter" (exciting opportunities) — sounds like a recruiter
- Vague promises: "Hitta ditt drömjobb" (Find your dream job) — not credible
- Staffing agency language: "Vi hjälper dig att ta nästa steg i din karriär"
- Anything implying a database of hundreds of companies (false claim)
- "AI-powered" or tech-forward language — this persona doesn't care about the tech

### Recruitment Timing

Most receptive when:
- YKB is due for renewal (within 90 days)
- Seasonal transitions (autumn — Q4 transport demand peaks)
- After a bad week at current job
- When a colleague mentions DriverNord ("social proof trigger")
- January — many reconsider their jobs at the new year

## Persona 2 — Flexibeln C (Secondary)

**Name for reference:** The Flexible C Driver

### Profile

A C-licensed driver (rigid truck, no trailer) who may also be working other jobs alongside driving. Often works for smaller local transport companies — grocery distribution, last-mile logistics, refuse collection, or municipal vehicles. May have a D license as well. Younger than the CE persona on average, or has been driving C for most of their career and never pursued CE.

**Age range:** 25–45
**Gender:** Mixed — slightly more diverse than CE persona
**Language:** Swedish; more likely than CE persona to have grown up in Sweden
**Digital behavior:** Facebook and Instagram. More likely to engage with Instagram than the CE persona.

### Work Situation

- Working C routes but wants more flexibility or better-matched work
- Looking for part-time or extra work alongside main employment
- Recently licensed and building experience
- Working through a staffing agency but unhappy with the terms

### Pain Points

1. **"Jag kör redan heltid men vill ha extra inkomst ibland."** Looking for flexible extra work without a full job change.
2. **"C-uppdrag är svåra att hitta utan att gå via bemanningsbolag."** Few direct channels for C drivers at smaller companies.
3. **"Jag vet inte om jag ska ta CE eller inte — är det värt det?"** Some C drivers are considering CE training but want to understand demand first.
4. **"Min chef tar inte hänsyn till min tillgänglighet."** Wants more control over working hours.

### What Resonates in Copy

- Flexibility and control messaging
- Part-time / extra work framing
- Short registration time
- No obligation to accept anything

### What Does NOT Work

- Heavy CE-focused language that makes them feel under-qualified
- Full-time commitment framing
- Complicated sign-up processes

## Persona 3 — D/Bussföraren (Tertiary)

**Name for reference:** The D-Licensed Bus Driver

### Profile

A D-licensed driver working in public transport (Storstockholms Lokaltrafik contractors) or tourist/charter bus operations. Often has very stable employment but faces different issues: shift assignments they dislike, limited geographical flexibility, or wanting to transition to truck driving. May have CE or be considering it.

**Age range:** 28–55
**Digital behavior:** Facebook, limited Instagram

### Pain Points

1. **"Jag kör buss men vill prova lastbil."** Wants to explore CE/truck opportunities without abandoning bus work.
2. **"Skiftarbetet passar inte min familj längre."** Bus shifts are incompatible with family life — early mornings, late nights.
3. **"Jag vill veta vad jag är värd på lastbilssidan."** Unsure about salary parity between bus and truck.

### Content priority for D drivers

**Low — build CE and C content first.** Reactivate D persona targeting only after CE content has been validated and if Supabase data shows D-licensed drivers registering. Do not dilute early messaging by addressing all three licenses simultaneously.

## The Passive Audience — "Inte nu, men kanske"

Beyond active job-seekers, there is a large segment of qualified drivers who are not currently looking but might register if the friction is low enough and the value clear enough.

### Characteristics

- Currently employed, stable, not dissatisfied enough to search
- Would consider a change if the right offer appeared
- Has never used a service like DriverNord
- Skeptical of "recruitment" services (bad experiences with cold calls or spam)

### What converts passive drivers

- "Registrera dig utan att binda upp dig" (Register without committing)
- "Inga bemanningsbolag, inga påtryckningar" (No staffing agencies, no pressure)
- "Du hör av dig om du vill — vi jagar dig inte" (You reach out when you want — we don't chase you)
- Future availability framing: "Söker du inte nu men om 3–6 månader?"

This segment is large but converts slowly. The content strategy should serve them at awareness level without trying to force immediate action.

## Cross-Persona Copy Principles

These rules apply regardless of which persona is targeted:

| Do | Don't |
|----|-------|
| Write in first or second person ("Du som kör CE") | Write in corporate third person ("Förare sökes") |
| Name the license specifically ("CE med YKB") | Be generic ("transportproffs") |
| Describe a concrete benefit ("slipp maila 10 företag") | Describe abstract value ("optimera din karriär") |
| Acknowledge real frustrations ("Rekryteringsprocesser tar för lång tid") | Promise perfection ("Hitta jobbet direkt") |
| Respect their experience ("Du vet vad du är värd") | Talk down to them ("Vi hjälper dig lyckas") |
| Mention consent explicitly ("Du godkänner alltid innan vi delar din profil") | Be vague about data use |
| Keep sentences short | Use subordinate clauses and industry jargon |
