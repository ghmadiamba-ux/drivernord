# 04 — Bemanning Pricing and Commercial Terms

**Research date:** 2026-05-12  
**Scope:** How Swedish driver bemanning is priced — the cost formula from driver wage to client invoice rate — and what commercial terms look like in practice.  
**Note:** Agencies do not publish rates. All figures below are derived from wage data, employer cost structure, industry margin benchmarks, and published Swedish employment cost sources.

---

## 1. The Cost Structure of Driver Bemanning

A staffing agency employing a CE-licensed driver and billing a client must cover all of the following in its invoice rate:

### 1.1 Base Wage — Driver

Per Transportavtalet (the collective agreement for road transport, 2023–2025 and current):

| Driver profile | Monthly gross wage | Hourly gross wage |
|----------------|-------------------|-------------------|
| Entry-level C (no YKB — unlikely in professional hire) | ~25,500 SEK | ~147 SEK |
| C + YKB (standard) | ~30,000–32,000 SEK | ~173–185 SEK |
| CE + YKB (standard, Stockholm region) | ~32,500–35,000 SEK | ~188–202 SEK |
| CE + YKB senior (5+ years, Stockholm) | ~35,000–38,000 SEK | ~202–219 SEK |
| D (bus) + YKB | ~32,000–35,000 SEK | ~185–202 SEK |

**Reference hourly wage used in cost model below:** 200 SEK (mid-range CE + YKB, Stockholm)

Night supplement (OB-tillägg): +25–50% for nights (23:00–06:00), weekends. Transportavtalet specifies exact rates. A CE night driver effectively costs 250–300 SEK/hour base wage.

### 1.2 Employer Social Costs (Arbetsgivaravgift)

| Component | Rate |
|-----------|------|
| Arbetsgivaravgift (standard, born 1957–) | 31.42% of gross wage |
| Semesterlön (vacation pay) | 12% minimum (25 days = 10.77% + semesterlönetillägg) |
| Sjuklöneansvar (sick pay, days 2–14) | ~3–5% of payroll (risk-based; 1 in 10 drivers sick 14 days/year) |
| Pension — SAF-LO (Fora) | ~4.5% of gross wage |
| Insurance — TFA/TGL/AGS/AGB (Fora) | ~1.5–2% of gross wage |
| Total employer on-cost rate | **~52–55% above gross wage** |

**Cost per productive hour at 200 SEK/hour base:**

```
Base hourly wage:                      200 SEK
Arbetsgivaravgift (31.42%):             63 SEK
Semesterlön (12%):                      24 SEK
Sick pay risk (4%):                      8 SEK
Fora pension + insurance (6%):          12 SEK
─────────────────────────────────────
Total employment cost per hour:        307 SEK
```

### 1.3 Billable Hours vs. Paid Hours

A full-time driver works 1,840–1,992 paid hours per year (249 working days × 8 hours = 1,992 nominal, minus sick leave ~5% = ~1,892 productive).

The agency bills for hours the driver is deployed to a client. Hours between assignments (bench time) are a cost with no revenue. Agencies managing their pools well aim for **85–92% utilization** on permanent driver pools.

At 88% utilization, the effective cost per billable hour increases:

```
Total annual employment cost (200 SEK base, full year):
  1,992 hours × 307 SEK/h = 611,544 SEK/year
  
At 88% utilization (1,753 billable hours):
  611,544 ÷ 1,753 = 349 SEK/h break-even (before agency overhead)
```

### 1.4 Agency Overhead and Margin

| Component | Typical % of billable revenue |
|-----------|-------------------------------|
| Staff (recruiters, account managers) | 8–12% |
| Office/admin/IT | 3–5% |
| Marketing / driver acquisition | 2–4% |
| Bad debt / payment risk | 1–2% |
| Total operating overhead | 14–23% |
| Target EBIT margin | 5–10% |
| Total overhead + margin | **19–33%** |

This is applied on top of break-even employment cost:

```
Break-even per billable hour:  349 SEK
Agency overhead + margin (25%): 87 SEK
─────────────────────────────
Invoice rate floor:             436 SEK/hour
```

## 2. Market Invoice Rate Ranges

Based on the cost model above and cross-referenced with industry benchmarks and general Swedish staffing data:

| Driver type | Estimated client invoice rate (SEK/hour) |
|-------------|------------------------------------------|
| C + YKB, standard hours | 360–420 SEK/h |
| CE + YKB, standard hours | 400–480 SEK/h |
| CE + YKB, night shift (OB applied) | 500–620 SEK/h |
| CE + YKB, weekend work | 520–640 SEK/h |
| D (bus) + YKB | 400–500 SEK/h |
| Short-notice premium (same-day call) | +20–30% above standard |
| Holiday/VAB coverage | At least 1.5× standard rate |

**Published range from industry sources:** General staffing invoice rates for skilled labor in Sweden range 250–650 SEK/hour depending on competence and contract length (Bemanningrekrytering.se). Driver staffing sits in the 360–620 SEK range.

## 3. Markup Calculation (Simplified)

The "markup" in staffing is defined as the multiple of driver gross wage charged to client:

```
Markup = (Invoice rate ÷ Driver hourly gross wage)

Example: 450 SEK invoice ÷ 200 SEK driver wage = 2.25× (125% markup on wage)
```

This looks high but is not profit — it covers employer costs, bench time, overhead, and a thin margin.

**Common markup range for Swedish driver staffing:** 1.9× to 2.8× driver gross hourly wage.

## 4. Cancellation Terms and Contract Flexibility

Staffing agencies typically operate under:

### 4.1 Client-Side (åkeri) Commercial Terms

| Term | Typical practice |
|------|-----------------|
| Minimum booking | 4 hours (half-day) |
| Notice for planned shifts | 24–48 hours |
| Short-notice/same-day call | Available at premium (+20–30%) |
| Minimum weekly commitment | Some agencies require 20–40 hrs/week minimum for dedicated driver |
| Contract length | No minimum for spot/project; monthly/quarterly for dedicated supply |
| Cancellation notice | 24 hours for most bookings; 48–72 hours for planned weeks |
| Payment terms | Net-30 standard; some agencies require Net-15 for new clients |
| Credit check | Required for new clients |

### 4.2 Key Commercial Risks for Agencies

1. **Sick leave from agency driver** — agency must replace or compensate client for no-show. This is the biggest operational risk in driver staffing.
2. **Driver poaching** — client hires the agency driver directly after 6 months. Most staffing contracts include a transfer fee (typically 2–3 months of equivalent billings or ~50,000–100,000 SEK for a driver).
3. **Seasonal demand volatility** — summer and Christmas peaks mean high utilization; Q1/Q2 slow periods mean bench cost.
4. **CE license expiry mid-contract** — agency liability if they place a driver whose license lapses.

## 5. Permanent Placement Fees (Rekrytering)

When a client wants to permanently employ a driver (not hire via staffing), recruitment agencies charge:

| Model | Typical fee |
|-------|------------|
| Contingency placement (success fee) | 15–25% of first-year annual salary |
| Retained search | Upfront fee + success fee |
| Example: CE driver at 34,000 SEK/month | 34,000 × 12 × 0.20 = 81,600 SEK placement fee |
| Guarantee period | 3–6 months (free replacement if driver leaves) |
| Trial-to-permanent via staffing | Transfer fee typically ~60,000–100,000 SEK or ~3 months billings |
