# 07 — Field Validation Interview Plan

**Research date:** 2026-05-12  
**Purpose:** Define who to interview, what to ask, and how to evaluate answers to validate DriverNord's commercial model before full launch.

---

## Overview

No amount of desk research replaces 3 phone calls with actual buyers. This plan defines the exact questions, targets, and scoring methodology needed to validate — or invalidate — DriverNord's pricing assumptions before committing to the model.

**Target: 28 interviews over 4–6 weeks.**

| Group | Count | Purpose |
|-------|-------|---------|
| SME transport owners (Segment A) | 10 | Primary buyers; validate price, pain, and urgency |
| Regional transport managers (Segment B/C) | 5 | Secondary buyers; validate larger-volume need |
| Staffing agency salespeople | 3 | Understand client buying behavior; learn incumbents' sales pitch |
| Recruitment agencies (transport-adjacent) | 3 | Validate fee structure, objections, conversion rates |
| Professional drivers (CE+YKB) | 5 | Supply-side; validate consent model and value proposition |
| Labor law / legal experts | 2 | Validate legal structure, GDPR consent design, contract risk |

---

## Interview Group 1: SME Transport Owners (10 interviews)

**Who to find:**  
- From `company_research_targets` database (Segment A, urgency_signal = repeated_ads)
- Blocket Jobb/Platsbanken: companies posting CE driver ads repeatedly
- LinkedIn: CEOs/owners of 5–30 truck åkeri in Stockholm/Mälardalen

**Approach:**  
- Cold LinkedIn message or email: "We're researching how transport companies find CE drivers. Would you take a 15-min call in exchange for our research findings?"
- Incentive: offer to share final research summary; optional Amazon voucher 300 SEK

**Interview Format:** 15–20 min phone call. Semi-structured.

---

### Questions — SME Transport Owner

#### Section A: Current Problem (5 min)

1. "How many drivers do you currently employ with CE or C license?"
2. "When was the last time you needed to hire a CE driver? How long did it take to fill?"
3. "What channels have you used to find drivers? Which worked best? Which failed?"
4. "Have you ever posted a driver job ad that got zero qualified applicants? What happened next?"
5. "On a scale of 1–10, how much of a problem is driver finding for your business right now?"

**What to extract:** Urgency level, current channels, evidence of pain and failed attempts, willingness to articulate cost.

---

#### Section B: Current Costs (5 min)

6. "Have you ever paid a recruitment agency to find a driver? What did it cost?"
7. "If a driver position is empty for 2 weeks, what does that actually cost your business?" [Probe: lost revenue? owner doing driving? overtime?]
8. "If you could hire a verified CE+YKB driver in 5 days for a flat fee, what would be a reasonable price?"
9. "Would you rather pay upfront for a shortlist of candidates, or only pay if you hire someone?"

**What to extract:** Current spend, quantified cost of vacancy, price threshold (spontaneous, before DriverNord is named), payment preference.

---

#### Section C: DriverNord Model Test (5 min)

10. "Imagine a service that shows you 3 pre-verified CE+YKB drivers in your area who are actively looking for work. Their credentials have been checked. They've agreed to be introduced to you specifically. You pay 18,000 SEK for the shortlist, regardless of whether you hire. Would you use this?"
    - If yes: "What would you need to see to trust the quality?"
    - If no: "What would need to be different for you to consider it?"

11. "What if instead you only paid 40,000 SEK flat if you actually hired one of them?"
    - Probe: "Does that feel better or worse than 18,000 for the shortlist?"

12. "What if you paid 2,500 SEK/month and could access the driver database anytime?"
    - Probe: "Would that replace your current approach to finding drivers?"

13. "What would make you NOT trust this service?"

14. "If we placed a driver and they left after 3 weeks, what would you expect to happen?"

**What to extract:** Willingness to pay at each price point, product acceptance, trust barriers, guarantee expectation.

---

#### Section D: Closing (2 min)

15. "How likely are you, on a scale of 1–10, to use a service like this in the next 6 months?"
16. "Is there anyone else in your network who has this problem regularly?"

## Interview Group 2: Regional Transport Managers (5 interviews)

**Who to find:**  
- Transport managers, fleet managers, or HR managers at 50–200 truck regional operators
- LinkedIn: "Transportchef" or "Driftchef" in Stockholm/Göteborg/Malmö regions

**Approach:** LinkedIn outreach; mention research into CE driver pipeline solutions.

**Interview Format:** 20 min. More structured than Group 1.

### Questions — Regional Transport Manager

1. "How many CE driver hires do you expect this year? Last year?"
2. "Do you have a recruitment process, or is it ad-hoc each time you need someone?"
3. "What is your current cost per driver hire (including agency fees, ad spend, onboarding)?"
4. "Have you calculated what a driver vacancy costs your business per week?"
5. "What's your biggest frustration with how the staffing agencies you use operate?"
6. "Would you use a service that sends you verified CE+YKB driver profiles on a monthly subscription basis?"
    - Probe: "What would you pay? 5,000/month? 10,000/month?"
7. "Would you pay 40,000–50,000 SEK as a success fee (only on hire) per driver, instead of 23–27% agencies charge? That's about 10% of annual salary."
8. "What is your procurement process for adding a new recruitment supplier? How long does it take?"
9. "What level of guarantee would you need for this to be worth trying?"
10. "If you could have 3 verified CE drivers presented to you within 5 days for a flat 18,000 SEK, would you sign today?"

**What to extract:** Volume, cost benchmarks, subscription vs. per-hire preference, procurement barriers, urgency signal.

## Interview Group 3: Staffing Agency Salespeople (3 interviews)

**Who to find:**  
- Account managers or sales reps at Simplex Bemanning, Veterankraft, Rekryteringsgruppen, or Submit AB
- LinkedIn "Account Manager Bemanning Transport"
- Approach as a potential customer initially; then reveal research purpose

**Interview Format:** 20 min. Aim to get intel on their pricing and client behavior.

### Questions — Staffing Agency Salespeople

1. "What is your standard invoice rate for a CE+YKB driver for a Stockholm regional distribution client?"
2. "What is the typical contract length — minimum booking, notice period?"
3. "How do clients typically find you? Cold call, referral, repeated need?"
4. "What objection do you hear most often from clients about your rates?"
5. "Have you ever lost a client because they hired a driver directly through another channel? How does that happen?"
6. "Do you offer any permanent placement service as an alternative to ongoing staffing?"
7. "What transfer fee do you charge if a client wants to permanently hire a driver they've been using through your agency?"
8. "Do clients ever complain about the quality of drivers you provide? What are the most common complaints?"
9. "What is your typical client size — SME with 5–30 trucks, or larger?"
10. "In your experience, do clients of 5–20 trucks use staffing agencies regularly, or only in emergencies?"

**What to extract:** Real invoice rates, transfer fees, typical contract terms, SME behavior, quality gap (what DriverNord can exploit).

## Interview Group 4: Recruitment Agencies (3 interviews)

**Who to find:**  
- Recruitment consultants at SV Bemanning, Lernia, Headscout, Manpower transport division
- Also: generalist agencies that have placed drivers before

**Interview Format:** 20 min professional call as a potential client.

### Questions — Recruitment Agencies

1. "What does it typically cost to recruit a CE+YKB driver through your agency?"
2. "Is it a percentage of salary or a flat fee? What percentage or amount?"
3. "How long does a typical driver search take from brief to hire?"
4. "What defines a 'qualified' candidate to you for a CE driver role?"
5. "Do you verify license class and YKB before presenting a candidate to a client?"
6. "What is your replacement guarantee — if the driver leaves in month 2, what happens?"
7. "How many CE driver searches have you done in Stockholm in the past 12 months?"
8. "What's the hardest thing about this market? Why do so many driver positions go unfilled?"
9. "Do smaller companies (5–15 trucks) typically use you, or do they find it too expensive?"
10. "If someone offered you verified CE+YKB driver leads on a pay-per-introduction basis, would that be useful?"

**What to extract:** Real fee data, time-to-hire benchmarks, verification processes, SME accessibility, and indirect validation of DriverNord's value proposition.

## Interview Group 5: CE/YKB Professional Drivers (5 interviews)

**Who to find:**  
- Facebook groups: "Chaufförer i Sverige", "CE-förare söker jobb Sverige"
- LinkedIn: CE/YKB drivers in Stockholm/Mälardalen
- Förartjänst.se, Chaffis.se registered driver community

**Approach:** "5-minute survey for CE drivers — tell us about your job search experience, we'll share results."

**Interview Format:** 10–15 min. Casual. Driver-friendly tone.

### Questions — CE/YKB Professional Drivers

1. "When you last changed jobs, how did you find your current position?"
2. "Did you register with any job platform, staffing agency, or driver community? Which one?"
3. "What is your biggest frustration about finding a good driving job?"
4. "Have you ever been presented to a company by a recruiter or agency without your knowledge? How did you feel about that?"
5. "Would you register with a platform that only shows your profile to companies you approve? (Driver-controlled visibility)"
6. "Would you use an app that lets you signal 'I'm open to new opportunities' to vetted transport companies?"
7. "What would make you trust a platform enough to share your license and YKB details?"
8. "Have you heard of Chaffis.se or Förartjänst.se? Have you registered there?"
9. "What is the most important thing to you in a new job? (pay / hours / route / company size / stability)"
10. "If a company wanted to hire you through DriverNord's service, would you prefer we asked your permission first, or is it OK to share your details proactively?"

**What to extract:** Supply-side willingness to register, consent model validation, platform trust barriers, driver value proposition.

## Interview Group 6: Labor Law / Legal Experts (2 interviews)

**Who to find:**  
- Swedish labor lawyers (arbetsrättsjurister) at Vinge, Wistrand, or smaller boutique firms
- HR legal consultants familiar with bemanningslagen, uthyrningslagen, and GDPR recruitment

**Approach:** Direct consultation fee (~2,000–5,000 SEK for 1-hour consultation).

### Questions — Legal Experts

1. "DriverNord presents verified driver candidates to åkeri companies for a placement fee. The driver is not DriverNord's employee. Is this a staffing agency (bemanningsföretag) under uthyrningslagen, or a recruitment service (rekryteringstjänst) outside that law?"
2. "What is the legal test that distinguishes placement/introduction services from regulated staffing?"
3. "Can DriverNord operate without Almega membership or any formal authorization in Sweden?"
4. "Under GDPR, what is the minimum required consent before sharing a named driver's profile (name, phone, license class, YKB date) with a transport company?"
5. "Is the Förartjänst.se model (driver-controlled consent per company) GDPR-compliant in your view?"
6. "If DriverNord's client hires a driver introduced by DriverNord, and the driver's role later becomes a 'regular' position, does DriverNord have any ongoing liability?"
7. "Can DriverNord include a clause preventing clients from sharing driver profiles with third parties, and is that enforceable?"
8. "What are the main legal risks DriverNord should be aware of from Year 1?"

**What to extract:** Legal classification of the service, compliance requirements, GDPR minimum standards, risk mitigation.

## Scoring Matrix: Business Model Viability

Each interview generates a score on 7 dimensions. Average scores across the cohort indicate model viability.

| Dimension | Scoring guide | Scale |
|-----------|--------------|-------|
| **Willingness to pay** | Did they name a price at or above DriverNord's target? (0=no, 1=below target, 2=at target, 3=above target) | 0–3 |
| **Urgency** | How quickly do they need a solution? (0=no urgency, 1=months, 2=weeks, 3=now) | 0–3 |
| **Current alternative cost** | Do they have a high-cost current solution? (0=free alternatives, 1=low cost, 2=medium cost, 3=high cost agency/vacancy) | 0–3 |
| **Trust barrier** | How easy is the trust barrier to overcome? (3=low barrier/no concern, 2=addressable, 1=significant, 0=deal-breaker) | 0–3 |
| **Legal risk** | Is the legal structure clear? (3=clean, 2=minor concerns, 1=unclear, 0=major risk) | 0–3 |
| **Operational feasibility** | Can DriverNord deliver this service reliably? (3=fully feasible, 2=mostly feasible, 1=possible, 0=not feasible) | 0–3 |
| **Speed to cash** | How quickly does this model generate revenue? (3=same day, 2=week, 1=month, 0=months+) | 0–3 |

**Maximum score per interview: 21**

| Score range | Interpretation |
|------------|----------------|
| 18–21 | Strong validation — proceed |
| 14–17 | Qualified validation — proceed with adjustments |
| 10–13 | Mixed signals — specific adjustments needed before launch |
| 7–9 | Weak validation — rethink the model |
| 0–6 | Invalidation — do not proceed without major pivot |

## How to Record Answers

**For each interview:**

1. Interviewer, date, interview type (Group 1–6)
2. Company/individual profile (anonymous or named with consent)
3. Verbatim quotes for key answers (especially willingness-to-pay statements)
4. Score on each of the 7 dimensions immediately after the call
5. Tags: [willingness-to-pay-confirmed] [price-anchor: X SEK] [objection: Y] [trust-barrier: Z]
6. Single "killer quote" from the interview — the one sentence that best represents their view

**Aggregation after 28 interviews:**

- Average score across all groups by dimension
- Distribution of spontaneous price anchors
- Most common objections (top 3)
- Most common trust barriers (top 3)
- "Deal-breaker" list (anything that appeared in 3+ interviews)
- Driver-side supply: % willing to register with consent model

**Decision gate:**  
- If average score ≥ 14 across 10+ SME owner interviews: proceed to soft launch
- If 3+ interviews mention same deal-breaker: fix it before launch
- If driver-side interviews show <60% willingness to consent: revisit database acquisition strategy
