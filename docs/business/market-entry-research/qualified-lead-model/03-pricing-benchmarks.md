# 03 — Pricing Benchmarks

**Research date:** 2026-05-12  
**Purpose:** Establish real, sourced pricing ranges for all relevant recruitment and lead models. Calculate DriverNord candidate pricing scenarios using CE/YKB driver salary assumptions.

---

## Part A: Market Benchmarks by Model Type

### A1. Subscription Access to Candidate Database

| Market | Price | Unit | Source | Reliability | Confidence |
|--------|-------|------|--------|-------------|------------|
| Sweden (Chaffis.se) | 4,995 SEK | /year + per-contract signing | Direct pricing page | 5 | High |
| Sweden (Förartjänst.se) | Undisclosed annual | /year | Company website | 4 | Medium |
| US (LinkedIn Recruiter Lite) | ~$170/month | /month (30 InMail) | Published pricing | 5 | High |
| US (Indeed Resume) | $100–$250 | Per 30–unlimited contacts | Published pricing | 5 | High |
| UK (Reed CV Search) | Variable; negotiated | /month subscription | Industry reports | 3 | Medium |
| UK (Totaljobs) | Variable; 21M CV database | /subscription | Industry reports | 3 | Medium |

**DriverNord implication:** The only fully public Swedish driver subscription benchmark is **Chaffis.se at 4,995 SEK/year**. This represents the absolute price floor for a Swedish driver database subscription. DriverNord must charge more (justified by verification quality) or offer more (AI matching, verification layer).

**Recommended DriverNord subscription range:** 5,995–12,000 SEK/year.

### A5. Success-Fee Recruitment (Percentage of Salary)

**CE/YKB driver salary assumptions:**
- Low: 32,000 SEK/month = 384,000 SEK/year
- Mid: 35,000 SEK/month = 420,000 SEK/year
- High: 38,000 SEK/month = 456,000 SEK/year

| Fee % | Low salary (384k) | Mid salary (420k) | High salary (456k) | Market comparison |
|-------|-------------------|--------------------|--------------------|-------------------|
| 10% | 38,400 SEK | 42,000 SEK | 45,600 SEK | Below market; entry offer |
| 15% | 57,600 SEK | 63,000 SEK | 68,400 SEK | UK logistics benchmark |
| 20% | 76,800 SEK | 84,000 SEK | 91,200 SEK | Sweden general mid |
| 25% | 96,000 SEK | 105,000 SEK | 114,000 SEK | Sweden general high |

**Sweden market fee benchmarks:**
- General recruitment agencies (Sweden): 23–27% of annual salary
- Logistics/transport specialist (lower end): 15–20%
- UK logistics agencies: 15–20%
- Blue-collar logistics (EU, Hiring Hub): lower end of 15–20% range

**DriverNord recommended test pricing:**  
**15% of annual salary** as success fee = **57,600–68,400 SEK** per CE+YKB driver placement.

This is:
- Below the Swedish general market (23–27%)
- At par with the UK logistics market (15–20%)
- Well below current agency staffing cost (140,000–200,000 SEK/year premium per driver vs. direct hire)
- Defensible as "specialist driver service with verified credentials"

| Market | Price | Unit | Source | Reliability | Confidence |
|--------|-------|------|--------|-------------|------------|
| UK (Schwarz & Vogel) | Published; "flat fee" | /month | Direct company page | 4 | High |
| UK (general RaaS) | £2,000–5,000 | /month per role specialty | Industry estimates | 3 | Medium |
| Claims of savings | 30–50% vs. agency fees | Comparative | Multiple sources | 3 | Medium |

**DriverNord implication:** RaaS at UK equivalent (25,000–65,000 SEK/month) is too expensive for Segment A SME. Appropriate for Segment B/C regional operators with 5–20 driver hires/year. At that volume, a monthly fee amortizes well: paying 8,000 SEK/month to hire 10 drivers/year = 9,600 SEK/hire vs. 63,000 SEK/hire success fee.

## Part B: DriverNord Candidate Pricing Scenarios

### Scenario A — Lead-Only Price (Verified driver name + contact + license confirmed)

Buyer receives: Driver name, phone, email, confirmed license class, YKB valid date, last known availability, transport domain.

| Tier | Price | Justification |
|------|-------|---------------|
| Minimum viable | 2,000 SEK/lead | 40% of a failed Blocket Jobb ad; no quality guarantee |
| Standard | 5,000 SEK/lead | Fully verified; guaranteed YKB valid; driver consented |
| Premium (same-day available) | 8,000 SEK/lead | Urgency premium; driver confirmed available this week |

**Recommended launch price: 3,500 SEK/verified introduction**

---

### Scenario B — Qualified Introduction Price

Buyer receives: Driver introduced by DriverNord with verified credentials + consent form + brief cover paragraph explaining fit.

| Level | Price |
|-------|-------|
| Standard introduction | 5,000–8,000 SEK |
| Urgent introduction (24h turnaround) | 8,000–12,000 SEK |
| Senior/specialized (ADR, Crane, specific vehicle) | 10,000–15,000 SEK |

---

### Scenario C — 3-Driver Shortlist Price

Buyer receives: 3 pre-screened, verified CE+YKB drivers with full profiles, consent obtained, availability confirmed. DriverNord presents in a structured PDF or cockpit view.

| Tier | Price | Per-driver unit cost |
|------|-------|---------------------|
| Basic shortlist | 12,000 SEK | 4,000/driver |
| Standard shortlist | 18,000 SEK | 6,000/driver |
| Premium (with interview scheduling) | 25,000 SEK | 8,333/driver |

**Recommended launch price: 15,000 SEK/shortlist of 3**

---

### Scenario D — Success Fee Placement (No hire = no fee)

Buyer pays only when driver is employed. All risk on DriverNord.

| Salary level | 10% | 12% | 15% | **Recommended (12%)** |
|-------------|-----|-----|-----|----------------------|
| Low (384k/yr) | 38,400 | 46,080 | 57,600 | **46,080 SEK** |
| Mid (420k/yr) | 42,000 | 50,400 | 63,000 | **50,400 SEK** |
| High (456k/yr) | 45,600 | 54,720 | 68,400 | **54,720 SEK** |

**Recommended: 12% of annual salary OR 50,000 SEK flat — whichever is lower.**

This positions DriverNord as significantly cheaper than incumbent agencies (23–27%) while still generating meaningful revenue per hire.

---

### Scenario E — Hybrid: Setup Fee + Success Fee

Reduces DriverNord cash flow risk while keeping client entry barrier lower than full upfront.

| Component | Amount | When |
|-----------|--------|------|
| Engagement fee | 5,000 SEK | On signed search agreement |
| Success fee | 25,000–35,000 SEK flat | When driver signs employment contract |
| **Total** | **30,000–40,000 SEK** | Split over 2–8 weeks |

This is the recommended Year 1 model for serious leads. The 5,000 SEK engagement fee filters out non-serious inquiries and covers DriverNord's initial sourcing effort.

---

### Scenario F — Monthly Subscription for SME

Buyer pays monthly; can access verified driver profiles directly; DriverNord maintains the database.

| Tier | Price | Included |
|------|-------|----------|
| Starter | 999 SEK/month | Browse 20 driver profiles; no direct contact |
| Standard | 2,500 SEK/month | Unlimited profile views; 5 contact requests/month |
| Pro | 5,000 SEK/month | Unlimited; priority matching alerts; phone support |
| Annual (Pro) | 4,995 SEK/year (= Chaffis.se parity) | Basic access; no personalized support |

**Note:** The 4,995 SEK/year Chaffis.se price point is the market floor for a Swedish driver subscription. DriverNord must justify a higher price with verified credentials or offer matching services rather than just database access.

---

### Scenario G — Monthly Subscription for Regional Operators

Buyers are Segment B/C companies needing 5–15 driver hires/year. Subscription replaces individual placement fees.

| Tier | Price | Expected hires/year | Per-hire cost |
|------|-------|---------------------|---------------|
| Regional | 8,000 SEK/month | 5–8 | 12,000–19,200 SEK/hire |
| Fleet | 15,000 SEK/month | 10–20 | 9,000–18,000 SEK/hire |
| Enterprise | 25,000 SEK/month | 20+ | ~15,000 SEK/hire |

These are well below the 50,000–84,000 SEK per-hire success fee, making subscription attractive at volume.

## Part C: Consolidated Recommended Price Ladder

```
LOWEST COMMITMENT
│
├─ Annual database subscription (Starter)   4,995–5,995 SEK/year
│   → Browse & contact drivers independently; no support
│
├─ Monthly subscription (SME Standard)      2,500 SEK/month
│   → Ongoing pipeline access + 5 contact requests/month
│
├─ Per verified introduction                3,500–8,000 SEK
│   → One-off; immediate; verified CE+YKB; driver consented
│
├─ 3-driver shortlist                       15,000 SEK
│   → Curated, screened; DriverNord presents; all verified
│
├─ Hybrid (5k engagement + success fee)     5,000 + 25,000–35,000 SEK
│   → Best for serious urgent hires; risk shared
│
├─ Success fee only                         50,000 SEK flat / 12% annual salary
│   → Zero upfront; DriverNord takes all risk; best first-sale offer
│
└─ RaaS monthly (regional operators)        8,000–25,000 SEK/month
    → Unlimited hires; for companies needing 5+ drivers/year
│
HIGHEST COMMITMENT / HIGHEST VALUE
```
