# 06 — DriverNord Commercial Offer Design

**Research date:** 2026-05-12  
**Purpose:** Design concrete, sellable commercial offers for DriverNord Model A. Each package is a complete product: defined deliverable, defined price, defined terms, defined operations, defined risk profile.

---

## Package 1: First Pilot Search

**Designed for:** Cold outreach to Segment E (repeated-ads) SME åkeri owner who has never paid for recruitment before.

**Problem solved:** "I've posted 3 CE driver ads and got nothing. I'm desperate but skeptical."

**Tagline:** "Zero upfront. Pay only when you hire."

| Element | Detail |
|---------|--------|
| **Customer type** | Segment A or B; first contact; high skepticism; repeated ads history |
| **Deliverable** | Up to 3 verified CE+YKB driver introductions over 4 weeks. Each introduction includes: driver name, phone, email, confirmed license class, YKB valid date, and a 1-paragraph fit summary. |
| **Qualification standard** | CE license (or C if client specifies) + YKB valid for 12+ months + availability confirmed within past 7 days + driver consented to this specific introduction |
| **Price** | Success fee only: **35,000 SEK** flat when driver signs employment contract |
| **Payment timing** | Invoice issued the day driver signs employment agreement; Net-30 |
| **Guarantee** | If the hired driver leaves within 90 days for any reason (other than client-side termination without cause), DriverNord performs one free replacement search |
| **Legal/GDPR requirements** | Client signs a one-page engagement letter confirming: (a) they will only use driver introductions to evaluate this specific hire; (b) they will not forward driver data to third parties; (c) they have signed a data-sharing acknowledgment |
| **Operational steps** | 1. Agree search specification with client (license class, transport domain, shift pattern, start date) 2. Search database for matching verified drivers 3. Notify matching drivers of the opportunity 4. Wait for driver consent (max 48h per driver) 5. Present up to 3 consented introductions 6. Support client with scheduling if needed 7. Follow up at 2-week intervals 8. Invoice on hire |
| **Risk for DriverNord** | High — no revenue if no hire. Mitigated by: (a) only accepting searches where DriverNord has 3+ matching verified drivers in database; (b) limiting to 3 introductions maximum |
| **Expected margin** | 35,000 SEK - ~2,000 SEK operational cost (staff time + admin) = ~33,000 SEK gross. At 50% conversion rate = ~16,500 SEK expected revenue per search initiated |
| **When to use** | First contact with skeptical SME; Segment E target with no trust relationship; when DriverNord needs to prove the model |
| **When NOT to use** | When the client has no budget at all; when the required driver type is outside current database coverage; when the search specification is unrealistic (non-existent combination of requirements) |

## Package 2: Qualified Driver Shortlist

**Designed for:** SME or regional operator who has a specific opening and wants an efficient hiring process without the overhead of a full recruitment search.

**Problem solved:** "I need a CE driver. Show me 3 qualified people I can actually talk to. I don't have time to sort through applications."

**Tagline:** "3 verified CE drivers. Ready to interview. In 5 business days."

| Element | Detail |
|---------|--------|
| **Customer type** | Segment A or B; has a specific vacancy; willing to pay for speed and quality |
| **Deliverable** | Curated shortlist of 3 verified CE+YKB drivers. Delivered as a PDF or cockpit link. Each profile includes: name, phone, email, license class, YKB date, ADR status, availability, years of CE experience, transport domain, brief fit note. |
| **Qualification standard** | CE license + YKB valid 12+ months + digital tachograph card + availability confirmed in past 72 hours + explicit consent to be presented to THIS specific client |
| **Price** | **18,000 SEK** (full shortlist of 3) or **7,000 SEK per single introduction** |
| **Payment timing** | Invoice on delivery of shortlist; Net-14 |
| **Guarantee** | If fewer than 3 drivers meeting the spec are available in the region, DriverNord notifies client before payment. No payment for partial shortlists unless client approves |
| **Legal/GDPR** | Same data-sharing agreement as Package 1. Each driver on the shortlist has individually consented to being presented to this company. |
| **Operational steps** | 1. Client completes a 5-minute search specification form 2. DriverNord runs database query against spec 3. Shortlist 5 candidates (buffer for consent drop-out) 4. Notify 5 candidates by SMS/app/email: "We have a match. Confirm to be introduced?" 5. Wait max 48h for 3+ confirmations 6. Build shortlist PDF/cockpit entry 7. Deliver to client with a brief cover note on each candidate 8. Invoice on delivery |
| **Risk for DriverNord** | Medium — client has already committed to pay on delivery (not on hire). Risk: database may not have 3 matching drivers in region for unusual specification. Mitigate: only accept when 5+ candidates are in database matching the spec |
| **Expected margin** | 18,000 SEK - ~3,000 SEK cost (staff time) = ~15,000 SEK gross. Payment is on delivery, not on hire — better cash flow |
| **When to use** | Client has confirmed budget; has a specific opening; wants speed |
| **When NOT to use** | When database coverage is thin for the region/specialization; when client wants a guarantee of hire (that's Package 3) |

## Package 3: Success Fee Placement

**Designed for:** Client who understands recruitment and is comfortable with a standard fee model. Wants DriverNord to find and deliver the hire, not just the shortlist.

**Problem solved:** "Find me a CE driver who will actually stay. I'll pay when it works."

**Tagline:** "We find, verify, and deliver. You pay when they sign."

| Element | Detail |
|---------|--------|
| **Customer type** | Segment A–C; has previously used recruitment agencies; understands the fee model |
| **Deliverable** | A hired CE+YKB driver who is employed by the client. DriverNord manages the full search, screening, and introduction process. |
| **Qualification standard** | Same as Package 2 plus: DriverNord does a brief pre-screening call with each candidate before presenting to client |
| **Price** | **Flat 40,000 SEK** OR **12% of first-year annual salary — whichever is higher** (min 40,000 SEK at standard CE driver salary) |
| **Payment timing** | Invoice issued when driver signs employment contract; Net-30 |
| **Guarantee** | 90-day replacement guarantee. Free replacement search if placed driver leaves within 90 days. |
| **Legal/GDPR** | Full service agreement required; data processing agreement between DriverNord and client as joint controllers for recruitment purpose |
| **Operational steps** | 1. Client brief (call + written spec) 2. Database search + community outreach if needed 3. Pre-screen 5–8 candidates by phone 4. Present 3 shortlisted candidates to client 5. Coordinate interviews 6. Reference check for finalist (if client requests) 7. Assist with offer process if needed 8. Invoice on hire |
| **Risk for DriverNord** | High — no revenue until hire. Mitigated by: taking only 2–3 active searches at a time; requiring database coverage before committing; 90-day replacement increases total value per successful placement |
| **Expected margin** | 40,000 SEK - ~8,000 SEK cost (staff time, ~4–6 hours) = ~32,000 SEK gross. At 60% conversion = ~19,200 SEK expected per search accepted |
| **When to use** | Client has decision-making authority and budget confirmed; client values the end-to-end service; driver type is well-represented in database |
| **When NOT to use** | Speculative searches with low likelihood of hire; client with slow decision-making culture; driver type with very thin database coverage |

## Package 4: Urgent Replacement Search

**Designed for:** Client who has a driver call in sick, a sudden departure, or a new contract starting in 72 hours.

**Problem solved:** "My driver quit on Friday. I have a route starting Monday. I need someone NOW."

**Tagline:** "Same-day CE driver introduction. Guaranteed qualified."

| Element | Detail |
|---------|--------|
| **Customer type** | Any segment; acute need; time is the primary variable |
| **Deliverable** | 1–2 verified CE+YKB drivers who are available immediately (within 24–72 hours), consented, and ready to contact |
| **Qualification standard** | CE + YKB valid + available THIS WEEK + confirmed by phone or app within last 12 hours |
| **Price** | **12,000 SEK per urgent introduction** (flat, no success fee — client pays on delivery) |
| **Payment timing** | Invoice on delivery; Net-7 |
| **Guarantee** | If driver introduced is not available as described, DriverNord refunds the fee |
| **Legal/GDPR** | Standard data-sharing acknowledgment; expedited consent process using SMS confirmation from driver |
| **Operational steps** | 1. Client calls/messages DriverNord with urgent request 2. DriverNord queries database for drivers flagged "immediately available" 3. Direct phone call to 3 best candidates to confirm availability 4. First confirmed candidate's profile sent to client within 4 hours 5. Invoice on delivery |
| **Risk for DriverNord** | Medium — payment on delivery, not on hire. Risk: driver says available but isn't when client calls. Mitigate: voice confirm availability with each driver before delivery |
| **Expected margin** | 12,000 SEK - ~1,500 SEK (30 min staff time) = ~10,500 SEK. Very high margin per hour of work |
| **When to use** | Client calls with a real emergency; database has "immediately available" drivers in region |
| **When NOT to use** | When no immediately available drivers exist for the spec; when the "urgency" is not genuine (client can wait a week) |

## Package 5: Monthly Driver Pipeline Access (SME)

**Designed for:** Segment A SME åkeri that has regular (but not constant) driver turnover and wants ongoing access to a driver pool without paying per-introduction every time.

**Problem solved:** "We hire 2–3 new drivers a year. We want to stop the fire drill every time someone quits."

**Tagline:** "Browse verified CE drivers anytime. From 2,500 kr/month."

| Element | Detail |
|---------|--------|
| **Customer type** | Segment A (5–30 trucks) with steady but low-volume hiring |
| **Deliverable** | Monthly subscription access to DriverNord's driver database. Client can browse anonymized profiles (Tier 2). 5 per-introduction "request to connect" credits per month. Excess at 3,500 SEK/introduction. |
| **Qualification standard** | Database-level verification (license class + YKB confirmed at registration) |
| **Price** | **2,500 SEK/month** (Standard) or **4,995 SEK/year** (Annual — matches Chaffis.se benchmark) |
| **Payment timing** | Monthly direct debit or annual upfront |
| **Guarantee** | Cancel anytime. No refunds on partial months but zero lock-in. |
| **Legal/GDPR** | Subscription service agreement; data processing agreement; client is end user of DriverNord's platform |
| **Operational steps** | Client registers, selects search criteria, browses anonymized profiles, clicks "Request introduction" on a specific profile. DriverNord notifies the driver. Driver approves or declines. If approved: named profile shared within 24 hours. |
| **Risk for DriverNord** | Low — recurring revenue regardless of hiring activity. Churn risk if database is not maintained with fresh, relevant drivers. |
| **Expected margin** | 2,500 SEK/month - ~200 SEK/month (support + infra/driver cost at 10 active subscribers) = ~2,300 SEK/month/subscriber. 50 subscribers = 115,000 SEK/month gross |
| **When to use** | Onboarding clients after first successful placement; for clients with annual hiring cadence |
| **When NOT to use** | For clients with one-time need only; for clients who need the full managed service of Package 2/3 |

## Package 6: Regional Operator Subscription

**Designed for:** Segment B/C regional operator (50–500 trucks) with steady, high-volume driver hiring needs who would benefit from unlimited introductions.

**Problem solved:** "We hire 10–20 drivers a year. Every placement agency we use charges 60,000+ SEK per hire. This is unsustainable."

**Tagline:** "Unlimited driver introductions. From 9,900 kr/month."

| Element | Detail |
|---------|--------|
| **Customer type** | Segment B/C; 50+ trucks; in-house HR or transport manager; structured hiring process |
| **Deliverable** | Monthly subscription. Unlimited database searches. Unlimited introduction requests (subject to driver consent). Monthly account manager check-in. Quarterly driver pool report showing depth in client's relevant region/specialization. |
| **Qualification standard** | Full database verification (license + YKB + availability confirmed at least monthly) |
| **Price** | **9,900 SEK/month** (Regional) or **18,000 SEK/month** (Fleet — includes interview scheduling and pre-screening calls by DriverNord) |
| **Payment timing** | Monthly subscription; annual contract preferred; 30-day notice cancellation |
| **Guarantee** | 90-day money-back guarantee if zero suitable candidates are found in first 3 months |
| **Legal/GDPR** | Full data processing agreement; client agrees to only use introductions for their own hiring purposes |
| **Operational steps** | Onboarding: agree search criteria and priority regions. Monthly: DriverNord provides updated list of matching drivers. Client requests introductions for specific vacancies. DriverNord manages driver consent and profile delivery. Monthly reporting on database depth and matching drivers. |
| **Risk for DriverNord** | Low (recurring) — medium operational risk if the client generates high introduction volume exceeding what the database can supply |
| **Expected margin** | 9,900 SEK/month - ~2,000 SEK/month (account management time) = ~7,900 SEK. 20 regional subscribers = 158,000 SEK/month |
| **When to use** | Post first placement with a regional operator; when client has asked "can we work with you on an ongoing basis?" |
| **When NOT to use** | When client is Segment A with minimal hiring (Package 5 is more appropriate) |

## Package Comparison Summary

| Package | Customer | Price | Payment | DriverNord Risk | Cash Flow |
|---------|----------|-------|---------|-----------------|-----------|
| 1: First Pilot | Cold SME | 35,000 SEK | On hire | High | Delayed |
| 2: Shortlist | Any | 18,000 SEK | On delivery | Medium | Fast |
| 3: Success Fee | Experienced buyer | 40,000 SEK+ | On hire | High | Delayed |
| 4: Urgent Search | Any, emergency | 12,000 SEK | On delivery | Medium-low | Fast |
| 5: SME Subscription | Regular SME | 2,500/month | Monthly | Low | Recurring |
| 6: Regional Sub | Regional operator | 9,900/month | Monthly | Low | Recurring |

**Recommended Year 1 go-to sequence:**  
1. Use Package 1 (free pilot / success only) to get first 5 clients
2. After first hire, offer Package 2 (shortlist) as the default product
3. Introduce Package 4 (urgent) when seasonal peaks arrive (summer/Q4)
4. After 3+ successful engagements with a client, offer Package 5/6 subscription
