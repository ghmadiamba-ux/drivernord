# 02 — Competitor Models: Sweden

**Research date:** 2026-05-12  
**Scope:** All categories of actors occupying the driver-supply market in Sweden  
**Source reliability scoring:** 1 (anecdotal) – 5 (official statistics/peer-reviewed)

---

## Overview

The Swedish driver supply market contains five actor categories:
1. **Staffing agencies (bemanningsföretag)** — hire out drivers on hourly/daily terms
2. **Recruitment agencies (rekryteringsföretag)** — permanent placement on contingency fee
3. **Job boards and aggregators** — passive matching; employer posts, driver applies
4. **Training schools with placement** — produce new-entrant drivers and connect them to employers
5. **Digital platforms and emerging AI tools** — matching, marketplace, lead-gen

No actor currently occupies the "verified driver pipeline / lead generation" category that DriverNord is targeting.

---

## Category 1: Staffing Agencies (Bemanningsföretag)

These companies employ drivers directly and hire them out to client transport companies. The agency is the legal employer (LAS, AML, taxes, insurance). Clients pay an hourly invoice rate covering driver wage + employer costs + agency margin.

### 1.1 Jobzone (Job&Talent group)

| Attribute | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| Founded | 2002 |
| Ownership | Part of Job&Talent (Spanish tech-staffing unicorn, $1B+ valuation) |
| Geography | Sweden (Stockholm, Jönköping, Sörmland, Västerås, Uppsala) + Norway, Denmark |
| Specialization | Transport & logistics, warehouse, construction |
| Driver segments served | C, CE, distribution, piece goods, waste, long-distance |
| Technology | Job&Talent app-based scheduling platform |
| Scale | Nordic-wide; one of the largest transport staffing companies in the region |
| Market position | Premium — targets medium and large operators (Segment B/C) |
| Website | jobzone.se |

**Competitive intelligence:** Jobzone operates a mobile app that lets drivers manage shifts and availability, and lets clients post requirements. Their Job&Talent parent has invested heavily in app-based shift matching. They are the most tech-forward incumbent in transport staffing.

**Weaknesses for DriverNord:** Jobzone's model requires drivers to be Jobzone employees, creating overhead. They do not serve the sub-50-truck SME segment cost-effectively. Their pricing (as a Job&Talent subsidiary with PE return expectations) is high. They have no qualification-guarantee SLA.

## Category 2: Recruitment Agencies (Permanent Placement)

These agencies charge a one-time placement fee (typically 15–25% of annual salary) to connect a driver with a client company that then employs the driver permanently. Less common for drivers than for white-collar roles.

### Key actors:
- **Prowork** — multi-sector recruitment including transport
- Generic rekryteringsbyråer using Blocket Jobb / Teamtailor / LinkedIn

**Pricing example:** CE driver annual salary ~400,000 SEK → placement fee 60,000–100,000 SEK. This is significant upfront cost. SME åkerier resist permanent placement fees.

## Category 3: Job Boards and Aggregators

| Platform | Type | Transport Focus | Reach |
|----------|------|-----------------|-------|
| Platsbanken (Arbetsförmedlingen) | Free government board | General | Largest in Sweden |
| Blocket Jobb | Paid listings | General | Very high SME use |
| Indeed SE | Aggregator/listing | General | International reach |
| Monster SE | Paid listing | General | Declining |
| Transportjobb.se | Niche transport | Transport/logistics only | Targeted |
| Chaufförsjobb.se | Niche driver | Driver-specific | Small but targeted |
| Jobbland.se | Aggregator | Nordic coverage | Aggregates other boards |
| LinkedIn | Professional network | White collar + professional | Useful for fleet managers/DMs |

**Critical weakness of job boards:** They are passive channels. Employers post; drivers (sometimes) apply. No pre-screening. No license verification. No availability tracking. A company posting a CE driver ad on Blocket Jobb gets applications ranging from CE-certified professionals to unqualified individuals who misread the ad.

## Category 4: Training Schools with Placement

| Actor | Type | Notes |
|-------|------|-------|
| Gymnasiet Fordon- och transportprogram | High school vocational | 3-year program; produces entry-level drivers |
| Yrkeshögskola transport programs | Post-secondary VET | 1-2 year programs; more advanced |
| Driving schools (körkortsskolor) | License-specific | Produce CE/C/D license holders; no job placement |
| TYA (Transportfackens Yrkes- och Arbetsmiljönämnd) | Industry body + training | Not a placement agency; produces research and sets standards |
| AMU courses (Arbetsmarknadsutbildning) | Government-funded upskilling | Arbetsförmedlingen-funded driver training for unemployed |

**Market dynamic:** Training supply is constrained. Municipalities are cutting seats on transport programs. The pipeline of new-entrant drivers is shrinking at the exact moment demand is growing.

## Category 5: Digital Platforms and AI Tools

### 5.1 Paradox AI (trucking/logistics module)

US-based AI recruiter ("Olivia") that handles chatbot screening, scheduling, and driver application intake for trucking and logistics companies. Not driver-specific database. Not present in Swedish market in any meaningful way (2024–2026).

### 5.2 Vinter AI (Nordic)

Swedish AI recruitment platform raised €1.4M seed in 2024. Focus on standardized assessments and structured video interviews. General recruitment, not driver-specific. No license verification. Targeting tech and professional roles primarily.

### 5.3 Alva Labs (Swedish)

Full-stack AI hiring system. Psychometric + competence-based. Focus on white-collar and professional roles. No driver market presence.

### 5.4 DrivenPeople (UK)

App-based driver community and recruitment platform targeting the UK market. "Eliminates the middleman." Direct operator-driver connection. Relevant model precedent for DriverNord — this is the closest analog internationally — but operating in UK, not Sweden.

### 5.5 Tenstreet / DriverReach (US)

US-focused truck driver recruiting CRM platforms. High-volume US market tools. Not relevant to Swedish market.

**The entire low-cost / high-tech / verified-driver-database quadrant is empty.** No actor currently provides:
- A pre-verified, searchable, available driver database
- At a price point accessible to SME åkeri (Segment A)
- With technology to manage the matching and presentation

## Key Competitor Weaknesses DriverNord Can Exploit

| Competitor Type | Key Weakness | DriverNord Opportunity |
|-----------------|-------------|------------------------|
| Staffing agencies | High markup (30–50% above employment cost); driver must be their employee | Offer verified leads without employment overhead |
| Job boards | Passive, no pre-screening, no license verification | Active, verified, pre-qualified database |
| Generalist recruiters | Not driver-specialized; no Transportstyrelsen knowledge | Deep specialist with YKB/CE expertise |
| No current actor | No searchable database of available verified drivers | Build this database = durable competitive moat |
