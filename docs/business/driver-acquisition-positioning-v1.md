## Final Positioning

**DriverNord is not a bemanningsbolag. Not a jobbsajt. Not a recruiter.**

DriverNord is a specialist matching service: transport companies pay to find the right CE- or C-driver. The driver pays nothing, controls their own profile, and decides whether to proceed with any match. Their data is never shared without their explicit approval.

The core emotional position is **relief from a broken system** — not career advice, not opportunity, not excitement. Relief.

Swedish CE- and C-drivers have spent years being treated as interchangeable units by bemanningsbolag, being ignored by generic job boards, and being cold-called by people who don't know the difference between a CE and a C license. DriverNord is positioned as the first service that understands their profession, respects their autonomy, and asks for nothing except 3 minutes.

## Audience Hierarchy

### Tier 1 — Primary (Highest campaign priority)

**Missnöjde Marcus** — Employed CE/C driver, 27–50, Stockholm/Mälardalen. Not actively job-seeking. Dissatisfied with current situation (schedule changes without notice, below-market salary, bemanning taking a margin). The right message opens a door he didn't think was available. Converts on Sunday evenings 19:00–22:00.

Why Tier 1: Largest reachable population (12,000–18,000 in target geography), converts at 2× rate of Johan on cold paid media, lower acquisition cost, sufficient lead quality for V1 clients.

### Tier 2 — Secondary (quality anchor)

**Aktive Johan** — CE freelancer/enskild firma, 33–52, Stockholm/Mälardalen. Actively managing his own pipeline. Has HIAB, kylfrys, or ADR specialty. Deeply skeptical of any service that looks or sounds like bemanning. Converts only after 3–5 touchpoints. Builds the highest-quality supply and becomes the strongest referral source once trust is established.

Why Tier 2: Premium lead quality, referral multiplier, but slow to trust and expensive to acquire cold.

### Tier 3 — Overlay (seasonal activation only)

**YKB-fönstret** — Any CE/C driver who recently renewed or is about to renew their YKB (Yrkeskompetensbevis). The investment of 35 hours and 5,000–12,000 SEK creates a strong natural motivation to activate their qualification. Seasonal: September–November and February–April.

### Tier 4 — Deprioritized in V1

**Unga CE-kortet** — 22–33, recently licensed CE driver. Less skeptical, but harder to place with V1 clients who want experienced specialists. Lower lead quality for V1. Revisit in V2.

## Tone of Voice

**What it sounds like:** Someone who has been in a truck cab at 04:30 in November. Someone who knows what a CMR is, who knows YKB renewal costs money and time, who knows what "inhyrd via bemanning" actually means for a driver's take-home pay. Not a startup founder. Not an HR person. Not a recruiter.

**What it does not sound like:** Corporate HR. Startup optimism. Recruitment agency. "Exciting opportunity." Anything that could run as copy for a staffing agency.

**Tone markers:**
- Direct. Short sentences. No subordinate clauses that can be removed.
- Uses the right technical words without explaining them.
- Acknowledges the broken system instead of pretending it doesn't exist.
- Never speaks down to the driver. They know their profession better than anyone.
- Permission-giving, not pressure-creating. "You can. You don't have to."

## Anti-Bemanning Positioning

Bemanningsbolag are the primary enemy of trust for both Johan and Marcus. The anti-bemanning message is not about attacking competitors by name — it is about naming the problem the driver already has.

**The problem to name:** "Bemanningsbolag tar 25–30% av det du genererar. Du kör — de tjänar."

**How to position DriverNord:**
- We are not a bemanningsbolag. State this explicitly. Not implied — stated.
- DriverNord is paid by the transport company for the introduction. Once. After that, the driver and company have a direct relationship.
- DriverNord takes no percentage of the driver's salary. Never. There is no ongoing margin.
- The driver is not "inhyrd" through DriverNord. They are matched and introduced. The relationship is direct.

**Copy formulas that work:**
- "DriverNord är inte ett bemanningsbolag. Vi tar aldrig en andel av din lön."
- "Vi tjänar pengar när du hittar rätt åkeri — inte på varje timme du kör."
- "En gång. Sedan är du fri."
- "Jobbar du via bemanning? Det är din tid de tjänar på."

**Compliance note:** Never claim that working via DriverNord is "better" than bemanning — claim only that the model is different. Describe the model accurately. The driver forms their own judgment.

## Core Promise

**"Vi matchar dig med transportföretag som söker exakt vad du kör. Din profil delas aldrig utan ditt godkännande."**

This is the single sentence that DriverNord must be able to honor for every driver, every time. Every piece of copy, every ad, every email, every SMS must be consistent with this promise. Never promise outcomes (placements, jobs, assignments). Promise only the matching and the consent model.

## CTA Rules

**What works:**
- "Registrera din profil" — neutral, low-commitment
- "Starta registrering" — action-oriented but not pressuring
- "Registrera dig på 3 minuter" — specific time makes it feel achievable
- "Kom igång" — minimal commitment framing
- "Se om vi har en match" — information-seeking frame (removes job-seeking stigma)

**What does not work:**
- "Ansök nu" — wrong frame. Never.
- "Sök jobb" — wrong frame.
- "Skicka in din ansökan" — wrong frame.
- "Börja din karriär" — wrong frame.
- "Ta chansen" — pressure. Activates reactance.
- Countdown timers or "limited spots" — fake urgency. Detected and punished.
- Exclamation marks in CTAs — diminishes credibility.

**Mobile CTA rule:** The CTA must make sense as a full sentence read aloud from a phone. "Starta registrering" passes. "Se om vi har en match" passes. "Ansök nu!" fails.

## Trust Rules

These rules apply to every piece of DriverNord driver-facing content, regardless of channel:

1. **The consent model is stated before anything else is asked.** Before a driver gives their phone number, they must understand their data is not shared without their explicit separate approval. This is stated in the `/chat` welcome screen and in the consent step. In ads, it is in the body copy.

2. **No false social proof.** Until real drivers have been matched and have agreed to be testimonial sources, no testimonials, no claims about scale ("hundreds of drivers"), no invented reviews. Nascent social proof ("X drivers have registered") is permitted when the number is real.

3. **No urgency fabrication.** No "only 3 spots left," no countdown timers, no "last chance." Swedish drivers detect and resent this. Use honest scarcity only: "We are currently accepting CE drivers in Stockholm" (true) is honest; "Only 5 spots left" (false) destroys trust permanently.

4. **Speak before asking.** Demonstrate understanding of the driver's world before asking for anything. An ad that opens with "Kör du CE-HIAB i Stockholm?" demonstrates understanding. An ad that opens with "Register now!" asks before demonstrating anything.

5. **One contact promise.** "Vi hör av oss bara när vi har ett relevant uppdrag" must be honored. If a driver registers and DriverNord contacts them for any other reason, the trust is broken. No newsletters, no speculative contacts, no "checking in."

6. **The revenue model is always transparent.** Transport companies pay DriverNord. Drivers pay nothing. State this when questioned. Never hide it.

## Campaign Implications

### What the persona research changes for campaign execution

**Old assumption (driver-acquisition-plan.md):** Target "Erik" and "Karim" with generic messaging about finding jobs.

**New assumption (this document):** Target Marcus first with permission-granting copy that doesn't require him to identify as a job-seeker. Target Johan second with specialist-recognition copy in Facebook groups, not cold ads.

**Old campaign objective:** Lead Generation (Meta)

**New campaign objective:** Traffic to `/chat` — with conversion tracked via UTM and Pixel. Conversion optimization requires volume that V1 won't have; traffic campaigns give more control at test scale.

**Old creative direction:** Professional branded images, clean studio quality.

**New creative direction:** Real trucks, real Swedish industrial locations, honest and unglamorous. Mobile-quality video is more authentic than studio quality.

**Old CTA:** "Ansök nu" / "Registrera dig gratis"

**New CTA:** "Starta registrering" / "Se om vi har en match" — never "ansök", never "gratis" as the primary word.

### First campaign angle — implement this first

**Ad:** Sunday evening Facebook Feed, Marcus (P2)
**Opening line:** "Det är söndag kväll. Imorgon tillbaka till samma sak. Det finns ett alternativ."
**Body:** 3 sentences: name the escape hatch, state the process, state the consent promise.
**CTA:** "Starta registrering"
**Delivery window:** Sunday 19:00–22:00

### Anti-bemanning angle — run as second creative

**Ad:** Static, dark background, white text only (signals serious content).
**Opening line:** "Trött på att bemanningsbolag tjänar 30% på varje timme du kör?"
**Body:** Explain DriverNord's model: transport company pays, driver pays nothing, direct relationship, no ongoing margin.
**CTA:** "Se hur det fungerar"
