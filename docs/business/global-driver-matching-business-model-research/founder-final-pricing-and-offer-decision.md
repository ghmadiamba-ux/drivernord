## 1. Which Business Model to Copy

**Primary: Flat fee per shortlist delivery (O Trucking trigger + LMDR framing)**

O Trucking charges ~5,200 SEK equivalent when they hand over a verified driver contact. Payment is on delivery — not on interview, not on hire. LMDR packages that same delivery as a *ranked shortlist*, not a raw lead. DriverNord combines both: a ranked, governance-cleared shortlist, paid on delivery.

That is the model. No further evaluation needed.

**Backup: Per-introduction flat fee (single candidate, Package 3)**

For employers who will not commit to a 3-candidate shortlist on first contact. Same trigger — paid on delivery of one verified introduction. Priced lower than Package 2 because volume is lower, not because quality is lower.

**Everything else is rejected:**

- Percentage of salary: requires salary disclosure, creates hire-confirmation disputes, cannot scale with AI
- Success fee (pay on hire): collection latency, disputed trigger, gives employer all control over payment
- Job board (posting fee): no differentiation, employer does all screening work
- Subscription: driver inventory is too thin to sustain a subscription promise today

## 3. Collection Trigger: Introduction Delivery — Not Hire

**Decision: Payment is due when DriverNord delivers the shortlist. Not when the employer interviews. Not when the employer hires.**

This is non-negotiable. Here is why:

Traditional recruiters charge on hire — and employers accept it because the recruiter claims to share the risk. DriverNord does not share the hiring risk. DriverNord delivers pre-screened, governance-cleared candidates. The employer's decision to interview, offer, and hire is theirs. Charging on hire would mean DriverNord's revenue depends on the employer's internal process speed, offer competitiveness, and budget availability — none of which DriverNord controls.

Delivery trigger also means: DriverNord can invoice and close the transaction on day 1. No chasing. No disputes about whether the hire "counts."

If an employer says "I'll pay when I hire," the answer is: "We charge on delivery of the shortlist. Manpower charges you 84,000 SEK whether you hire or not. We charge you 15,000 SEK for three pre-screened candidates you can contact today. The risk is already 80% lower."

## 5. Pilot Terms (Exact, for All 5 Pilot Companies)

### What DriverNord provides

- One Kortlistpaket-equivalent delivery: 3 ranked, governance-cleared CE/C/D candidates
- Match rationale per candidate: license, YKB, region, availability, match score
- Candidate contact details: name, phone, email where available
- Governance status per candidate: Clean or Blocked with explanation
- One 20-minute debrief call within 30 days of delivery

**DriverNord will not deliver the shortlist until all 3 candidates carry governance-clean status.** If the pool has fewer than 2 clean candidates for the need, the pilot waits.

### What the pilot employer provides

**Non-negotiable (must be agreed before delivery):**
1. Written feedback within 14 days of receiving the shortlist (3-question form — see below)
2. Permission to reference "a [sector] company in [region] pilottestade vår tjänst" (anonymised)
3. Participation in a 20-minute debrief call (scheduling flexible within 30 days)

**Optional (ask, do not require):**
- Named case study (company name on drivernord.com)
- Testimonial quote (attributed or anonymised)
- Reference call for one future prospect

### What the employer does not owe

- No payment
- No obligation to hire from the shortlist
- No exclusivity
- No minimum notice to exit

### The 3-question feedback form

> 1. Did you contact any of the three candidates? (Yes / No / Not yet)
> 2. Did you schedule an interview with any of them? (Yes / No)
> 3. One sentence: What was your impression of the shortlist quality?

That is the entire feedback form. No longer.

### Pilot terms expiry

The pilot shortlist is valid for 90 days from delivery. After 90 days without feedback, DriverNord may close the pilot record and offer the slot to a new company.

## 8. What to Explicitly Avoid

These are permanent exclusions — not things to revisit later:

| What to avoid | Why |
|---|---|
| "Betala när ni anställer" (pay on hire) | Collection latency; hire-confirmation disputes; employer controls payment |
| Percentage of salary fee | Requires salary disclosure; incompatible with AI-scale model |
| Monthly subscription (before 1,000+ verified drivers) | Churn risk if supply cannot meet demand; destroys trust in first month |
| Calling the product "rekrytering" | Anchors employer to traditional cost expectations and recruiter-relationship model |
| Offering discounts on Package 2 below 9,000 SEK | Below that price, the product is perceived as a raw lead, not a curated introduction |
| Delivering a shortlist with flagged candidates | One DQ-flagged candidate who turns out to be fraudulent destroys the pilot |
| Charging a job posting fee | Positions DriverNord as a job board; no differentiation from Arbetsförmedlingen |
| Promising a replacement shortlist if the employer doesn't hire | Introduces open-ended liability; the product is the shortlist, not the hire |

## 9. The 3 Strongest Pricing Anchors

Use these in every sales conversation. In this order.

### Anchor 1: Traditional Swedish rekrytering — 63,000–105,000 SEK

"A traditional recruitment firm for a CE driver charges 15–25% of annual salary. At 420,000 SEK/year, that is 63,000 to 105,000 SEK — and they invoice whether you hire or not."

This anchor makes 15,000 SEK look like a relief, not a cost. Lead with this every time.

### Anchor 2: Bemanning monthly cost — 48,000–72,000 SEK per month

"If you solve this with a bemanningsföretag, you're paying 300–450 SEK per hour, or 48,000–72,000 SEK per month per driver. That's for a temporary solution with no path to permanent hire. DriverNord's shortlist gives you three candidates to hire permanently for 15,000 SEK — once."

Use this when the employer says "we normally use bemanning."

### Anchor 3: O Trucking US benchmark — ~5,200 SEK per unverified lead

"In the US market, companies pay $500 — around 5,200 SEK — for a single unverified CDL driver lead with no ranking and no data quality check. DriverNord's Kortlistpaket gives you three pre-screened, ranked, governance-cleared candidates for 15,000 SEK. That's 5,000 SEK per verified introduction."

Use this when the employer says "that seems expensive." It reframes 15,000 SEK as 5,000 SEK × 3.
