# Global Competitor Map — Driver Matching & Recruitment Platforms

**Research date:** 2026-05-21  
**Scope:** Global — USA, UK, EU, Canada, Nordics  
**Companies researched:** 25  
**Purpose:** Competitive landscape mapping for DriverNord positioning  

---

## Classification Key

| Label | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Very close** | Same model: AI/quality-matched driver pool → employer pays per introduction/shortlist, non-staffing |
| **Close** | Same buyer (employer), driver pool, introduction/per-hire logic, non-staffing |
| **Adjacent** | Same sector or same payment model but different structure (staffing, SaaS, or pure jobboard) |
| **Benchmark only** | Useful for pricing/positioning reference; not a direct model competitor |

## 1. USA — CDL/Trucking Recruitment Platforms

### 1.1 O Trucking
**Classification: Very Close**  
**URL:** https://otrucking.com  
**Country:** USA  
**What they sell:** Pre-screened CDL driver placements from their own driver network  
**Who pays:** Employer (carrier)  
**Pricing:** $500 per solo driver placed; $750 per team. Pay-only-after-hire. No subscription, no upfront cost.  
**Driver pool:** Yes — active CDL network with MVR/PSP/DOT documentation on file  
**AI/matching:** No — manual matching on equipment type, lanes, pay range, location  
**Verified/prequalified:** Yes — CDL check, MVR, PSP, DOT physical, drug screen, employment history  
**Staffing (bemanning):** No — carrier hires directly  
**Jobboard:** No  
**Similar to DriverNord:** Flat-fee pay-per-introduction from a pre-screened pool; pay-only-on-hire; non-staffing; guarantee if driver backs out; "done-for-you recruiting, not a job board"  
**Different from DriverNord:** No AI/agent; USA-only; $500 price point is low vs DriverNord's 8,000–15,000 SEK; no structured shortlist format; no demand-signal detection  
**Lesson:** The flat-fee-per-introduction model works and has a paying customer base. Employer messaging "no subscriptions, no hidden charges, pay only after hiring" resonates strongly.

### 1.2 CDLers

**URL:** https://www.cdlers.com  
**Country:** USA  
**What they sell:** Access to consent-based CDL driver profiles, max 7 days old  
**Who pays:** Employer

**Driver pool:** Yes — drivers submit profiles directly; continuous fresh intake  
**AI/matching:** No — employer browses and selects  
**Verified/prequalified:** Profiles filtered by experience, trailer type, endorsements  
**Staffing:** No  
**Jobboard:** No — "neither a recruiting agency nor a job board"  
**Similar to DriverNord:** Consent-based driver pool; employer pays to access profiles; quality filters  
**Different from DriverNord:** No AI matching; no structured shortlist; employer does their own selection; US-only  
**Lesson:** "Neither a recruiting agency nor a job board" is proven category-creation language. Consent-based pool freshness (max 7 days) is a strong quality signal.

**Different from DriverNord:** Subscription model not per-introduction; human matchmakers augment technology; US-only; no demand-signal detection  
**Lesson:** Matching on 20+ attributes before either party commits is powerful employer pitch: "Both parties see the match quality before entering the formal hiring process."

**Country:** UK  
**What they sell:** Direct sourcing platform giving operators 24/7 access to a verified HGV driver pool  
**Who pays:** Operator (employer)

**Lesson:** Transparent pricing and "direct sourcing, not traditional agency" framing is effective. Per-use pricing (vs subscription) works in temp markets; per-introduction works better for permanent placement.

## 3. Swedish Market (Most Strategically Important)

### 3.1 Förartjänst.se
**Classification: Close (same market)**  
**URL:** https://www.forartjanst.se  
**Country:** Sweden — SAME TARGET MARKET  
**What they sell:** Swedish job board + matching platform for truck drivers and transport companies. "En naturlig mötesplats" for Swedish transport. 20,000+ registered drivers.  
**Who pays:** Employers pay annual subscription; drivers free  
**Pricing:** Annual subscription; exact SEK amount not publicly listed. Some add-on marketing options.  
**Driver pool:** Yes — 20,000+ registered Swedish drivers  
**AI/matching:** Algorithmic matching  
**Verified/prequalified:** Standard profile-based, not deeply pre-screened  
**Staffing:** No  
**Jobboard:** Partially yes — companies can post positions; matching is layered on top  
**Similar to DriverNord:** SAME market; Swedish transport; driver pool; matching; non-staffing  
**Different from DriverNord:** Passive job board logic (post + wait) vs DriverNord's active agent-led outreach; no quality scoring depth; annual subscription not per-introduction; no AI agents; **currently rebuilding platform ("Vi bygger om 2026") — the competitive window is now**  
**Lesson:** 20,000+ Swedish drivers is a real market signal. Annual subscription shows employers will pay. DriverNord's active AI-agent approach is a fundamentally different product.

DriverNord's proposed model is unoccupied in the Swedish market as of May 2026.
