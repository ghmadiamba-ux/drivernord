# Recommended DriverNord Pricing — Based on Global Benchmarks

**Research date:** 2026-05-21  
**Scope:** Pricing proposals grounded in global benchmarks, Swedish market anchors, and DriverNord's current operational state  
**Status:** Research recommendation — not yet validated by a paying customer

## Proposed Package Structure

### Package 1: Single Verified Introduction — 5,500 SEK

**Use case:** Employer wants to test DriverNord with minimal commitment before a larger engagement  
**What they receive:** One pre-screened, governance-cleared driver introduction for their specific need  
**Payment trigger:** On delivery of the introduction (not on hire)  
**Rationale:**
- Above O Trucking ($500 = ~5,200 SEK) and above UK The Drivers Hub top end (4,550 SEK)
- Below LMDR equivalent (~8,000 SEK) — appropriate for a single intro
- Positioned as a "taste test" — not the preferred package, but lowers entry barrier for skeptical employers
- Removes "we don't know you yet" objection
- If the employer hires the driver, they already have proof of quality before Package 2

**When to offer:** Only to employers who explicitly resist Package 2 after full pitch. Do not lead with this.

---

### Package 2: Priority Shortlist — 15,000 SEK

**Use case:** Standard DriverNord product. Employer has an active CE/C/D hiring need. They receive 3 ranked, pre-screened candidates with match scores, license verification, YKB status, availability, and region.  
**What they receive:** Shortlist of 3 verified candidates, ranked by match score, with governance clearance status per candidate  
**Payment trigger:** On delivery of the shortlist  
**Rationale:**
- 76–86% below traditional rekrytering (63,000–105,000 SEK) — primary anchor in sales conversation
- 5× O Trucking's per-lead fee but justified by: 3 candidates, pre-screened, ranked, governance-cleared
- Consistent with LMDR US equivalent for a 3-candidate shortlist
- Covers ~150,000 SEK in employer time-cost savings (screening, interviewing, no-shows)
- The clearest ROI story: "one successful hire from this shortlist pays back 12× the introduction fee against what Manpower would charge"

**This is the primary product.** All marketing, pilot conversations, and pilot agreements should default to this package.

---

### Package 3: Urgent Single Introduction — 9,500 SEK

**Use case:** Employer has an urgent, time-sensitive hiring need (typically "within 2 weeks"). They want DriverNord to surface the best single candidate immediately.  
**What they receive:** One top-ranked, governance-cleared driver for their specific need, with a written match rationale  
**Payment trigger:** On delivery (same day or next business day if candidate is in current pool)  
**Rationale:**
- Price reflects urgency premium over Package 1 (5,500 SEK) but is below Package 2 (15,000 SEK)
- Justified: urgent needs carry higher employer pain and lower price sensitivity
- O Trucking charges $500 for a non-urgent unverified lead. Urgent + verified + ranked = 9,500 SEK is defensible
- The "same-day" promise is only valid if the candidate pool includes a clean match — do not over-promise

**Note:** Package 3 is the former "Package 4" in earlier documentation. Renamed for clearer tiering.

---

### Package 4: Repeat Employer Retainer — 35,000 SEK / 3 shortlists

**Use case:** Employer has ongoing or recurring CE/C/D hiring needs (e.g. growth-stage transport company, distribution firm in expansion).  
**What they receive:** 3 shortlists (each 3 candidates) delivered over a 90-day window, for any combination of their open needs  
**Payment trigger:** Upfront or 50% upfront + 50% on first shortlist delivery  
**Rationale:**
- Effectively 11,667 SEK per shortlist (22% discount vs. Package 2 standalone)
- Creates predictable revenue for DriverNord
- Creates commitment signal from employer (reduces ghosting risk after shortlist delivery)
- Requires at minimum 9 clean candidates in pool across the employer's regions and license categories — only offer once inventory is confirmed

**When to introduce:** After a successful Package 2 engagement. Never as the first offer.

## Pilot-Specific Pricing (Pre-Validation Phase)

For the 5 pilot employers (Enskede Bilexpress, JPC, Edvardssons, and 2 TBD):

### Pilot terms:
- **Price:** 0 SEK for the first shortlist delivery (one per pilot employer)
- **What they receive:** A Package 2-equivalent shortlist (3 ranked, governance-cleared candidates)
- **What DriverNord receives:** Written feedback, permission to reference the engagement (anonymised or named with consent), and a right-of-first-offer to Package 2 after the pilot
- **No strings:** Pilot employer has zero obligation to hire or pay
- **Expiry:** Pilot offer is not open-ended. The pilot is time-limited (90 days from introduction delivery)

**Rationale:** The pilot is not a discount — it is a proof-of-concept in exchange for social proof and case study material. DriverNord earns no cash in the pilot phase. It earns validation. Five validated pilots with positive employer feedback are worth more than five failed first sales.

## Price Increase Roadmap

| Phase | Trigger | Package 2 price |
|---|---|---|
| Pilot (now) | 0 validated employers | 0 SEK (pilot) |
| Post-pilot | 2+ employer validations | 9,000 SEK (introductory) |
| Early traction | 5+ paid employers | 12,000 SEK |
| Established | 10+ paid employers, repeat customers | 15,000 SEK |
| Premium | Named case studies, AI governance story proven | 18,000–22,000 SEK |

**Principle:** Do not increase price until each prior tier has at least 2 repeat customers. Price increases are justified by proof, not by ambition.

## Pricing Psychology Notes

### Lead with the savings anchor, not the fee

Wrong opening: "The package is 15,000 SEK."  
Right opening: "Traditional recruiting for a CE driver costs 63,000–105,000 SEK. We deliver a pre-screened shortlist for 15,000 SEK — you save 80–85%."

### The introduction fee vs. the hire cost argument

Employer objection: "What if we don't hire anyone from the shortlist?"  
Response: "You pay for the introduction, not for the hire. Manpower charges you 63,000 SEK and also doesn't guarantee you'll want to hire their candidate. We charge 15,000 SEK and give you three candidates — the probability is in your favour."

### Urgency pricing is not about urgency for DriverNord

Package 3 (urgent) should be positioned as: "We prioritise your need above all other shortlists in the queue." The urgency premium reflects queue priority, not DriverNord working harder — it's a capacity allocation signal.

## What Not to Do on Pricing

1. **Do not offer percentage-of-salary fees.** Requires salary disclosure, creates disputes, incompatible with AI-first model.
2. **Do not offer success fees (pay on hire).** Creates collection latency and hire-confirmation disputes.
3. **Do not offer monthly subscriptions before 1,000+ verified drivers are in pool.**
4. **Do not discount Package 2 below 9,000 SEK for non-pilots.** Below that, the product is priced as a lead, not an introduction.
5. **Do not negotiate on the collection trigger.** Payment is always on delivery, never on hire.
