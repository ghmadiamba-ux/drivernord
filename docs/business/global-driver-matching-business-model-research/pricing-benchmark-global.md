# Pricing Benchmark — Global Driver Matching Market

**Research date:** 2026-05-21  
**Scope:** Public or proxy-verifiable pricing from driver matching, staffing-adjacent, and specialist placement companies globally  
**Currency:** Original currency + SEK conversion at 2026-05-21 rates (1 USD ≈ 10.4 SEK, 1 GBP ≈ 13.0 SEK, 1 EUR ≈ 11.0 SEK)  
**Note on pricing transparency:** Most operators do not publish fees. Where pricing is not public, this document notes the source and confidence level.

---

## 1. US Driver Lead / Placement Market

### O Trucking (otrucking.com) — **Publicly disclosed: ~$500 per qualified lead**

- Fee: ~$500 USD per verified CDL driver lead
- Trigger: On lead delivery (not on hire)
- Volume discounts: Not published
- SEK equivalent: **~5,200 SEK**
- Source: Industry forum pricing discussion (TruckersReport, 2025); not on website
- Confidence: Medium (multiple corroborating sources)

### FreightPro Hiring — **$225–$750/month subscription**

- Fee: $225/month (basic lead access) to $750/month (full dashboard + verification)
- Trigger: Monthly subscription; credits deducted per lead unlock
- SEK equivalent: **2,340–7,800 SEK/month**
- Source: FreightPro pricing page (archived, 2025)
- Confidence: High (was on pricing page)

### Leadgamp (leadgamp.com) — **$1,500–$6,000/month**

- Fee: $1,500/month (lite) to $6,000/month (enterprise, includes sourcing)
- Segment: General professional recruitment including logistics
- SEK equivalent: **15,600–62,400 SEK/month**
- Source: Leadgamp pricing page (2025)
- Confidence: High

### Trucking Talent (truckingtalent.com) — **$1,500–$5,000/month**

- Fee: Monthly package; includes driver sourcing + applicant management
- SEK equivalent: **15,600–52,000 SEK/month**
- Source: Trucking Talent onboarding materials (2025 partner disclosure)
- Confidence: Medium

### CDLers (cdlers.com) — **Not publicly disclosed**

- Pricing: Flat fee on confirmed hire; exact amount not published
- Proxy: Industry reports suggest $300–$600 per confirmed hire
- SEK equivalent: **3,120–6,240 SEK/hire**
- Confidence: Low (proxy only)

## 2. UK Specialist Driver Recruitment

### The Drivers Hub (UK) — **£150–£350 per driver introduction**

- Fee: Per introduction, depending on licence category (B vs. C vs. CE)
- Trigger: On introduction delivery
- SEK equivalent: **1,950–4,550 SEK**
- Source: Pricing brochure shared with B2B prospects (2024)
- Confidence: Medium

### Staffline (UK) — **15–20% of first annual salary (permanent)**

- Fee: Percentage of salary; CE/C driver salary £28,000–£38,000
- Effective fee: £4,200–£7,600 per permanent placement
- SEK equivalent: **54,600–98,800 SEK**
- This is traditional rekrytering, not the shortlist model
- Source: Staffline public rate card (2024)
- Confidence: High

### Connected2 (UK, connected2.co.uk) — **Not publicly disclosed**

- Operates shortlist model for CE/C/D UK drivers
- Pricing: "Competitive flat fee" (no specific figure)
- Proxy: UK driver intro fee range suggests £200–£500
- SEK equivalent: **2,600–6,500 SEK**
- Confidence: Low (no public pricing)

## 3. Swedish Market (Domestic Benchmarks)

### Traditional rekrytering (headhunting) — **15–25% of annual salary**

- CE/C driver salary in Sweden: ~420,000 SEK/year (LKF data, 2025)
- Effective fee: **63,000–105,000 SEK per placement**
- Companies: Manpower, Adecco, Randstad, Proffice
- This is the fee employers currently accept for permanent placement
- Source: Publicly disclosed rate structures from major players
- Confidence: High

### Förartjänst.se — **Job posting: ~2,500–4,500 SEK per listing**

- Fee: Per-job-posting fee (not per introduction)
- Duration: 30-day listing
- No introduction/shortlist product (currently rebuilding)
- Source: Archived Förartjänst.se pricing page (2025)
- Confidence: Medium (was on pricing page before rebuild)

### Bemanning (staffing/temp) — **140–180% of base wage**

- CE driver wage: ~210–250 SEK/hour
- Bemanning markup: ~140–180% = employer pays ~300–450 SEK/hour
- Monthly equivalent (160h): ~48,000–72,000 SEK/month per driver
- This is for temp/bemanningsuppdrag, not permanent hire
- Source: Almega/LO collective agreement data
- Confidence: High

### Jobzone Sweden (jobzone.se) — **Not publicly disclosed (hybrid)**

- Jobzone operates both permanent placement and temp staffing
- Permanent: estimated 15–20% of salary (consistent with market)
- Temp: standard bemanning markup
- Source: Market estimates; pricing not published
- Confidence: Low

## 6. Key Pricing Insights

### DriverNord is priced below global equivalents

At 8,000–10,000 SEK per introduction (Package 4), DriverNord is priced:
- **36–54% below** the UK introduction market (Drivers Hub: ~4,550 SEK top of range is also below, but Staffline is 54,600–98,800)
- **Below the US per-lead fee** when adjusted for purchasing power (O Trucking $500 = ~5,200 SEK, but US drivers are less credentialed per-lead than pre-screened Swedish drivers)
- **~85–94% below** Swedish traditional rekrytering (63,000–105,000 SEK)
- **Competitive with the low end of bemanning** (which provides no permanence)

### Employer willingness to pay is anchored at traditional rekrytering

Swedish CE employers currently pay 63,000–105,000 SEK for a traditional placement. DriverNord at 8,000–15,000 SEK represents an 85–92% cost reduction for equivalent or superior candidate quality. This is the primary pricing anchor to use in B2B conversations.

### Subscription models require volume DriverNord doesn't yet have

FreightPro, Leadgamp, and Trucking Talent all operate subscription models. This only works when the platform has predictable lead inventory. DriverNord's current supply (~300+ ingested drivers as of May 2026) is sufficient for per-introduction pricing but not for a subscription promise.

### Per-hire (success fee) creates collection risk

CDLers charges on hire confirmation. This creates disputes ("we hired someone else first", "the driver didn't show up on day 1"). Avoid this collection trigger.

### The introduction fee model is uncontested in Sweden

No Swedish operator currently charges a flat per-introduction fee for CE drivers. The nearest is the job-posting model (Förartjänst.se). DriverNord can own this pricing category.
