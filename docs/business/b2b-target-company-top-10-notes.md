The CEO is still directly reachable (joachim@ytterstene.se + LinkedIn), but the pitch must acknowledge their scale. A "zero-risk pilot" pitch designed for a 10-truck operation will not land here. Revise to peak-period supplementary sourcing angle. Medium barrier.

Original research listed "Kyl- och Frysexpressen Nord AB" — this is a Umeå-based entity and was incorrect. The Stockholm/Årsta entity is Mälardalen AB (org 556526-4396). This has been corrected in the CSV. The first-50 notes file still contains the wrong name ("Kyl- och Frysexpressen Nord AB") — update the first-50 notes if re-published.

Active Jobwise AB placement for C-chaufförer (confirmed via Jobwise job listing). Also uses Jobtip social recruiting. This means there is an established external sourcing relationship. Two sources give different phone numbers: website shows 08 419 36 090, allabolag shows 08-410 36 90. These may both be valid (different lines) or one may be stale. Do not call until verified. Email first.

**Alexis Bud & Transport AB — title gap**
Josef Markus is listed as styrelseledamot (board member), not VD. There may be a separate operating manager. However, in family-run subcontractors of this size (since 2014, Tumba), the board member is typically the decision-maker. Sara Markus (styrelsesuppleant) is secondary. Treat Josef Markus as primary DM unless direct phone contact reveals otherwise.
