# Persona 1 — "Aktive Johan" — The Active CE Freelancer

**Priority:** V1 PRIMARY — Highest lead quality. Hardest to convert. Worth the effort.
**License:** CE (truck + trailer, full combination)
**Volume in Stockholm/Mälardalen:** ~2,000–4,000 active drivers in this segment
**Acquisition difficulty:** HIGH — skeptical, seen it all
**Lead quality:** VERY HIGH — exactly what transport companies want to pay for

---

## 1. Demographic Reality

**Age:** 33–52. Peak segment: 38–47.
**Gender:** 90%+ male. Occasional woman (roughly 4–6% of CE drivers nationally are female — proportionally more in distribution/kyl than long-haul).
**Region:** Primarily inner Mälardalen radius: Södertälje, Järfälla, Upplands Väsby, Sigtuna, Enköping, Västerås. Some inner Stockholm (Hammarby Sjöstad terminal area, Lunda industrial, Jordbro). Fewer from actual city center.
**Experience:** 8–25 years professional driving. Not a beginner. Has driven everything from flatbed to tanker, has opinions about which is better.
**License:** CE (always). Additional: often ADR (dangerous goods), sometimes HIAB/crane. Driver card (tachograph) valid. YKB valid or renewed within past 2 years.
**Employment type:** Enskild firma (sole trader) — registered at Bolagsverket as AB or enskild firma. OR: between direct employment contracts (voluntarily left, not fired). OR: recently finished a long-term bemanning assignment and refusing to renew through the agency.

**Salary reality:**
- As enskild firma billing companies: 185–260 SEK/hour depending on specialty (HIAB/ADR commands premium)
- As employed: would expect 31,000–40,000 SEK/month gross in Stockholm market (Transportavtalet base + OB + overtime)
- Know their worth. Can calculate what a bemanningsbolag takes in margin.

## 2. Professional Reality

### A typical week
Johan is hauling kyl (refrigerated) on a regional Mälardalen circuit — ICA distribution from Västerås to central Stockholm retail. Up at 04:00. Truck loaded by 05:15. First stop Nacka by 08:00. Done by 14:00 if loading docks cooperate. Usually they don't. Back by 16:00. Total: 10–12 hours including unloading wait.

Or: Johan runs a Hiab crane truck. Construction site deliveries around Stockholm — concrete forms, steel, timber. More stop-start, more coordination, more responsibility. Better rate per hour.

### Current situation
He's either:
- **Just left a company** — gave 30 days notice because management wanted him to take routes he didn't want ("de körde på mig med distribution men jag kör inte paket"), or salary didn't move for 2 years
- **Working as enskild firma** but current main client is winding down a project or cutting back — needs new pipeline
- **Between a rock and a hard place with bemanning** — has been working through Proffice or a local transport staffing company, hates it, but can't get direct contact with the end clients

### What he knows about the market
He knows:
- DB Schenker is hiring but their interview process takes 6 weeks
- DHL pays less than the market rate but is stable
- Axfood Dagab pays well but schedules are hard
- Several smaller construction logistics companies pay very well for HIAB but you have to know someone to get in the door

He does NOT know:
- What a service like DriverNord is (genuinely new concept to him)
- Whether DriverNord will be "another bemanningsbolag with a nice website"

### YKB situation
Valid. Renewed 2023 or 2024. He took the 35 hours at an approved training center — paid himself as enskild firma or negotiated with previous employer. He knows exactly when it expires again.

### Transport sub-sector status hierarchy (his internal ranking)
1. Long-haul international (glamour, freedom, but family sacrifice)
2. HIAB/crane (specialist, well-paid, demanding)
3. Kylfrys/refrigerated (steady clients, strict times, good pay)
4. ADR/hazmat (specialist bonus, few drivers qualified)
5. Bulk/tipp (physical, less clean, decent pay)
6. Regional flatbed (solid, unglamorous, reliable)
7. Schakt/construction (seasonal variation)
8. Pakettransport/distribution (bottom of the hierarchy — "det är inte riktigt lastbilsköring")
9. Systemtransport/pallet networks (anonymous, disposable feeling)

**He does NOT want to be placed in distribution delivery if he's a HIAB specialist.** Getting this wrong destroys the match and the trust.

## 3. Digital Behavior

### Facebook
**Primary discovery and community channel.** Johan is in:
- "Lastbilsförare i Sverige" (50,000+ members)
- "Yrkeschaufförer" (30,000+ members)
- "CE-förare Sverige" (15,000+ members)
- Possibly a regional group: "Transportarbetare Mälardalen" or similar

What he does in these groups:
- Reads others' complaints (validation of his own frustrations)
- Occasionally posts a warning about a bad employer or late payment
- Shares funny truck content (stuck trucks, bad drivers on roads, big loads)
- Asks questions about legal rights (overtime calculation, Transportavtalet interpretation)
- Sometimes tips a colleague about a job

**He does NOT post his availability publicly.** That feels desperate. He tells his network via private message.

**Facebook timing:** Morning 05:00–07:00 (before first load), during loading dock waits 09:00–11:00, late afternoon 15:00–17:00, and weekend evenings 20:00–22:00. **Saturday morning is high engagement time** — he's off, relaxed, scrolling.

### Instagram
Moderate. Follows: truck culture accounts (Nordic Trucking, European truck content), equipment accounts (Volvo Trucks Sweden, Scania Sverige), some humor accounts. Does not engage much — more a consumer than creator.

### TikTok
Low for this age group. Some 38–45 year olds are on TikTok but consume rather than create. Growing usage year over year but not primary channel for V1.

### LinkedIn
Near-zero for this profile. Professional drivers do not maintain LinkedIn profiles and find the platform "lite pretentiöst." Irrelevant for acquisition.

### Job search behavior
1. **Facebook groups** — posts or reads "tips wanted"
2. **Platsbanken** — searches "CE Stockholm" — reads and applies selectively
3. **Personal network** — calls ex-colleagues, asks who's hiring
4. **Direct approach** — drives past a company, looks up the HR contact, sends a WhatsApp
5. **Bemanningsbolag** — absolute last resort, and he resents it every time

**He does not use headhunters. He does not update his LinkedIn. He trusts word of mouth more than any digital channel.**

## 4. Emotional Profile

### Top 3 Frustrations

**1. "Jag skickar ut ansökan och hör aldrig nåt."**
He has applied to 8 companies in the past 6 months. Heard back from 2. Got an interview with 1. This is normal in Swedish transport. He has internalized it as the system being broken, not him being rejected. The silence is infuriating.

**2. "Bemanningsbolagen tjänar på mig utan att ge mig nåt."**
He calculates: the bemanningsbolag charges the transport company 240 SEK/hour for him, pays him 160 SEK/hour, pockets 80 SEK/hour. He resents this intensely. Not because he doesn't understand business — he does — but because the agency provided zero value. They made one phone call. He did all the work. They take 33% forever.

**3. "Ingen vet vad jag kan. De ser bara ett körkort."**
Companies that don't distinguish between a CE-HIAB specialist with 15 years experience and a 25-year-old with a new CE license insult his professional identity. Generic recruitment treats him like a commodity.

### Top 3 Fears

**1. "Vad händer om jag är utan uppdrag i 3 månader?"**
Income gap risk. As enskild firma, he has no unemployment insurance equivalent. A bad 3 months means depleted reserves. He worries about this more than he admits.

**2. "Tänk om det nya stället är sämre än det gamla?"**
Job change risk. Every driver who has left a company and found the new one worse (and this happens constantly) has been scarred by it. He needs some form of quality signal before he commits.

**3. "Vad händer med min data om jag registrerar mig nånstans?"**
Privacy fear. He has received cold calls from bemanningsbolag he never signed up with. He suspects his data circulates. He's right — CV databases are shared.

### Hidden Ambitions

- Wants to be **known by name** at a few good companies — "de ringer Johan när de behöver en bra HIAB-körare"
- Wants to **earn enough to own his own truck** eventually — the enskild firma dream
- Wants **his expertise to be recognized and compensated** appropriately — not as a generic driver but as a specialist
- Wants **stability without sacrifice of autonomy** — the core tension of his professional life

### What He Secretly Wants

He wants a **curated introduction** to 2–3 transport companies that actually need his specific skill set, where the company has been told specifically why he is valuable, and where he is positioned as the expert — not screened like an applicant. He wants to feel like a specialist being recruited, not a worker applying for a job.

DriverNord's model is structurally designed to give him exactly this. The challenge is making him believe it before he has evidence.

### Trust Triggers
- Specific and accurate knowledge of the industry (uses the right words for his specialty)
- Clear explanation of what DriverNord is NOT (not bemanning, not selling his data)
- Consent model explained simply and credibly
- No sales pressure, no "apply now" urgency
- Recommended by a colleague (strongest possible)

### Skepticism Triggers
- Any language that sounds like a recruitment ad
- Vague claims about "thousands of companies" or "hundreds of opportunities"
- Asking for a CV (immediate exit)
- Pushy CTA ("Registrera dig nu!")
- Unclear about revenue model (he will wonder who is paying and how)

## 5. Language Map

### He Says
- "Jag kör CE" — identifies by license, not job title
- "Min rig" / "min dragbil" — his truck (ownership language even when employed)
- "Uppdrag" — assignment (not "jobb" when freelancing)
- "Lossa/lasta" — unload/load (never "unloading operations")
- "Kör kyl" — drives refrigerated (by specialty, not generic)
- "Ekipage" — truck + trailer combination
- "Facket" — the union, Transport
- "Ob-et" — OB supplement (unsocial hours pay)
- "Kollektivavtalet" — the Transport collective agreement
- "Det funkar sådär" — Swedish understatement for "it's shit"
- "En bra firma" — a good company (firm culture matters more than brand)
- "Ta kontakt" — reach out (not "apply")

### Words He Hates in Job Ads
- "Spännande möjligheter" — exciting opportunities
- "Du som brinner för transport" — you who are passionate about transport
- "Ta nästa steg i karriären" — take the next step in your career
- "Samhällsviktig funktion" — socially important function (condescending)
- "Driven och engagerad" — driven and committed (meaningless filler)
- "Erfarenhet är meriterande" — experience is an advantage (so what isn't?)
- "Vi erbjuder en dynamisk miljö" — we offer a dynamic environment
- "Rätt person" — the right person (so vague it says nothing)

### Phrases That Create Recognition
- "CE med YKB och [specialty]" — speaks his identity directly
- "Inga bemanningsbolag" — relieves a core pain
- "Din profil delas aldrig utan ditt godkännande" — specific consent promise
- "Registrering tar 3 minuter" — respects his time
- "Vi matchar på vad du faktiskt kör — inte bara körkortsklassen" — addresses the commodity problem
- "Vi tar betalt av företaget, aldrig av föraren" — clear economic model

### How He'd Describe DriverNord to a Colleague
"Det är en tjänst där transportföretag betalar för att hitta bra förare. Du lägger upp din profil och om det finns ett uppdrag som passar din grej — kyl, HIAB, vad du nu kör — så hör de av sig. Du behöver inte söka aktivt. Och de delar inte din profil utan att du sagt ok."

Note: He would describe it practically, without enthusiasm. The key is that this description is accurate and sounds like something worth trying.

## 6. Acquisition Psychology

### The Moment He's Most Receptive
- Saturday morning, relaxed, coffee in hand, scrolling Facebook
- First 20 minutes after parking at the terminal during a loading wait
- The week after finishing a contract or leaving a company (peak transition moment)
- After complaining in a Facebook group about the recruitment process — he's just articulated the problem himself

### What Stops the Scroll
- An image of a specific truck type he drives (not a stock photo of a random truck — a well-shot HIAB or kyl truck in motion, Swedish roads, recognizable context)
- A headline that names his license class and specialty: "CE-förare med HIAB i Stockholmsregionen"
- A simple, direct statement that names his core frustration: "Trött på att bemanningsbolag tar 30% av vad du är värd?"
- A video of another driver describing DriverNord in plain, unscripted language (UGC — social proof)

### What Creates Curiosity Without Trust Yet
- "3 minuter. Inget CV. Din profil delas aldrig utan ditt godkännande." — low commitment, specific, answers three key objections in one sentence

### What Makes Him Not Click (Despite Stopping)
- Clicking through and finding a corporate-looking website with stock photos
- Being asked to fill out a long form
- Seeing vague promises ("hundreds of opportunities" etc.)
- Any page that looks like a job board

### CTA That Works
- "Registrera dig" (direct but calm — no exclamation mark)
- "Se hur det funkar" (low-commitment curiosity)
- "Kör CE? Registrera din profil →" (identity trigger + action)

### CTA That Fails
- "Ansök nu!" (job application frame)
- "Börja idag!" (artificial urgency, sounds like a scam)
- "Hitta ditt drömjobb" (not credible)

## 7. DriverNord Fit Analysis

**Why This Persona is Strategically Critical**

Johan is exactly who transport companies are willing to pay 8,000–15,000 SEK to access. He is experienced, specialized, has valid YKB, and knows how to work. He is not a bulk commodity — he is a verified professional with a specific skill set. Every "Johan" in the DriverNord database multiplies DriverNord's ability to deliver premium matches.

**Expected Conversion Quality:** 9/10 when matched correctly. The risk is mismatching — placing him in distribution when he's a HIAB specialist destroys the relationship.

**Expected Lead Quality Score:** 8–9/10. High.

**Registration-to-Qualified-Lead Conversion:** ~70–80% of Johan personas who register will be genuinely matchable within 6 months. They are not tire-kickers.

**Long-Term Strategic Value:**
- Will refer colleagues if experience is positive (word-of-mouth multiplier)
- Stays in the database even when employed if managed correctly
- 1 well-served Johan generates 2–4 referrals over 12 months

**Risks:**
- Slow to trust. Will not register on first exposure.
- Will test DriverNord: registers a minimal profile, waits to see if anything real happens
- Will leave the database without notice if contacted poorly
- Expects that when DriverNord says "we'll contact you when there's a match," they actually do

**What DriverNord Needs Before Targeting Johan**
- At least 2–3 real transport companies in Supabase who would pay for a CE-HIAB or CE-kyl introduction
- A contact process that feels like a curated introduction, not a cold call
- The 46elks SMS configured — he will not read emails but will respond to SMS

## 8. Meta Campaign Implications

### Creative Direction

**Image concept:** Realistic shot of a HIAB crane truck on a Stockholm construction site, early morning light, driver visible but not posed. Shot from ground level looking up at the crane arm. Not a stock photo — a real truck in a real Stockholm location. The driver is not smiling. He's working. This is professional pride, not a career poster.

**Video concept (15–30 sec):** A driver (real or actor who looks the part — not a marketing actor in a clean jacket) speaks directly to camera from inside his cab or at a terminal. No studio. No music. He says something like: *"Jag körde i fem år via bemanning. De tjänade mer på mig än jag. Så jag registrerade mig på DriverNord. Tre minuter. Sen kom ett uppdrag som faktiskt passade min profil."* Fast cut. End card: DriverNord logo, "Registrera dig. Gratis. 3 minuter." This is not produced marketing — it's a testimonial that sounds like one.

**Copy tone:** Direct. Short sentences. No adjectives. Technical specificity that signals insider knowledge.

**Best placement:** Facebook Feed (primary), Facebook Groups (boosted posts in transport groups), Instagram Feed.

**Best time:** Saturday morning 08:00–11:00 CET. Weekday 05:30–07:30.

**Meta audience targeting:**
- Location: Stockholm, Uppsala, Västerås, Södertälje, Järfälla (20km radius)
- Age: 33–52
- Interests: Trucking/transport, Volvo Trucks, Scania, professional driver, Facebook groups (transport Sverige)
- Behaviors: Small business owners (enskild firma segment)
- Exclude: Standard bemanningsbolag employees if targetable

**Lookalike source (when available):** Supabase driver registrations with license=CE and YKB=valid — this is the gold standard lookalike.
