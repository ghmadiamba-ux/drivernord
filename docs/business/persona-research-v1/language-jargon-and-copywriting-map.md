# Language, Jargon and Copywriting Map

**Date:** 2026-05-18
**Scope:** Swedish transport sector language for CE/C driver acquisition copy
**Usage:** Every piece of DriverNord driver-facing content must be checked against this document

---

## The Fundamental Rule

**If a Swedish truck driver reads your copy and thinks "that sounds like a recruiter wrote it," you've failed.**

The target is: copy that reads like it was written by someone who has spent years in transport — who knows the routes, the collective agreement, the terminal culture, the grudges. Not a marketing professional pretending.

---

## Section 1 — Authentic Transport Jargon

### License and Certification Terms

| Term | Meaning | Usage context |
|------|---------|---------------|
| **CE** | Truck + trailer (full combination) license | Always use this, not "lastbilskörkort" alone |
| **C** | Rigid truck (no trailer) license | Specify when relevant |
| **D** | Bus/coach license | Less relevant for V1 |
| **YKB** | Yrkeskompetensbevis — professional competence certificate | Core qualifying criterion — use always |
| **Giltig YKB** | Valid YKB | Key filter phrase |
| **Förarkort** | Tachograph card (digital tacho) | Signals insider knowledge when used |
| **Körkortsklass** | License class | More formal, use in explanatory contexts |
| **ADR** | Dangerous goods license (hazmats) | Signals specialist segment |
| **HIAB** | Trade name used generically for crane trucks | Use "HIAB" not "krantransport" in informal copy |
| **Yrkesbevis** | Informal shorthand for YKB sometimes | Less common, avoid |

### Vehicle and Equipment Terms

| Term | Meaning | Authenticity signal |
|------|---------|---|
| **Ekipage** | Full truck + trailer combination | Very insider — use it |
| **Dragbil** | Tractor unit (the front part) | Correct technical term |
| **Dragare** | Informal for tractor unit | Common in conversation |
| **Påhängsvagn / semitrailer** | Semi-trailer (the back part) | Use appropriately |
| **Rig** | Informal for the whole truck setup | Casual, authentic |
| **Flak** | Flatbed trailer/truck | "Kör flak" = drives flatbed |
| **Tipp / Tipper** | Tipper truck | Construction segment |
| **Tanker / Tankbil** | Tanker truck | ADR/fuel segment |
| **Kran** | Crane truck | "Kör kran" = drives crane truck |
| **Kyl** | Refrigerated truck | "Kör kyl" — common identity phrase |
| **Frys** | Frozen-goods truck (colder than kyl) | Precision matters — they're different |
| **Kylfrys** | Refrigerated/frozen combined | |
| **Bockvagn / boggie** | Bogie/double-axle trailer | Technical, signals expertise |

## Section 2 — The Collective Agreement Vocabulary

Swedish drivers under Transportavtalet know these terms. Using them correctly signals DriverNord understands their rights and context:

| Term | What it is | Copy use |
|------|-----------|---|
| **Kollektivavtalet / Transportavtalet** | The Transport sector collective agreement | Reference it directly when relevant |
| **Facket / Transport** | The union (Transportarbetareförbundet) | Not all drivers are members but all know the union |
| **Ob-ersättning** | Unsocial hours supplement (nights, weekends) | Critical to salary calculation |
| **Semesterlön** | Holiday pay | Relevant in job comparison |
| **Traktamente** | Per diem / subsistence allowance | Long-haul critical |
| **Övertidsersättning** | Overtime compensation | |
| **Körpenning** | Per-kilometer bonus (historical, some contracts) | Less common now |
| **Lönerevisionen** | Annual salary revision | When salary discussions happen |
| **Tjänstepension** | Occupational pension | Important to 35+ drivers |

Mentioning these correctly signals market knowledge:

- Dagab — ICA's logistics arm, well-paid, demanding hours

## Section 4 — Words and Phrases That Kill Conversion

These phrases appear in standard job ads and recruiter communications. They have conditioned CE/C drivers to scroll past or exit. **Never use these in DriverNord copy:**

### Recruiter/Corporate Clichés
| Phrase | Why it fails |
|--------|---|
| "Spännande möjligheter" | Sounds like every recruiter ever. Immediate scroll-past. |
| "Ta nästa steg i karriären" | They're not thinking about "careers." They're thinking about routes, pay, and management. |
| "Du som brinner för transport" | Absurd. They don't "burn" for transport. They drive trucks. |
| "Vi söker dig som är driven och engagerad" | Overused filler. Zero signal value. |
| "Välkommen till oss!" | Premature and fake-warm. |
| "Bra villkor" / "Attraktiv lön" | Vague. Triggers: "so what are the actual numbers?" |
| "Lön enligt överenskommelse" | Evasive. They know this means "we'll lowball you." |
| "Samhällsviktig funktion" | Condescending. Transport workers don't need to be told their work matters. |
| "Rätt person" | So vague it's meaningless. |
| "Dynamisk miljö" | Marketing speak. No transport professional believes this. |
| "Möjlighet till personlig utveckling" | They develop by driving better trucks, not by attending workshops. |
| "Karriärutveckling" | Mostly irrelevant. Their career is their license and expertise. |
| "Team-player" | Drivers work alone. This signals the ad writer doesn't know transport. |

### Recruitment Industry Language
| Phrase | Why it fails |
|--------|---|
| "Vi tar hand om dig" | Paternalistic. They don't want to be taken care of. |
| "Vi hjälper dig lyckas" | Condescending. They already know how to do their job. |
| "Stor databas av arbetsgivare" | Sounds like a job board. Could be another Platsbanken. |
| "Kontakta oss för mer information" | Friction. They won't. |
| "Ansök nu" | Job-application frame. Wrong for a matching service. |
| "Submit your CV" (in English even if ad is Swedish) | Not authentic, not Swedish |
| "Registrera dig gratis" | Sounds like a lead gen scam. "Gratis" in this context feels suspicious. |

### False Urgency Language
| Phrase | Why it fails |
|--------|---|
| "Sista ansökningsdag..." | Artificial scarcity — they know this. |
| "Begränsat antal platser" | Inauthentic. |
| "Missa inte denna chansen!" | Feels like a lottery ad. |
| "Nu söker vi aktivt!" | When aren't you? |

## Section 5 — Phrases That Create Instant Recognition

These phrases pass the transport authenticity test — they sound like they were written by someone who understands the world:

| Phrase | Why it works |
|--------|---|
| "CE med YKB i Stockholmsregionen" | Specific. Accurate. No one outside the industry writes this. |
| "Du kör — vi hittar rätt uppdrag." | Short. Active voice. Accurate description. |
| "Inga bemanningsbolag. Inga påtryckningar." | Names the enemy. Creates relief. |
| "Din profil delas aldrig utan ditt godkännande." | Specific promise. Consent model stated correctly. |
| "Inget CV. 3 minuter." | Removes two objections simultaneously. |
| "Vi matchar baserat på vad du faktiskt kör — inte bara körkortsklassen." | Signals specificity. Honors expertise. |
| "Du bestämmer om du vill gå vidare." | Autonomy framing. Critical. |
| "Vi tjänar pengar när du hittar ett uppdrag — aldrig på din arbetstid." | Honest revenue model. Differentiates from bemanning. |
| "Jobbar du via bemanning och vill prova nåt annat?" | Directly addresses the bemanningsbolag pain. |
| "Söker du inte aktivt — men är öppen?" | Validates the passive position. Gives permission to register. |

## Section 6 — How a Driver Talks About DriverNord to a Colleague

This matters. If the natural word-of-mouth description is accurate and positive, DriverNord has authentic social proof potential.

**Target description:** What we want a satisfied driver to say:
> "Det är en tjänst där transportföretag betalar för att hitta erfarna förare. Du lägger upp din profil — körkortsklass, vad du kör, när du är tillgänglig — och om det finns ett uppdrag som passar, hör de av sig. De delar inte din profil med nåt företag utan att du godkänt det. Det är gratis för förare. Tog 3 minuter att registrera mig."

**Contrast with what we do NOT want:**
> "Det är nåt rekryteringsprogram, tror jag. De ringer väl upp en sen."

The difference: the target description is accurate, specific, and includes the consent model. The bad version is vague and implies unwanted contact. The /chat registration experience and all post-registration communication must be designed to produce the first description.

## Section 7 — Platform-Specific Language Calibration

| Platform | Tone | Length | Key style notes |
|----------|------|--------|---|
| Facebook Feed | Direct, slightly informal | 50–150 words for caption | No bullet points in first 3 lines (hidden behind "see more"). Hook must work in first sentence. |
| Facebook Groups | Conversational, not promotional | 80–200 words | Sound like a group member, not a company. Ask questions. |
| Instagram Feed | Slightly more visual-led | 30–80 words | Image does more work. Caption amplifies. Hashtags at end. |
| Instagram Reels | Spoken word | 15–30 seconds | Script in natural spoken Swedish, not written Swedish. |
| Platsbanken | Standard job ad conventions | Standard format | Must follow Platsbanken conventions while differentiating |
| SMS (post-registration) | Very short, respectful | 1–2 sentences | Never promotional. Only transactional. "Vi har hittat en match — vill du veta mer?" |
