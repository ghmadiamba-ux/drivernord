## Acquisition Funnel Comparison

```
                      Johan (P1)         Marcus (P2)        Young (P4)
                      ─────────────      ─────────────      ─────────────
Impression            100%               100%               100%
Stop scroll           ~8%                ~15%               ~20%
Click to /chat        ~3%                ~7%                ~10%
Start registration    ~65% of clickers   ~75% of clickers   ~80% of clickers
Complete /chat        ~75% of starters   ~65% of starters   ~70% of starters
Qualified lead        ~80% of completers ~65% of completers ~40% of completers

```

**Key insight:** Marcus (P2) generates almost 2x the qualified leads per 1,000 impressions compared to Johan (P1), despite lower individual lead quality. This is why he is the core volume persona even if Johan is the premium persona.

## Recommended Campaign Sequencing

### Phase 1 (V1 launch — first 60 days)
- **Primary spend:** Marcus (P2) — broad awareness and registration volume
- **Secondary spend:** Johan (P1) — targeted, smaller budget, high-intent Facebook group placements
- **YKB overlay:** Apply seasonally (autumn/spring windows)
- **Young CE:** Organic-only (no paid)

### Phase 2 (V1 scale — days 61–180)
- **Primary spend:** Marcus (P2) — scale what works from Phase 1
- **Johan (P1) upgrade:** Increase budget once testimonials exist and trust mechanics are proven
- **YKB seasonal push:** Dedicated autumn campaign if Phase 1 data supports it
- **Young CE:** Begin considering if supply-side gaps emerge

### Phase 3 (V2)
- All personas with dedicated budgets
- YKB targeting as standalone campaign with full seasonal calendar
- TikTok campaigns for Young CE persona
- Lookalike audiences built from qualified completions in Supabase
