# DriverNord Persona Research V1 — Executive Summary

**Date:** 2026-05-18
**Purpose:** Strategic intelligence for V1 driver acquisition in Stockholm/Mälardalen
**Scope:** CE/C/D driver personas relevant to Meta Ads, organic social, and funnel conversion
**Status:** Pre-commercial — zero drivers in database, unknown brand

---

## The Single Most Important Finding

**The Swedish CE driver market has a recruitment infrastructure problem, not a supply problem.**

There are approximately 8,000–12,000 active CE-licensed professional drivers in Stockholm and Mälardalen. They are not hard to find — they are hard to reach in a way that doesn't insult them. Every standard recruitment channel (Platsbanken, Monster, bemanningsbolag cold calls, mass email) is associated with friction, opacity, and bad faith. DriverNord's competitive advantage is not technology — it is the *model*: consent-based, direct, no-margin staffing skimmed off the driver's value.

The opportunity is not to invent a new channel. It is to speak to drivers who are already skeptical of existing channels, in a way that sounds nothing like those channels.

---

## The Market in 2026

- **Transport sector employment** is tight in Stockholm. CE driver shortage is structural — an aging workforce, slow YKB renewal uptake among younger workers, and high attrition to long-haul European routes.
- **Bemanningsbolag** (staffing agencies: Randstad, Proffice, Manpower Transport, Vårdlänken Logistics, smaller local players) dominate CE matching — and are broadly disliked by experienced drivers.
- **Platsbanken** is used but passive — drivers post CVs or apply, then wait. Response rates are perceived as poor.
- **Facebook groups** ("Lastbilsförare i Sverige", "Yrkeschaufförer", "Svenska Lastbilsförare") are the primary informal labor market — where drivers share job tips, warn each other about bad companies, and seek references.
- **Förartjänst.se** was the main CE-specific matching service but is in a rebuild phase — this is the competitive window.

## The Two V1 Acquisition Personas

### Persona 1 — "Aktive Johan" (The Active CE Freelancer)

The enskild firma or recently-between-jobs CE driver who manages his own career and is actively in market. Has valid YKB. Specializes in a specific transport domain (HIAB, kylfrys, ADR, flatbed, bulk). **This is DriverNord's highest-value supply-side asset.** Small in volume but extremely high lead quality. Hardest to convert because he has seen every recruitment trick and is appropriately skeptical. Wins over slowly — but stays in the database.

**Priority:** V1 PRIMARY. Acquire first, even if slow.

### Persona 2 — "Missnöjde Marcus" (The Dissatisfied Employed Driver)

The CE or C driver currently employed — at a company or via bemanning — who is not actively looking but is one bad week away from it. Knows something is off (salary, routes, management) but hasn't mobilized. The largest volume segment. Converts at lower rates but at scale. Needs a "low friction" registration message that doesn't ask him to commit to job searching — just to "exist in the right database."

**Priority:** V1 SECONDARY. Highest volume. The core of any paid campaign.

## Two Optional Personas

### Optional Persona 3 — "YKB-Perioden" (The YKB Renewal Window)

Not a demographic — a timing trigger. Any CE/C driver within 12 months of YKB expiry or who just renewed is at peak career-reflection moment. This window applies as an *overlay* on Personas 1 and 2. A separate campaign trigger, not a standalone persona. Addressed in `optional-persona-3.md`.

### Optional Persona 4 — "Det Nya CE-kortet" (Young C-to-CE Aspirant)

The 22–32 year old new CE holder. Less skeptical, TikTok-active, future-high-value. Deprioritized for V1 because conversion quality is lower (less specialized, less in-demand) and they require different creative. Addressed in `optional-persona-4.md`.

## Key Research Conclusions

**1. Identity comes before job search.**
Swedish CE drivers do not think of themselves as "job seekers." They think of themselves as "CE-förare med [specialty]." Every message that puts them in the job-seeker frame gets rejected. Every message that puts them in the professional-expert frame gets read.

**2. Anti-bemanningsbolag sentiment is the strongest universal hook.**
No other topic generates as consistent a negative emotional response across all CE/C driver segments as the bemanningsbolag experience. "Vi är inte ett bemanningsbolag" is the single most important trust statement DriverNord has.

**3. Privacy and consent are non-negotiable, not a differentiator.**
Drivers expect their data will be misused. They've been burned. The consent model is not a selling point — it is the price of admission to their trust. It must be stated clearly and early, but stating it alone is not enough.

**4. The phone number step is the highest drop-off risk in the funnel.**
Swedish drivers are extremely wary of cold calls from unknown recruiters. The fear is: "jag ger mitt nummer och nästa dag ringer 5 bemanningsbolag." This must be pre-emptively addressed in the acquisition copy, in the /chat flow, and in the post-registration communication.

**5. Facebook is the primary channel. TikTok is the growth channel.**
For 35+ year-old drivers: Facebook is where they discover, discuss, and decide. For under-30 drivers: TikTok truck content is exploding — Sweden has a growing "trucker TikTok" subculture. Meta (Facebook/Instagram) is the V1 channel. TikTok is worth organic exploration for V2.

**6. Timing matters enormously.**
The best moment to reach a driver is: during loading/unloading breaks (05:30–07:30, 10:00–12:00), weekend evenings (Saturday/Sunday 19:00–22:00), and the week after a holiday break when drivers reassess their situation. YKB renewal windows are the single best targeting trigger.

## The Biggest Mistake to Avoid

**Sounding like a recruiter.**

Every word that sounds like standard recruitment copy — "spännande möjligheter," "ta nästa steg i din karriär," "vi söker dig som är driven och engagerad" — destroys credibility instantly with the CE driver demographic. They have been subjected to this language for 20 years and have developed an allergic response to it.

DriverNord's copy must sound like it was written by someone who has spent time in a truck cab, at a terminal loading dock, and knows what a CMR document is. Not by a marketing team.

---

## Recommended First Campaign Emotional Strategy

**Frame:** *Relief from an absurd system*

Not inspiration. Not career opportunity. Not "find your dream job."

The emotional frame is: *"The way recruitment works in transport is broken and everyone knows it — here's the first thing that works differently."*

This frame:
- Validates existing frustration (creates instant rapport)
- Positions DriverNord as the solution (without over-promising)
- Does not require DriverNord to have a reputation yet (the system's badness is the reference point, not DriverNord's goodness)
- Works equally well for Persona 1 and Persona 2
