# Optional Persona 4 — "Det Nya CE-kortet" — The Young CE Aspirant

**Recommendation:** Deprioritize for V1. Worth an organic content awareness play but NOT a paid acquisition focus.
**Priority for V1:** LOW for paid. MEDIUM for organic brand building.
**Age range:** 22–33
**License:** Recently acquired CE (within 3 years), or currently holding C and actively pursuing CE

---

## Why This Persona Exists

A meaningful number of young drivers in Stockholm/Mälardalen are in career-building mode — they have their C or CE, they're hungry for good work, and they don't carry the 15 years of recruitment scar tissue that older drivers have. They are less skeptical, more exploratory, and consume digital content very differently.

This segment is strategically interesting because:
1. They are the future supply base — someone who registers at 27 may drive for DriverNord clients for 25+ years
2. They are less resistant to new digital services (no bad recruitment history to overcome)
3. They are on TikTok and Instagram Reels — platforms where DriverNord could build organic reach cheaply

---

## Profile

**Work situation:** Often working their first 1–3 CE jobs. May be at a bemanning company because it was the easiest entry point. Often driving distribution or pakettransport (the lower-status segments) while building hours and reputation. Wants to move to better-paying specialty work (HIAB, kyl, ADR, long-haul) but doesn't yet have the contacts or experience to break in.

**Salary reality:** 24,000–30,000 SEK/month. Knows this is lower than the market for experienced CE drivers. Wants to build toward 32,000–38,000.

**Digital behavior:**
- TikTok: real presence. Swedish truck driver TikTok content (route videos, equipment showcase, loading humor) is growing. Creators in this space get 10,000–100,000 views on good content.
- Instagram Reels: more than any other driver demographic
- Facebook: uses it, but it feels like the "old platform" to him. Still in relevant groups.
- Follows: Scania, Volvo Trucks, truck accessory brands, route content creators

**Emotional profile:**
- Wants to be taken seriously as a professional despite age
- Eager to learn specialty skills (HIAB, ADR) but doesn't know how to access those opportunities
- Less cynical about recruitment services — hasn't been burned yet
- Responds to aspirational content: "how I went from C to CE specialist in 3 years"
- Status-conscious about license class and specialty — wants peers to know he runs something serious

---

## Why NOT a V1 Priority

**Lead quality is lower.** Transport companies paying 8,000–15,000 SEK for an introduction want experienced, specialized CE drivers. A 25-year-old with 8 months CE experience and no specialty is harder to place, even if the driver is genuinely talented.

**Matching is harder.** Without specialty (HIAB/ADR/kyl), the young CE driver competes for the same distribution routes as dozens of others. DriverNord's value proposition ("we match you on what you actually drive") is weaker when there's no specialty to match.

**The pipeline is longer.** This persona needs 1–2 years of experience before they become premium leads. Registering them early builds the database for future use, but delivering value to clients with young CE drivers is difficult in V1 when trust is still being built.

---

## When to Activate This Persona

**V2 or when:**
- DriverNord has a supply-side problem (not enough experienced CE drivers) and needs to build depth
- A transport company client asks for "motivated CE drivers who want to learn specialty" — this happens
- DriverNord has enough brand recognition to attract organic applicants (vs. paid acquisition)
- TikTok organic content program is underway and growing

---

## One Organic Tactic Worth Trying Now (Even in V1)

A single TikTok/Instagram Reels series aimed at young drivers — not selling DriverNord explicitly, but building brand awareness in the segment. Content idea: **"Hur tar du steget från distribution till specialtransport?"** (How do you go from distribution to specialty transport?) — 3-part series from a driver's perspective.

This builds awareness, brand association with "CE progression," and an audience for future retargeting. Zero paid spend required. Possible if someone on the team can produce authentic-looking short video content.

---

*Verdict: Deprioritize for V1 acquisition spend. Track organically. Revisit when first 20 transport companies are paying clients and supply-side depth becomes the constraint.*
