# Acquisition Strategy by Persona

**Date:** 2026-05-18
**Scope:** V1 acquisition strategy for Stockholm/Mälardalen CE/C driver supply

---

## Persona 1 — Johan (Active CE Freelancer)

### Channel Mix
| Channel | Role | Budget allocation |
|---------|------|---|
| Facebook Feed (paid) | Primary reach | 35% |
| Facebook Groups (organic + boosted) | Community trust-building | 25% |
| Platsbanken (free) | Passive capture | 0% (free) |
| Word of mouth | Referral multiplier | 0% (earned) |
| Instagram Feed | Secondary reach | 20% |
| Cold outreach (0%) | Never | 0% |

### Funnel Approach

**Touch 1 — Awareness (Facebook Feed):**
Stop-scroll with identity-based hook. CE-specific language, specialty acknowledgment. No immediate CTA. Goal: recognition and curiosity. Video or image ad that signals DriverNord knows the transport world.

**Touch 2 — Consideration (Facebook Groups / Retargeting):**
Retarget video viewers with a consent-focused explanation post. "Here's exactly how DriverNord works — no bemanningsbolag, no data sharing without approval." FAQ-style content that answers his objections before he asks.

**Touch 3 — Conversion (Registration):**
Soft CTA: "Registrera din profil — 3 minuter, inget CV." Only after trust is partially established. Johan does not convert on a cold ad — he needs 2–4 exposures minimum.

**Touch 4 — Referral activation:**
When Johan completes a match: send a personal SMS (not automated template). Ask if he'd mention DriverNord to colleagues. Offer nothing in exchange — that would insult him. Just ask.

### Posting Frequency
- Facebook paid: 3–5 ad variations running simultaneously (avoid ad fatigue in small audience)
- Facebook organic/groups: 2–3 posts per week, not every day (respect community norms)
- Rotate angles every 3 weeks to avoid creative fatigue

### Expected Cost-per-Qualified-Lead
At Facebook CPM of 80–120 SEK for transport-interested audience:
- ~3% click rate → ~7% /chat start rate → ~70% completion → ~80% qualified
- Rough estimate: **250–500 SEK per qualified CE freelancer lead**
- This is acceptable given clients will pay 8,000–15,000 SEK per placement

### Key Conversion Triggers
1. CE + specialty language in headline (HIAB, kyl, ADR — named specifically)
2. Consent model stated explicitly and early
3. "Not a bemanningsbolag" stated early
4. Zero CV requirement stated early
5. Colleague recommendation (if earned organically)

## Persona 2 — Marcus (Dissatisfied Employed Driver)

### Channel Mix
| Channel | Role | Budget allocation |
|---------|------|---|
| Facebook Feed (paid) | Primary reach + volume | 50% |
| Instagram Feed (paid) | Secondary reach | 25% |
| Instagram Reels | Under-40 reach | 15% |
| Facebook organic | Awareness + trust | 10% |
| Platsbanken | Passive (free) | 0% (free) |

### Funnel Approach

**Touch 1 — Awareness (Facebook/Instagram paid):**
Volume-first. Broad audience. Hook targets the "not looking but not happy" position. Message: "You don't have to search actively. Just register and we'll come to you if there's a match."

**Touch 2 — Consideration (Retargeting):**
Retarget anyone who clicked but didn't register. Address the most common exit point: "It takes 3 minutes. No commitment. No pressure." Use a simpler, more direct version of the registration CTA.

**Touch 3 — Conversion:**
Mobile-optimized /chat registration. The landing page IS the registration flow — no intermediate marketing page that adds friction. Marcus converts best when the path from ad click to "registration complete" is under 4 minutes total.

**Touch 4 — Re-engagement:**
For Marcus who registered but hasn't been contacted yet: periodic SMS (quarterly maximum) with one of: (a) a question about his availability update, (b) a relevant market insight ("CE driver demand is up 15% in Q4 in Stockholm — is your profile up to date?"). No spam.

### Posting Frequency
- Facebook paid: 5–8 ad variations (higher volume needed for broader audience)
- New creative every 4–6 weeks (Marcus has shorter fatigue cycle than Johan)
- Sunday premium slot: ensure ad delivery is optimized for Sunday 19:00–22:00

### Expected Cost-per-Qualified-Lead
At Facebook CPM of 60–100 SEK for employed transport worker audience:
- ~7% click rate → ~10% /chat start rate → ~65% completion → ~65% qualified
- Rough estimate: **120–250 SEK per qualified employed CE/C driver lead**
- Higher volume at lower per-unit cost than Johan

### Key Conversion Triggers
1. Low-commitment framing in headline
2. Salary reality check ("do you know what you should be earning?")
3. Concrete time claim ("3 minutes")
4. No CV required
5. Mobile-optimized, fast registration experience

## Platsbanken Strategy (Free Channel)

Maintain a DriverNord presence on Platsbanken for passive discovery:
- Post a "driver seeking" style listing once per month: "DriverNord söker CE-förare i Stockholm" — not a job ad but a registration invitation
- Cost: 0 SEK
- Reach: Low but free quality traffic from actively searching drivers
- Note: Platsbanken listings look like job ads — copy must quickly differentiate DriverNord from employers

### Sample Platsbanken headline
*"DriverNord — Matchar CE-förare med transportföretag i Stockholm. Registrering gratis. Inget CV. Du godkänner alltid innan din profil delas."*

## Facebook Groups Strategy

### Target groups
- "Lastbilsförare i Sverige" (50,000+ members) — largest
- "Yrkeschaufförer" (30,000+ members)
- "CE-förare Sverige"
- Regional transport groups in Mälardalen

### Approach
1. **Never lead with an ad.** Groups have explicit rules against marketing posts. Admins ban commercial accounts.
2. **Founder presence:** The founder of DriverNord should participate genuinely in these groups for 4–6 weeks before posting anything about DriverNord. Comment on discussions. Answer questions about collective agreements, YKB, routes. Build recognition.
3. **Organic mention:** After building presence, post a transparent introduction: "Vi har byggt en tjänst för CE-förare. Ingen bemanning. Gratis att registrera. Vill höra vad ni tycker om konceptet." Open question, not a pitch.
4. **Boost organic posts:** Facebook allows boosting organic posts to people similar to those who engage — use this to amplify genuine group discussions without violating group norms.

### Expected outcome from groups
Group organic mentions generate the highest-quality leads of all channels — because they come with social proof ("someone in this group vouches for it"). Even 5–10 quality group mentions can generate 30–50 registrations with very high qualified rates.
