# Optional Persona 3 — "YKB-Perioden" — The YKB Renewal Window

**Recommendation:** Do NOT treat as a standalone persona. Use as a TARGETING OVERLAY on Personas 1 and 2.
**Priority for V1:** HIGH as an overlay trigger. LOW as a separate campaign.

---

## Why This Is Not a Separate Persona

"YKB-Perioden" describes a timing condition, not a demographic type. A driver in the YKB renewal window can be Johan (Persona 1) or Marcus (Persona 2) — they share the same professional identity and emotional profile. What changes is the *timing of their receptivity*.

A CE/C driver whose YKB expires within 12 months, or who just renewed it, is at a distinct psychological moment:

1. **They are thinking about their professional future.** YKB renewal forces an explicit decision: invest in this career, or let it lapse? The 35-hour training (and 5,000–12,000 SEK cost) is not trivial.
2. **They have just spent money on their career.** If they paid themselves (enskild firma) or negotiated with their employer, they have a fresh investment in their professional identity. A service that helps them monetize that investment has natural appeal.
3. **If the employer didn't pay for renewal, resentment is fresh.** This is an extraordinarily effective window for DriverNord: "You just renewed your YKB at your own cost — let us help you make sure you're with a company that values that."
4. **If they're about to let it lapse, they're at a crossroads.** A driver letting YKB expire is exiting professional transport. DriverNord cannot convert them — but can confirm that leaving the database is appropriate.

## The YKB Renewal Cycle

- YKB validity: 5 years from issue
- The 35-hour renewal training can be spread over the 5-year cycle or taken all at once before expiry
- Training centers: TÅ (Transport & Arbete), MHF, many local driving schools
- Cost paid by employer: common for larger companies under Transportavtalet
- Cost paid by driver: common for enskild firma drivers, bemanning workers (agency often refuses to pay)
- The Swedish Transport Agency (Transportstyrelsen) sends no reminder — drivers must track this themselves
- A significant minority (~15–20% of CE drivers nationally) let YKB lapse and then must re-train fully — a more expensive process. These drivers are exiting the professional market temporarily.

## How to Use This as a Campaign Layer

### Timing targeting
Facebook allows broad interest targeting but not YKB-specific triggers. However:
- **Seasonal patterns**: YKB renewal tends to cluster in autumn (October–November) when transport companies plan the next year's compliance, and in spring (March–April). Running YKB-themed campaigns in these windows increases relevance.
- **Content hook**: A YKB-specific hook used during these windows converts better than at other times.

### YKB-specific hooks that work

**For Persona 1 (Johan) who just renewed:**
> "YKB-förlängning klar? Bra tillfälle att se till att rätt transportföretag vet vad du kan."
> *Translation: "YKB renewed? Good time to make sure the right transport companies know what you can do."*

**For Persona 2 (Marcus) whose employer didn't pay:**
> "Betalade du YKB-kursen själv? Vi hjälper dig hitta arbetsgivare som värdesätter din kompetens."
> *Translation: "Did you pay for the YKB course yourself? We help you find employers who value your competence."*

**For the driver thinking about letting it lapse:**
This segment is not DriverNord's target. No campaign resources here.

## When to Make This a Standalone Campaign

Consider a dedicated YKB campaign after:
- 20+ drivers registered and confirmed YKB status data in Supabase
- At least 2 matches completed via DriverNord
- Meta Pixel has enough conversion data to optimize an audience

At that point, run a retargeting campaign specifically targeting CE/C drivers who engage with transport content in September–October with a YKB-specific message. This is a V2 tactic.
