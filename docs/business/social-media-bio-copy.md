### Content direction for feed

Per the research, Instagram Feed ads for driver acquisition should:
- Use real trucks in real Swedish industrial locations (Södertälje, Jordbro, Huddinge, Rosersberg)
- Overcast or early-morning light — NOT golden hour or studio
- If people appear: work clothes, realistic age (35–45), not smiling at camera
- Text overlays (white on dark): short, direct, insider language
- Aspect ratio: 1:1 for feed, 9:16 for Reels

For Reels (targeting Marcus P2, under-40 segment):
- Spoken-word format, text on screen
- 15–30 seconds max
- Script in natural spoken Swedish, not formal written Swedish
- Mobile-shot quality is more authentic than produced quality

## Content voice rules for all social posts

These rules apply to all organic posts on both platforms:

1. **Never sound like a company posting about itself.** Sound like someone in transport talking to other people in transport.

2. **Never use the word "ansök" in any form.** Not "ansök nu", not "ansökan", not "ansökningsprocess".

3. **Always name the license class.** "CE-förare" not "chaufförer" generically.

4. **Anti-bemanning references are permitted and encouraged** — but describe the problem, don't attack competitors by name.

5. **Consent model appears in every outreach post** — "Din profil delas aldrig utan ditt godkännande" is not a disclaimer. It is the headline trust signal.

6. **No urgency language.** No "nu", no "sista chansen", no "begränsat antal platser".

7. **One CTA per post.** Not two. Not a link AND "message us". One destination.

## Facebook Groups posting rules

Separate from Page posts — organic group posts require different handling:

- **Do not post from the DriverNord Page account** in most transport Facebook groups. The founder posts under their own name.
- **First 4–6 weeks:** Founder participates in group discussions without mentioning DriverNord.
- **First DriverNord mention:** Transparent, conversational, asking for feedback — not a pitch.

**Sample first group post (founder's voice):**
"Hej, jag driver en tjänst som heter DriverNord — vi matchar CE-förare direkt med transportföretag i Stockholm, utan bemanningsbolag inblandade. Vill höra vad ni tycker om konceptet innan vi kör igång ordentligt. Gratis för förare, 3 minuter att registrera sig. Är det här något som skulle vara intressant för er?"

**What not to post:**
- Sales copy
- Urgency language
- Anything that looks designed
