# DriverNord — Distribution Doctrine

*Effective: 2026-06-16. Applies to all content, platform, and acquisition work.*

---

## Core Principle: Production and Distribution Must Move Together

Every product or system improvement must create a distribution action.
Every distribution action must feed the worker database or the client pipeline.

**If a technical task produces no supply, no trust signal, no distribution asset, and no commercial trigger, ask whether it should be deprioritized.**

---

## What Distribution Must Do

Distribution for DriverNord serves four simultaneous functions:

1. **Worker acquisition (supply side)** — attract logistics workers to register at drivernord.com/chat
2. **Brand trust (both sides)** — establish DriverNord as a credible logistics capacity network before first commercial transaction
3. **Client awareness (demand side)** — make transport companies aware that DriverNord exists and what it offers
4. **Community signal capture** — observe what the market is actually asking for (which roles, which regions, which pains)

---

## Required Channels

### Facebook/Meta — NOT Optional

Facebook Page (`DriverNord` at drivernord.com) must become actively posting. This is required, not optional.

**Why Facebook is primary:**
- Swedish logistics workers and small transport company owners are active on Facebook
- Organic content builds trust before paid campaigns
- Logistikklubb community infrastructure is already built on a WhatsApp CTA from the Facebook page
- Paid Meta Ads can be activated later — organic must run first

**Minimum posting cadence:** At least 2 posts per week. Content types:
- Worker acquisition posts (chauffeurs, lagerpersonal, truckförare — "Är du förare? Registrera dig")
- Community/industry posts (market news, CE driver shortage context, logistik tips)
- Trust posts (what DriverNord does, how verification works, client testimonials when available)
- Logistikklubb membership invitations

**Worker categories to include in content:**
- CE/C/D chauffeurs
- Distributionsförare
- Truckförare (A, B, C, D truckkort)
- Lagerpersonal
- Terminalpersonal
- Orderplock
- Lastning/lossning

**Do not limit Facebook content to CE/D drivers.** The community is broader. Content that only speaks to CE/D drivers misses the full logistics workforce that DriverNord is building supply from.

### Logistikklubb (drivernord.com/logistikklubb)

The Logistikklubb page is live. It serves as:
- A community entry point for logistics workers who are not yet ready to register as candidates
- A trust-building mechanism (industry news, content, discussion)
- A soft pipeline — workers who join the community are pre-qualified for future registration

**Content must now support all worker categories, not only heavy transport drivers.**

**WhatsApp CTA** on the Logistikklubb page routes to a WhatsApp community managed by the founder.

### Platsbanken / Arbetsförmedlingen

- Posting open positions (when supply gap exists) generates both applications and market signals
- The market agent already scans Platsbanken for company needs — postings from DriverNord should appear here when relevant

### Organic search / SEO

- drivernord.com must be discoverable for:
  - "CE förare jobb Stockholm"
  - "truckförare jobb"
  - "logistik personal Stockholm"
  - "bemanning chaufför"

### Direct client outreach (demand side)

- Outreach to transport companies must not happen until worker supply exists (minimum viable shortlist)
- When outreach starts, it introduces DriverNord as a logistics capacity network with three paths, not only shortlists

## Distribution-Production Loop

Every production task should have a distribution connection. Examples:

| Production task | Distribution action |
|----------------|-------------------|
| Add truckförare intake to /chat | Post on Facebook: "Truckförare? Registrera dig" |
| Add lagerpersonal category | Logistikklubb post: "Jobbar du på lager? Vi söker dig" |
| Run agency scan → find chronic CE shortage signal | Facebook post: "Stor brist på CE-förare i Stockholm" |
| First client paid shortlist delivered | Social proof content: client testimonial (with permission) |
| New pilot company interested | No public post — private pipeline only |
| Logistikklubb market news relay | Post auto-generated and approved by founder → Facebook |

## Content That Feeds Both Supply and Trust

The most efficient content does two things at once:

**Example:** "Vi söker CE-förare med giltigt YKB i Stockholm. Registrera dig på drivernord.com/chat och vi matchar dig med rätt jobb."
- Acquires workers (supply)
- Shows clients that DriverNord has an active pipeline (demand trust)

**Example:** "Stor brist på C- och CE-förare i Stockholmsregionen. Företag väntar på rätt kandidater. DriverNord löser det."
- Shows market awareness (trust)
- Invites clients to contact (demand)
- Invites workers to register (supply)

## Facebook/Meta Organic Activation Checklist

Before first paid ad:

- [ ] Facebook Page has ≥10 published posts
- [ ] At least 3 posts directly invite workers to register
- [ ] At least 2 posts explain what DriverNord offers to companies
- [ ] Logistikklubb WhatsApp CTA is visible on page
- [ ] Page profile is complete (description, contact, website link)
- [ ] At least one post per worker category (chaufför, truckförare, lagerpersonal)

Paid ads can begin after this checklist is complete.

---

## What Distribution Is NOT

- Cold email blasts to companies without worker supply
- Paid ads before organic trust is established
- Outreach to individuals without consent infrastructure
- Automated posting without founder approval of content

---

## Agent-Distribution Integration

The AI system must connect distribution to market intelligence:

- **Agency scan signals** → Facebook/Logistikklubb content signal ("shortage in X sector")
- **Platsbanken data** → content signal ("these roles are chronically unfilled")
- **Worker registrations** → supply confirmation → triggers readiness to post "we have CE drivers available"
- **Client inquiries** → demand signal → if unmet by supply, triggers acquisition post

The Logistikklubb admin panel already has a 5-day AI content plan feature. This should be expanded to cover all worker categories, not only CE drivers.
