# DriverNord — Offer Positioning Against Competitors

**Date:** 2026-05-14
**Purpose:** Define exactly how DriverNord's Package 2 (Verifierad Förarlista) and Package 4 (Akut Förarsökning) are positioned against each identified alternative. Include the objections each competitor triggers and the responses DriverNord can give.
**Governing rule:** Positioning based on verified market data only. No claims made about DriverNord outcomes not yet demonstrated.

---

## The Core Positioning Logic

DriverNord's V1 model occupies a specific position that no current competitor holds:

**"Flat fee. Verified drivers. Delivered in 48 hours. You pay when we deliver — not when you hire."**

This position is defensible because:
1. No Swedish competitor offers verified, consented CE driver introductions at a flat, transparent fee
2. The flat-fee-on-delivery model removes the "pay and hope" risk of job board ads
3. The verification differentiates from raw directory access (Chaffis) and unverified job board applications (Yrkesförarjobb)
4. The speed differentiates from agency recruitment (3–8 weeks vs. 48 hours)
5. The price differentiates from agency fees (15,000 SEK vs. 63,000–126,000 SEK per hire)

## Positioning Against Each Competitor

### 1. vs. Chaffis.se (4,995 SEK/year)

**What Chaffis offers:**
- Annual access to a self-service driver directory
- Automated matching on submitted attributes
- No verification, no availability confirmation, no consent chain

**The Chaffis buyer:**
A company willing to spend 4,995 SEK/year for search access and hoping to find a good driver organically.

**DriverNord's positioning:**
*"Chaffis gives you access. We give you drivers."*

| Dimension | Chaffis | DriverNord Package 2 |
|-----------|---------|---------------------|
| Price | 4,995 SEK/year | 15,000 SEK per shortlist |
| What you get | Search access to self-registered drivers | 3–5 matched, verified, available, consent-confirmed drivers |
| Verification | None | License + YKB structured confirmation |
| Availability | Self-reported; not confirmed at contact time | Confirmed within 7 days of delivery |
| Consent | Unknown | Per-company explicit consent |
| Outcome guarantee | None | Replacement policy (TBD) |
| Time to result | Unknown — search + contact by company | 3–5 business days from order |

**Price objection: "Chaffis is only 4,995 SEK — why pay 15,000?"**
Response: "Chaffis gives you names to contact. We give you drivers who are available, have the right license, and have agreed to hear from you specifically. The difference is the outcome, not the access."

**Key vulnerability:** If a buyer is very cost-sensitive and willing to do their own contact work, Chaffis is a rational alternative at 4,995 SEK. DriverNord must justify the 3x price premium with demonstrably better driver quality and less buyer effort.

### 2. vs. Yrkesförarjobb.se (5,900 SEK/ad)

**What Yrkesförarjobb offers:**
- One job ad posting to a transport-specific audience
- Impressions and organic applications from interested drivers
- No verification, no screening, no qualification

**DriverNord's positioning:**
*"5,900 SEK buys you applicants. 8,000–15,000 SEK buys you drivers who are ready."*

| Dimension | Yrkesförarjobb | DriverNord Package 4 | DriverNord Package 2 |
|-----------|---------------|---------------------|---------------------|
| Price | 5,900 SEK/ad | 8,000–10,000 SEK | 15,000 SEK |
| What you get | Applications from interested candidates | 1 named driver ready within 48h | 3–5 shortlisted verified drivers |
| Qualification | None — applicant self-selected | CE + YKB confirmed | CE + YKB confirmed |
| Speed | Ads run for weeks; applications trickle | 48 hours | 3–5 business days |
| Urgency fit | Poor (no urgent response) | Designed for urgent need | Planned hire |
| Guarantee | None | None yet (design needed) | None yet |

**When the buyer prefers Yrkesförarjobb:** When they have time, want to manage the process themselves, and are willing to screen applications.

**When the buyer prefers DriverNord:** When they have an acute shortage, need one driver this week, and don't want to manage a screening process.

**Price objection: "I can run a Yrkesförarjobb ad for 5,900 and get applications."**
Response: "Yes — you'll get applications. You'll then need to filter them, call them, and discover which ones actually have a valid CE+YKB and are genuinely available. That process takes 1–3 weeks. Package 4 gives you one ready driver in 48 hours. What is one week of lost capacity worth to your operation?"

### 3. vs. Swedish Recruitment Agencies (Manpower, Randstad, Lernia, Rekryteringsgruppen)

**What agencies offer:**
- Full recruitment service: sourcing, screening, presenting candidates
- Large CV databases
- Human judgment and relationship management
- Satisfaction guarantee (Rekryteringsgruppen: 10 days)
- Standard fee: 15–30% of gross annual salary (~63,000–126,000 SEK for one CE driver)

**DriverNord's positioning:**
*"We deliver in 3–5 days. They deliver in 3–8 weeks. We charge 15,000 SEK. They charge 63,000–126,000 SEK."*

| Dimension | Major Agency | DriverNord Package 2 |
|-----------|-------------|---------------------|
| Price | 63,000–126,000 SEK (15–30% of salary) | 15,000 SEK introductory |
| Time to shortlist | 1–3 weeks | 3–5 business days |
| Time to hire | 3–8 weeks | Buyer-dependent (after introduction) |
| Payment trigger | On hire | On shortlist delivery |
| Minimum viable | Mid–large company | Any company |
| Customization | High — recruiter manages process | Standardized — algorithm-driven |
| Relationship | Human account management | Digital delivery |
| Guarantee | Replacement within 3–6 months | Not yet defined |
| Price transparency | None — requires conversation | Stated publicly |

**When the buyer prefers an agency:** When they want full process management, have a complex hire (senior operations manager, not just a CE driver), and have budget for the full service. Or when the hire failed and they need the replacement guarantee.

**When the buyer prefers DriverNord:** When they need qualified CE drivers specifically, have a clear need, want to know the price upfront, and can't afford or justify 63,000–126,000 SEK per hire.

**Objection: "We work with Manpower / Randstad already."**
Response: "We're not replacing them for senior hires. We're a faster, cheaper alternative for CE driver introductions specifically. If they delivered your last CE driver in 6 weeks, we would have had a shortlist ready in 5 days."

**Objection: "What's the guarantee if the driver doesn't work out?"**
Response: This is a genuine weakness in Phase 1. DriverNord must define its guarantee policy before competing head-to-head with agency guarantees. See `best-practice-playbook.md` for recommendation.

### 4. vs. Simplex Bemanning / Staffing Agencies (Model C)

**What staffing agencies offer:**
- Deployed drivers the next day — they employ the driver and send them to the client
- No hire commitment — flexible, hourly billing
- Full employer responsibility for the driver

**DriverNord's positioning:**
*"Staffing costs you 832,000 SEK/year per driver. A permanent hire via DriverNord costs you 15,000 SEK once, then 420,000–450,000 SEK/year in direct salary."*

| Dimension | Staffing Agency (Simplex) | DriverNord Package 2 |
|-----------|--------------------------|---------------------|
| Cost per driver/year | 832,500 SEK (450 SEK/h × 1,850h) | 15,000 SEK + 450,000 SEK salary |
| Annual saving vs. staffing | — | ~367,500 SEK per driver hired |
| Commitment | None — hourly | Full employment (via client) |
| Speed | Same-day or next-day | 3–5 business days |
| Urgency fit | Best (same-day) | Good (48–120 hours) |
| Flexibility | Maximum (scale up/down hourly) | Fixed (permanent hire) |
| Employer risk | Agency bears it | Client bears it after hire |

**When the buyer prefers staffing:** When the need is temporary (<3 months), variable (seasonal), or they can't commit to a hire.

**When the buyer prefers DriverNord:** When they need a driver for 6+ months and the economics of permanent hire are better than staffing. ROI is clear: 15,000 SEK introduction vs. 367,500 SEK annual staffing premium.

**Best positioning approach for Segment E (staffing conversion buyers):**
Present the ROI calculation directly: "You're paying 832,000 SEK/year to staff one full-time CE driver. For 15,000 SEK, we introduce a driver you hire at 450,000 SEK/year. Payback: 15 days."

### 5. vs. Förartjänst.se (Currently Dark)

**What Förartjänst offers when operational:**
- Large driver pool (claims 20,000+)
- Per-company consent model (best design observed in Swedish market)
- Major logistics company partnerships (DSV, DHL, HAVI, ICA, Martin Servera)

**Current competitive risk:** LOW — platform is rebuilding in 2026 and has no live functionality.

**Future competitive risk:** HIGH — if Förartjänst relaunches with verified profiles, per-company consent, and competitive pricing, they will be DriverNord's closest direct competitor.

**DriverNord positioning vs. Förartjänst (when relaunched):**
The differentiator will be verification quality, AI-driven matching, and pricing transparency. DriverNord's `buildShortlist()` algorithm is more sophisticated than a directory. But Förartjänst's driver pool of 20,000+ will be a volume advantage that DriverNord cannot match in Phase 1.

**Strategic implication:** Build market presence and client relationships before Förartjänst relaunches. The window is now.

## Positioning Statement (V1)

**For transport companies that need to hire qualified CE drivers:**

*DriverNord delivers a shortlist of 3–5 verified, available, consent-confirmed drivers in 3–5 business days for 15,000 SEK — compared to 63,000–126,000 SEK and 3–8 weeks via traditional recruitment, or 5,900 SEK per ad with no quality guarantee via job boards.*

*For urgent needs: one named, available driver within 48 hours for 8,000 SEK.*

## What DriverNord Must Prove on the First Sale

The positioning above is a claim, not yet a fact. The first 3 sales must demonstrate:
1. Driver is actually available when delivered (not a stale profile)
2. Driver has the claimed license and YKB
3. Driver responds when the company contacts them (consent model works)
4. The introduction leads to a productive conversation (not necessarily a hire — that is the company's decision)

If these four things are true for the first 3 shortlists, the positioning becomes evidence-based rather than claimed. Until then, it is a promise.
