# DriverNord — Competitor Business Model Map

**Date:** 2026-05-14
**Purpose:** Map all companies operating in the Swedish professional driver acquisition and placement space, plus international analogues. Identify what model each uses, what problem they solve, and where the gaps are.
**Governing rule:** Public data only. No contact. No legal conclusions stronger than the evidence supports. All assessments marked with confidence level.

---

## Model Taxonomy

Before mapping companies: four distinct business models exist in this space. Every company operates one or more of these.

| Model | Definition | Revenue logic | Who bears employer risk |
|-------|-----------|---------------|------------------------|
| **A — Directory / Job Board** | Driver self-registers; company searches or posts; platform collects listing or subscription fee | Per listing, per ad, or annual subscription | Neither party — platform is neutral |
| **B — Permanent Placement (Rekrytering)** | Agency finds and presents candidates; client hires; agency charges fee | % of annual salary or flat fee; one-time per hire | Client (after hire) |
| **C — Staffing / Bemanning** | Agency employs driver; client rents driver hourly; agency invoices client | Hourly bill rate with markup; ongoing recurring revenue | Staffing agency (ongoing) |
| **D — Verified Introduction (proposed DriverNord model)** | Platform qualifies, consent-confirms, and presents matched drivers; client pays per shortlist or per introduction; client hires directly | Flat fee per shortlist or per introduction delivered | Client (after hire) |

**Key distinction:** Model D does not currently exist as a marketed product in the Swedish transport sector. This is a market gap, not a proven model. See `strategic-conclusion.md` for full analysis.

## International Analogues

### 11. CDL Lead Source (USA)

**Model:** A — Raw Lead List Sales
**Confidence:** HIGH (website verified)

| Dimension | Data |
|-----------|------|
| Product | Raw CDL driver contact data (name, phone, location) |
| Pricing | 500 contacts: $199 / 1,000: $299 / 3,000: $499 |
| Cost per lead | $0.17–$0.75 |
| Quality claim | 70–80% answer rate |
| Consent | NOT consented introductions — raw contact data from public sources |
| Outcome guarantee | None |
| Equivalent in DriverNord terms | A "worse Chaffis" — no consent, no verification, pure data list |

**Why this model is inferior to DriverNord's:** No driver consent, no credential verification, no availability confirmation. Buying a list is not delivering a shortlist.

## Model Gap Analysis

| Model | Exists in Sweden? | Who serves it | Price transparency | Consent design | Verification | Flat fee |
|-------|-----------------|--------------|-------------------|----------------|-------------|---------|
| A — Directory | Yes | Chaffis, Förartjänst, Yrkesförarjobb | Partial (Chaffis: 4,995/year) | None or unknown | None | Yes |
| B — Permanent Placement | Yes | All major agencies | None public | None documented | Standard agency screening | None (% of salary) |
| C — Staffing / Bemanning | Yes | Simplex, Tranpenad, Manpower, Randstad, Lernia, Rekryteringsgruppen | None public | None documented | Operational (tachograph, license) | None (hourly) |
| D — Verified Consented Introduction | **NO** | **NONE** | N/A | N/A | N/A | N/A |

**Finding:** Model D — a paid shortlist of verified, available, consent-confirmed CE drivers at a transparent flat fee — does not exist as a marketed product in Sweden.

This is the gap DriverNord proposes to occupy.
