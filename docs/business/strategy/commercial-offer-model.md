# DriverNord — Commercial Offer Model

*Effective: 2026-06-16. Supersedes Package-only framing in earlier monetization documents.*

---

## Three Commercial Paths

DriverNord offers clients three entry points. These are not strictly sequential — a client may enter at any path depending on their need.

---

### A. Matchning / Kortlist

**What:** DriverNord identifies and presents a curated shortlist of qualified logistics workers matching the client's specific need (license, YKB, region, availability, domain).

**How it works:**
- Client communicates the need (via recruiter intake or future self-service form)
- AI matches against the registered worker database
- Shortlist of 3–5 verified, scored candidates is prepared
- Founder reviews and approves before delivery
- Client receives the shortlist with candidate profiles

**Who it's for:** Transport companies, logistics operators, fleet owners who need to find qualified drivers or logistics staff without using a traditional agency.

**Pricing logic:**
- Package 4 "Akut Förarsökning": 8,000–10,000 SEK — urgent single-driver delivery within 48h
- Package 2 "Verifierad Förarlista": 15,000 SEK intro (3–5 drivers); raise to 18,000 SEK after 3 sales
- Payment on shortlist delivery, not on hire

**Client commits to:** DPA (signed before any profile is shared); service agreement; payment within 30 days.

**Operational prerequisite:** Worker database with ≥3 relevant workers per category.

---

### B. Rekrytering / Introduktion

**What:** DriverNord helps clients identify, screen, and recruit relevant logistics personnel for permanent or long-term positions. The client hires the worker directly.

**How it works:**
- Client need is captured with more detail (headcount, role specifics, timeline, culture)
- AI-assisted screening of candidates from the database
- Introduction facilitates direct dialogue between client and candidate
- DriverNord's role ends at verified introduction; employment is direct client-candidate

**Who it's for:** Companies seeking permanent hires, where the cost of a wrong hire is high and they want a verified, pre-qualified introduction rather than a CV stack.

**Pricing logic:**
- Flat-fee introduction model (avoids % of salary disputes)
- Pricing to be confirmed per mission; range: 15,000–25,000 SEK per introduction
- Can bundle with Package 2 delivery for efficiency

**Operational prerequisite:** DPA template (shared data), service agreement, legal review of introduction model. Worker database supply.

**Note on scope:** This covers the same worker categories as Matchning — chauffeurs, lagerpersonal, truckförare, terminalpersonal.

---

### C. Bemanning / Logistikpersonal

**What:** DriverNord provides logistics staffing capacity for short, urgent, or recurring needs. DriverNord is the formal employer; the worker is hired out to the client company.

**How it works:**
- Client has a short-term or recurring need that doesn't justify a permanent hire
- DriverNord supplies a pre-vetted, employed logistics worker
- Worker is directed by client on-site; DriverNord handles employer administration
- Invoice is issued per hour or per assignment

**Who it's for:** Transport companies with peak-load needs, sick-leave replacement, seasonal capacity, and recurring logistics coverage.

**Pricing logic:**
- Hourly billing with employer cost markup (salary + arbetsgivaravgift + insurance + margin)
- Pricing TBD based on first mission and market benchmarking
- Recurring client relationships possible; builds predictable revenue

**What the founder handles before first live staffed mission:**
- Formal employer registration as bemanningsföretag (if not already registered)
- Applicable collective agreement (Bemanningsavtalet via Almega + transport union)
- Fora insurance (group accident insurance, non-occupational accident)
- Payroll system (Fortnox or equivalent)
- Employment contracts for assigned workers
- Work environment responsibility documentation
- Insurance for work-related injury liability

**What the system must support now (before bemanning is active):**
- Worker registration with all relevant categories (not only CE/D drivers)
- Supply database that includes lagerpersonal, truckförare, terminalpersonal
- Matching against logistics roles, not only heavy transport
- Availability and shift-type capture for all worker types
- Administrative hooks for future invoicing integration

**Operational status:** Planned commercial path. Not yet active. Founder handles legal/operational prerequisites before first mission. The AI system should prepare supply and tooling without blocking on bemanning being live.

## Commercial Entry Doctrine

When approaching a client, DriverNord does not pitch only one product. The appropriate path depends on the client's situation:

| Client situation | Recommended entry |
|----------------|------------------|
| Urgent single-driver gap | Package 4 (Matchning) |
| Needs several verified candidates | Package 2 (Matchning) |
| Wants to hire permanently | Rekrytering / Introduktion |
| Recurring or peak-load capacity | Bemanning / Logistikpersonal (future) |
| Unsure of need | Discovery conversation → recruiter recommends path |

**Prospecting must no longer only offer "driver leads" or "shortlists."** Future outreach should introduce DriverNord as a logistics capacity network with multiple entry points.

---

## What Is NOT a Commercial Offer

These are intelligence or community assets, not commercial products:

- Agency signal data (staffing company posting patterns)
- Logistikklubb community membership
- Organic Facebook/Meta content

These feed supply, trust, and market intelligence — not direct revenue.

---

## Pricing Principles

1. **Flat fee, not % of salary** — avoids disputes, simplifies invoicing
2. **Payment on delivery** — shortlist or introduction delivered; invoice issued
3. **Below agency, above self-service** — confirmed competitive at 8,000–25,000 SEK range
4. **Price to value, not to cost** — CE driver shortage means clients will pay for certainty
5. **First 3 sales at intro pricing** — then raise to validated market rate
