# DriverNord — Agency Partnership Model

*Created: 2026-06-16*
*Type: Business strategy and pricing design. No code. No outreach.*

---

## A. Executive Recommendation

DriverNord should pursue **one narrowly defined agency partnership track**, tested with one partner, before building any infrastructure around it.

**The recommended first offer:**
Position DriverNord as a **niche candidate source** for specialist transport profiles that agencies struggle to fill quickly — specifically CE kylfrys, CE ADR, and CE schakt/bygg in the Stockholm region. Charge a fixed fee per accepted, consent-captured profile.

**Do not:**
- Attempt to partner with agencies whose hidden client you are actively trying to identify as a direct prospect
- Approach more than one agency simultaneously in the first round
- Build a full agency CRM before testing whether agencies will pay

**The single best first move:**
Contact Rekryteringsgruppen i Stockholm AB once DriverNord has ≥3 CE drivers registered. They have C/CE distribution demand, no known exclusive supplier, and represent a clean first test of agency willingness to pay.

**Why not Jobwise first:** Jobwise has an active client (Kyl-och-Frysexpressen) that DriverNord wants to approach directly for CE. Do not compromise that direct prospect by entering a supplier relationship with Jobwise first.

## C. Collaboration Models

### Model A — Complementary Sourcing Partner

**When it makes sense:**
Agency has an active client need in a specialist category (CE kylfrys, CE ADR, CE schakt/bygg) and their own pool cannot fill it within the required timeframe. DriverNord has at least 1–2 matching verified profiles.

**What DriverNord provides:**
- 3–5 pre-screened, consent-captured candidate profiles
- License, YKB, and availability verified
- Structured profile (no CV) — license type, domain experience, shift preference, region, availability date
- Candidate has been told they may be introduced to a staffing agency (consent scope must include this)

**What the agency gets:**
- Fast access to profiles it cannot source internally
- Maintains its client relationship (DriverNord never contacts the agency's client)
- Predictable cost (fixed per profile or per batch)
- No obligation to hire DriverNord's candidates — they pay per accepted profile

**Risks:**
- Agency "cherrypicks" one candidate then disappears; DriverNord has no leverage post-delivery
- Agency forwards profiles to their clients without attribution, then denies placement
- Candidate is frustrated if multiple agencies approach them with the same role
- Race-to-bottom pricing if agency frames DriverNord as just another CV provider

**Mitigation:**
- Written agreement before any profile is shared
- Fee triggered at acceptance (agency confirms profile is relevant), not at placement
- Non-bypass clause: if agency places the candidate, full fee applies regardless
- DriverNord tracks which candidates are introduced to which agencies (system)

**Operational requirements:**
- At least 3 matching registered workers
- Simple written partner agreement (1–2 pages)
- Candidate consent explicitly covering agency introduction
- Profile delivery format agreed in advance (email PDF or portal; not live DB access)

---

### Model B — Niche Transport/Logistics Candidate Supplier

**When it makes sense:**
Agency's primary recruitment challenge is in rare/specialist profiles that DriverNord specifically focuses on: CE+ADR Tank, CE+kylfrys domain, CE schakt/bygg, truckkort specialist, lagerpersonal with specific equipment experience.

**What DriverNord provides:**
- Verified specialist profiles with documented rare qualifications (ADR Tank, FRC kylfrys, specific truckkort types)
- Depth of qualification documentation that generalist sourcing cannot match
- Regional specificity (Stockholm primary)

**What the agency gets:**
- Access to a pool they cannot build efficiently themselves
- Differentiation in client delivery (can promise "CE+ADR Tank within 5 days" if DriverNord has supply)
- Reduces time-to-fill on specialist mandates

**Risks:**
- DriverNord's specialist pool is small initially; delivery risk is high
- Agency may overpromise to client based on DriverNord's pipeline that isn't fully built yet
- Reputational risk if delivery fails

**Mitigation:**
- Start with supply that already exists in the database before making any delivery promises
- Never commit to a timeline without confirming candidate availability first
- Start with Model A (complementary), graduate to Model B as supply grows

**Operational requirements:**
- Specialist pool of ≥5 candidates in the relevant category before offering this model
- Strict qualification documentation (ADR certificate number, truckkort type, domain evidence)
- Delivery SLA agreed in writing (e.g., 3 working days for 3 profiles, or money back)

---

### Model C — Success-Fee Recruitment Partner

**When it makes sense:**
Agency prefers to pay only on successful placement. DriverNord accepts this risk in exchange for a higher fee per placement. Appropriate when the agency is reluctant to pay per profile but has high placement volume.

**What DriverNord provides:**
- Unlimited profile introductions within the agreed scope
- Candidate is available for employer contact
- DriverNord tracks and verifies placement status

**What the agency gets:**
- Zero upfront cost — only pay if they place and bill their client
- No financial risk

**Risks:**
- DriverNord takes all the risk
- Agency may use DriverNord profiles as "backup" and only pay when their primary source fails
- Placement tracking is complex — agency controls the process; DriverNord cannot verify independently
- Cash flow: payment comes weeks or months after profile delivery
- Agency has no incentive to prioritize DriverNord candidates

**Verdict:**
Do not lead with this model. Only offer it as a concession if an agency refuses per-profile pricing after a relationship has been tested. Even then, require a small upfront commitment (e.g., 2,000 SEK retainer applied against success fee).

**Operational requirements:**
- Robust tracking of which candidates were introduced to which agency
- Clear written definition of "successful placement" (start date, not offer acceptance)
- Non-bypass clause (agency cannot hire candidate direct and avoid fee)
- 30-day placement window after introduction

---

### Model D — Fixed-Fee Shortlist Provider

**When it makes sense:**
Agency has a specific mandate ("we need 5 CE distribution Stockholm") and wants a defined deliverable for a fixed price. Clean, transactional, easy to invoice.

**What DriverNord provides:**
- A batch of 3 or 5 profiles matching agreed criteria
- Delivered within 3–5 working days
- Each profile: license, YKB, availability, domain, region, shift preference, consent status

**What the agency gets:**
- Predictable cost
- Defined deliverable
- Easy to budget for

**Risks:**
- DriverNord must have the supply ready; cannot promise delivery without confirmed candidates
- If delivered profiles are rejected (wrong criteria), dispute about payment

**Mitigation:**
- Clear written brief from agency before commitment
- Profiles are "relevant but not guaranteed placed" — fee is for delivery of qualified profiles, not for placement
- If fewer than the agreed number of matching profiles exist, reduce the fee proportionally

**Verdict:**
This is the recommended model to lead with — simple, clean, easy to test.

**Operational requirements:**
- 3–5 matching registered workers per batch
- Written brief (3–5 criteria) agreed before delivery commitment
- Invoice template ready

---

### Model E — Subcontracted Sourcing Mission

**When it makes sense:**
Large agency wins a major contract requiring 10+ logistics workers in a specific category and hires DriverNord as a sourcing subcontractor. DriverNord handles sourcing; agency handles client delivery.

**What DriverNord provides:**
- Active sourcing (recruiting, not just supplying from existing pool)
- Screening and qualification
- Structured profile delivery

**What the agency gets:**
- Capacity without internal investment
- Faster delivery on large mandates

**Risks:**
- DriverNord would know agency's client (major confidentiality issue)
- DriverNord is positioned as subcontractor, not partner — margins are compressed
- Requires DriverNord to actively recruit on demand, not just match from pool
- Creates dependency, not partnership

**Verdict:**
Do not pursue until DriverNord has an established supply base AND a reason to accept subcontractor positioning. This model reduces DriverNord's strategic position. Only pursue if the fee is high enough to justify the subcontractor role (minimum 3,500 SEK per delivered profile to cover sourcing cost).

---

### Model F — Future Bemanning Capacity Partner

**When it makes sense:**
DriverNord has its own employed workforce (bemanning operational prerequisites complete). Agency has a peak-load or sick-leave replacement need. DriverNord supplies an employed worker to the agency's client via the agency.

**What DriverNord provides:**
- An employed logistics worker, directed by agency's client on-site
- Worker is on DriverNord's payroll; agency invoices client with markup; DriverNord invoices agency

**What the agency gets:**
- Capacity relief without the hiring risk
- Can offer their clients wider coverage

**Risks:**
- Tri-party employment is complex
- DriverNord's margin is compressed (agency takes a cut)
- Worker is directed by two layers of management

**Verdict:**
Not available until DriverNord completes bemanning operational prerequisites (Bemanningsavtalet, Fora, payroll). After that — this is a legitimate revenue model. Tag as FUTURE in system.

---

### Model G — No-Contact Intelligence-Only Track

**When it makes sense:**
Agency's primary value is market signal, not partnership. Their postings reveal hidden-client demand, regional patterns, and chronic unfilled categories.

**What DriverNord gets:**
- Market intelligence via `agency_posting_signals` table
- Hidden-client identification leads
- Supply gap signals that drive acquisition campaigns

**What agency gets:**
- Nothing — they are observed, not engaged

**Verdict:**
All agencies start here. Simplex and Arena Personal stay here until hidden clients are identified or confirmed inaccessible. JKT stays here until research is complete.

## D. Pricing Table

### Guiding Principles

1. **Charge from day one.** A free pilot trains agencies to expect free sourcing. A paid pilot (even small) tests real willingness to pay.
2. **Per-profile acceptance triggers payment** — not delivery, not placement. Agency confirms profile is relevant → invoice issued.
3. **Below traditional recruitment, above zero.** The fee must be low enough that the agency's margin on their client placement makes it obviously worthwhile. A CE driver placement earns an agency 25,000–60,000 SEK in margin. DriverNord's profile fee should feel like a rounding error.
4. **Specialist profiles command a premium.** ADR Tank, CE kylfrys, specific truckkort types are genuinely harder to source. Price reflects that.
5. **No free substitutions.** If agency rejects a profile after it's delivered based on criteria DriverNord met, the fee still applies.

### Price Table

| Model | Product | Scope | Price (SEK) | Notes |
|-------|---------|-------|------------|-------|

### Price Positioning Rationale

A Stockholm CE driver placement is worth 25,000–60,000 SEK to a transport agency (markup on hours billed to client, or recruitment fee from client). DriverNord's per-profile fee at 3,000–5,000 SEK is 5–15% of that value. This is the pricing logic to use in any agency conversation:

> "Om ni placerar en CE-förare på ett 6-månaders uppdrag, genererar det troligen 30 000 – 50 000 SEK i marginal för er. Vår profilavgift på 3 000 – 5 000 SEK per profil är en marginalkostnad i det sammanhanget."

### What NOT to price

- Do not offer free profiles as a "test" — it signals no value and creates a precedent
- Do not match agency counter-offers below 2,000 SEK per accepted profile — the economics break
- Do not offer a monthly retainer before at least 3 successful paid deliveries

## E. Recommended First Offer

### Offer Name (Swedish)
**"DriverNord Kandidatkälla — Verifierade transport- och logistikprofiler på beställning"**

Sub-tagline: *Specialiserade CE- och C-förare med YKB, kylfrys-vana och schakt/bygg-erfarenhet — Stockholm.*

### Target Agency Type
Specialist transport/logistics agencies in Stockholm, 20–150 employees, with recurring mandates for CE/C drivers that they struggle to fill within 5–10 days.

Specifically: **Rekryteringsgruppen i Stockholm AB** as first test.

### Target Profiles (what DriverNord delivers)
- CE + YKB, Stockholm distribution (primary — highest supply probability)
- CE + YKB + kylfrys domain, Stockholm (secondary — highest agency demand)
- C + YKB, Stockholm distribution (tertiary — broader pool)

Not included in first offer: ADR Tank, truckkort, lagerpersonal (until intake is expanded and supply exists).

### What Is Included
- 3–5 structured candidate profiles per delivery
- Each profile contains: license type, YKB status, domain experience, shift preference, Stockholm sub-region, earliest availability date, consent status
- Candidate has been informed that their profile may be shared with a recruitment/staffing agency for matching purposes
- DriverNord confirms profile is not simultaneously introduced to another agency for the same mandate (exclusivity per mandate, not per candidate)

### What Is NOT Included
- CV or personal documents
- Employment (DriverNord is not the employer in this model)
- Guarantee of placement
- Interview scheduling
- Agency's client relationship management
- Reference checks (agency handles)
- Candidate's contact information before agency confirms acceptance and payment commitment

### Agreed Before Any Profile Is Shared
1. **Written partner agreement** (1–2 pages) covering: fee structure, acceptance definition, non-bypass clause, GDPR handling, exclusivity scope
2. **Candidate consent scope confirmation**: agency confirms they understand candidates have consented to agency introduction (DriverNord documents this; agency cannot claim they received data without consent)
3. **Profile criteria brief**: agency provides written criteria (license, YKB, region, domain, shift) before DriverNord commits to delivery
4. **Acceptance mechanism**: agency commits to confirming acceptance within 5 working days of profile delivery; silence = non-acceptance (no fee)
5. **Payment terms**: invoice on acceptance confirmation, 20-day payment terms

### Pilot Structure
- First batch: 3 profiles, 6,000 SEK flat
- If agency accepts ≥2 profiles and proceeds: 1 profile in the next batch is free (effective standard rate for that batch)
- If agency accepts 0 profiles: DriverNord investigates criteria mismatch — either brief was unclear or supply doesn't match yet; no fee applies
- After first successful batch: move to standard pricing (3,000–5,000 SEK per accepted profile)

## G. Risks and Guardrails

### Risk 1 — Candidate Data Without Consent
**Risk:** Sharing a candidate profile with an agency when the candidate only consented to being introduced to transport companies directly — not to staffing agencies.

**Guardrail:** The `/chat` intake must capture consent that covers agency introduction explicitly. The consent step must say something like: "Dina uppgifter kan delas med matchande arbetsgivare eller rekryteringspartners för relevant jobberbjudanden." Until consent is updated to include this scope, no profiles can be shared with agencies.

**Owner:** Founder — update consent text with lawyer before first agency profile share.

### Risk 2 — DriverNord Misrepresented as Licensed Staffing Company
**Risk:** An agency tells their client "we have a staffing partner who will supply workers" — but DriverNord is not yet a licensed bemanning company and cannot legally employ workers for hire.

**Guardrail:** In all agency communications and agreements, DriverNord is described as a **kandidatkälla** (candidate source) — not a staffing company, not an employer, not a bemanning partner. The distinction must be explicit. Model F (bemanning capacity partner) is only available after operational prerequisites are complete.

### Risk 3 — Direct Client Conflict
**Risk:** Agency relationship is established with Jobwise → DriverNord then approaches Kyl-och-Frysexpressen (Jobwise's client) → Jobwise feels betrayed → relationship destroyed.

**Guardrail:** Map all known agency-client relationships before establishing any agency partnership. Do not enter a partner agreement with an agency whose client you are actively pursuing. The system's `agency_posting_signals` table with hidden-client fields exists for this purpose. Founder must review this map before any agency contact.

### Risk 4 — Same Candidate Introduced to Multiple Agencies for Same Role
**Risk:** Candidate is introduced to Jobwise AND Rekryteringsgruppen for CE distribution Stockholm. Both agencies approach the same end client. Client is annoyed; agencies fight over who gets the fee; candidate is in an awkward position.

**Guardrail:** Per-mandate exclusivity: once a candidate profile has been sent to an agency for a specific mandate, do not send the same candidate for the same mandate to another agency. Track in system which candidate has been introduced to which agency for which mandate. Exclusivity is per mandate, not per candidate globally — the same candidate can be introduced to different agencies for different roles.

### Risk 5 — Profile Rejection Dispute
**Risk:** Agency receives 3 profiles, uses 2 of them, then disputes whether they "accepted" the profiles within the meaning of the agreement.

**Guardrail:** Define "acceptance" clearly in the written agreement: *"Acceptans innebär att byrån bekräftar att profilen är relevant för ett aktivt uppdrag och vidarebefordrar profilen till en kund eller kandidatprocess. Bekräftelse sker skriftligen (e-post räcker) inom 5 arbetsdagar från leverans."* If the agency places a candidate introduced by DriverNord without confirming acceptance — the full fee still applies (non-bypass clause).

### Risk 6 — Pricing Race to Bottom
**Risk:** Agency counters with "we get candidates for free from LinkedIn — why pay you 3,000 SEK?"

**Response:** "Då konkurrerar ni med 171 andra aktiva CE-annonser i Stockholm som alla hämtar från LinkedIn. Vi levererar verifierade profiler med bekräftat YKB, körkortstyp och tillgänglighet — som vi kan nå inom 5 dagar. Det är det ni betalar för." Do not go below 2,000 SEK per accepted profile. At that price the economics barely work.

### Risk 7 — GDPR Data Chain Liability
**Risk:** DriverNord shares candidate data with agency; agency fails to handle it correctly (stores indefinitely, forwards without basis, shares with client without agreement).

**Guardrail:** The written partner agreement must include a GDPR data sharing clause: agency is a data controller for the data once received; they must delete profiles not used within 30 days; they cannot share with third parties without DriverNord's confirmation that consent covers that scope. DriverNord is not liable for agency's misuse after delivery, but must document the agreement. Lawyer review recommended before first delivery.

## H. Product/System Requirements

*No code in this section — these are product requirements for future Claude Code implementation.*

### 1. Separate Agency Pipeline (not standard client outreach)

Agencies must never enter the standard `company_need_drafts → promotion → outreach` pipeline. The existing `hold_agency` gate handles this at the draft level. A separate partner pipeline is needed for agencies that have been approved as partners.

### 2. Partner Status Field

The `agency_posting_signals` table (or a linked `agency_partners` table) needs a `partner_status` field:

```
partner_status: 
  'potential_partner'   — candidate for partnership, not yet active
  'active_partner'      — agreement in place, active relationship
  'intelligence_only'   — observe market signals, no contact
  'competitor'          — do not approach
  'unknown'             — research required before classification
```

### 3. Hidden Client Fields (already partially in system)

The existing `agency_posting_signals.source_context` JSON and `commercial_classification` field provide the foundation. Add explicit fields:

```
end_client_name:        TEXT | NULL     — identified end client name if known
end_client_confirmed:   BOOLEAN         — true if identification is verified
end_client_target_id:   UUID | NULL     — FK to company_research_targets if identified
is_hidden_client:       BOOLEAN         — already in parseHiddenClientContext()
location_hint:          TEXT | NULL     — already captured
```

### 4. Agency Notes Field

Each agency in `agency_posting_signals` (or `agency_partners`) needs a free-text `founder_notes` field for manual founder observations — relationship history, conversation notes, contact status. This is not automated.

### 5. Founder Approval Gate

No agency contact can be triggered by automation. A manual flag `contact_approved_by_founder` (boolean, default false) must be set before any outreach template is shown or any profile sharing workflow is initiated. The system logs when this flag changes and who changed it.

### 6. Partner-Specific Profile Delivery Workflow

When an agency partnership is active, a new workflow is needed:
- Founder selects agency and mandate brief
- System queries `ingested_drivers` for matching profiles (license, YKB, region, domain, availability)
- Founder reviews matching profiles
- Founder approves profiles for delivery
- System generates a structured PDF/export (no raw DB access given to agency)
- System logs the introduction: `candidate_id → agency_id → mandate_brief → date → profiles_shared`
- System tracks acceptance confirmation from agency (manual entry by founder)
- System tracks placement status (manual entry by founder)

### 7. Per-Mandate Exclusivity Tracking

The system must prevent accidentally sharing the same candidate for the same mandate type with two competing agencies. A simple table: `agency_introductions (candidate_id, agency_id, mandate_type, region, date_introduced, status)` — before sharing, system checks if candidate has an active introduction for a similar mandate.

### 8. No Standard Outreach Automation

Agency contacts must never appear in the standard `contact_suggested` / `outreach_email_queue` pipeline. If an agency name appears in those systems, it is a bug (already handled by `hold_agency` gate).

### 9. Separation in Admin Cockpit

The recruiter cockpit should show a separate "Agency Partners" section, distinct from "Company Needs" and "Drivers." This section shows:
- Agency list with partner_status
- Active mandates per partner
- Profile deliveries and acceptance status
