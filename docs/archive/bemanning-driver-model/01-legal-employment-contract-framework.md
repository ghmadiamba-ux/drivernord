## 1. The Legal Model: Who Does What

### 1.1 The Triangular Relationship

Swedish bemanning (staffing) operates as a **tri-party arrangement** governed primarily by:

- **Lag (2012:854) om uthyrning av arbetstagare** ("uthyrningslagen" / the Agency Work Act), which implements EU Directive 2008/104/EC on temporary agency work
- **Lag (1982:80) om anställningsskydd** (LAS — the Employment Protection Act)
- **Arbetsmiljölagen (AML)** — the Work Environment Act
- The applicable **collective agreement** (see Section 3)

The three parties are:

| Party | Swedish term | Role |
|---|---|---|
| **Staffing company** | Bemanningsföretag | Formal employer — employs and pays the driver |
| **Client company** | Kundföretag | Work direction — where the driver works |
| **Driver** | Arbetstagare / konsult | Employed by staffing company, works at client |

> **Source:** Lag (2012:854) om uthyrning av arbetstagare, §1 and §5 (definitions). [Riksdagen text](https://www.riksdagen.se/sv/dokument-och-lagar/dokument/svensk-forfattningssamling/lag-2012854-om-uthyrning-av-arbetstagare_sfs-2012-854/)

---

### 1.2 Who Is the Employer?

**The staffing company is the employer.** This is explicit in §5 of uthyrningslagen: the bemanningsföretag employs workers for the purpose of hiring them out to client companies.

The staffing company:
- Signs the employment contract with the driver
- Pays salary, employer contributions, and vacation pay
- Manages sick pay, parental leave, and other statutory entitlements
- Carries primary employer responsibility under AML 3:2 (see Section 5.5)
- Is bound by and responsible for compliance with the applicable collective agreement
- Issues P/tax reports (arbetsgivardeklaration) and pays employer contributions (arbetsgivaravgift) to Skatteverket

> **Source:** §5 uthyrningslagen; Skatteverket, [Ditt ansvar som arbetsgivare](https://skatteverket.se/foretag/arbetsgivare/arbetsgivarregistrering/dittansvarsomarbetsgivare.4.361dc8c15312eff6fd16ec2.html)

---

### 1.3 Who Directs Daily Work?

**The client company directs the driver's daily work.** Once a driver begins an assignment, the client company gives instructions about:

- Route, vehicle, load
- Daily start and end times
- Specific logistics requirements
- Workplace-level safety procedures

This is the defining characteristic of a staffing arrangement: work direction (arbetsledning) passes temporarily to the client company for the duration of the assignment.

> **Source:** §5 uthyrningslagen (definition of kundföretag as entity that directs the work of leased-out workers)

---

### 1.4 Who Invoices the Client?

**The staffing company invoices the client company.** The commercial contract is between the staffing company and the client, not between the driver and the client.

The invoice rate typically reflects:
- The driver's actual wage/hour
- All mandatory employer costs (see Section 5)
- The staffing company's margin

There is no publicly standardised margin percentage for driver staffing; industry practice varies. The staffing company and client negotiate a rate per hour or per assignment. Costs that the staffing company must cover are fixed (employer contributions ~31.42%, vacation accrual ~12%, sick pay risk, insurance) and form the floor under any viable rate.

> **Note:** No official source publishes a standard margin. This is a commercially negotiated matter. See Section 6 for practical cost floor.

---

### 1.5 What Responsibility Remains With the Client Company?

The client company is **not the employer** but carries specific statutory duties:

| Obligation | Legal basis |
|---|---|
| Workplace-level work environment | AML 3:12 — the client controls the worksite |
| Workplace introduction for the driver | AML + uthyrningslagen §11 |
| Inform driver of vacant permanent positions | Uthyrningslagen §12 |
| Equal access to facilities and amenities | Uthyrningslagen §11 |
| Offer permanent employment (or pay compensation) after 24 months | Uthyrningslagen §12a |
| Inform staffing company of working conditions for equal-treatment compliance | Uthyrningslagen §12c |
| Provide details for cross-border postings if applicable | Uthyrningslagen §12d |

> **Source:** Lag (2012:854) §§11–12d. [Lagen.nu](https://lagen.nu/2012:854); [Prevent.se on shared responsibility](https://www.prevent.se/bransch/tjanster/in--och-uthyrd-personal/)

## 2. Employment Contract Types

### 2.1 Overview

A staffing company may use different employment forms under LAS. There is **no special employment form for staffing** — normal LAS rules apply to the employment relationship between the staffing company and the driver. The assignment at the client company is not itself the employment; it is the deployment of an existing employment.

> **Source:** LAS (1982:80) general principles; [Lagen.nu LAS](https://lagen.nu/1982:80)

---

### 2.2 Tillsvidareanställning (Permanent / Open-Ended Employment)

The default form under LAS §4. The driver is permanently employed by the staffing company and deployed on sequential assignments. Between assignments, the driver receives **garantilön** (guarantee wage — the minimum wage paid when not assigned to a client). This is the most common model for established staffing companies with regular client demand.

**Guarantee wage under 2025–2027 Bemanningsavtalet (from 1 May 2025):** 133.29 SEK/hour.

> **Source:** LAS §4; Almega/Kompetensföretagen collective agreement; [Transportarbetaren, new staffing agreement](https://www.transportarbetaren.se/bemanning-har-nytt-avtal/); [Arbetet salary article 2025](https://arbetet.se/2025/04/30/lonerna-klara-for-anstallda-i-bemanningsforetag/)

---

### 2.3 Särskild Visstidsanställning — SÄVA (Fixed-Term Employment)

Under the 2022 LAS reform, **särskild visstidsanställning** (SÄVA) replaced the old "allmän visstidsanställning" (ALVA). Key rules:

- No requirement to justify the fixed term
- **Conversion to permanent employment:** if the driver has been employed in SÄVA at the same employer for more than 12 months in total over a 5-year period, the employment automatically converts to permanent employment (LAS §5a)
- Stack: SÄVA, vikariat, and seasonal work can combine to trigger conversion

**Practical implication for DriverNord:** Using SÄVA allows flexible staffing initially, but conversion after 12 months means every driver who continues past a year must be offered permanent employment or their employment ends at expiry. This adds administrative management.

> **Source:** LAS (1982:80) §§5–5a, as amended 2022. [Riksdagen LAS text](https://www.riksdagen.se/sv/dokument-och-lagar/dokument/svensk-forfattningssamling/lag-198280-om-anstallningsskydd_sfs-1982-80/)

---

### 2.4 Provanställning (Probationary Employment)

Allowed under LAS §6 for up to **6 months**. Either party may terminate during or at the end of the probationary period without grounds (though the employer must give at least 2 weeks' notice of termination under most collective agreements). After 6 months, automatically converts to permanent employment unless either party has terminated.

Used by staffing companies to assess a new driver before offering full permanent employment. A driver may be deployed to clients during their probationary period.

> **Source:** LAS (1982:80) §6

---

### 2.5 Intermittent / On-Demand Employment ("Vid behovs-anställning")

"Intermittent anställning" or "vid behov-anställning" is **not a separate LAS employment form** — it is a practical arrangement where the driver is offered individual engagements and only has an employment when actually working. In practice, it often sits on a SÄVA, vikariat, or tillsvidareanställning foundation.

**Key risk:** Courts and Arbetsdomstolen (AD) have repeatedly found that if a pattern of repeated call-ins becomes sufficiently regular, the arrangement may be re-classified as a permanent employment regardless of what the contract says. A driver who is repeatedly called in over several months may acquire full LAS protections.

**Legal requirement:** Even a "vid behov" arrangement requires the employer to give written information about working conditions under LAS §6c (see 2.7 below).

> **Source:** AD case law general principle; [Juridik.blog employment forms 2025](https://www.juridik.blog/typer-av-anstallningsavtal/)

---

### 2.6 Vikariat (Substitute Employment)

Allowed without time limit when replacing a named absent employee (e.g. parental leave, sick leave). Converts to permanent employment under the same stacking rules as SÄVA.

---

### 2.7 Written Agreement and Written Information Requirement

**Strictly: Swedish law does not require a written employment contract.** An employment can be concluded orally. However, LAS **§6c** requires the employer to provide **written information about employment conditions** within prescribed deadlines:

- **Within 7 calendar days** of the employee's first day of work: employer identity, workplace address, job title or description, start date, duration (for fixed-term), base salary and pay period, working hours, and entitlement to paid leave (holidays/vacation)
- **Within 1 month** of start: information about pension, insurance, probationary period terms if applicable, notice periods

**Practical implication:** A written contract is strongly recommended and standard practice — it constitutes the required information and protects both parties. Verbal agreements are technically valid but create proof problems. All professional staffing operations use written contracts.

> **Source:** LAS (1982:80) §6c; [Arbetsgivarverket on employment terms directive](https://www.arbetsgivarverket.se/arbetsgivarguiden/arbetsgivarverket-informerar/2022/det-har-innebar-arbetsvillkorsdirektivet-for-statliga-arbetsgivare/)

---

### 2.8 Conversion Rules Summary

| Form | Converts to permanent when |
|---|---|
| SÄVA | 12 months in 5 years at same employer |
| Vikariat | Same stacking rules as SÄVA |
| Provanställning | Automatically after 6 months (unless terminated) |
| Tillsvidareanställning | Already permanent |

## 3. Collective Agreements

### 3.1 Which Collective Agreement Applies to Staffing-Employed Drivers?

This is a critical question. A driver employed by a staffing company does **not** fall under the transport sector's collective agreement (Transportavtalet/Biltrafikavtalet). The staffing company's collective agreement applies.

**Verified:** Swedish Transport union (Transportarbetareförbundet) negotiated the staffing sector's **Bemanningsavtalet** with Kompetensföretagen (under Almega) alongside all 13 other LO-affiliated unions. This agreement specifically covers drivers and other blue-collar workers employed at staffing companies — **even when the assignment is in the transport sector**.

The Transportavtalet (negotiated between Transportarbetareförbundet and Biltrafikens Arbetsgivareförbund / Transportföretagen) applies to **transport companies employing their own drivers** — not to drivers who are formally employed by a staffing company.

> **Verified fact.** Source: [Transportarbetaren — Bemanning har nytt avtal](https://www.transportarbetaren.se/bemanning-har-nytt-avtal/) (Swedish Transport union newsletter): "For the employed, this is a good agreement" — referring to Transportarbetareförbundet's role as signatory to the staffing agreement.

> Additional confirmation: Transportarbetaren writes that drivers employed by staffing companies are covered by the bemanningsavtalet, and wage increases above the agreement minimum follow the client company's applicable agreement.

---

### 3.2 The Bemanningsavtalet (LO — Kompetensföretagen)

**Current agreement:** 1 May 2025 – 30 April 2027

- **Employer side:** Kompetensföretagen (member organization under Almega)
- **Worker side:** All 14 LO-affiliated unions, including Transportarbetareförbundet (Transport)
- **Scope:** Approximately 35,000 blue-collar workers (årsanställda) at approximately 700 companies
- **Total cost increase:** 6.4% over 2 years

**Wage provisions:**
- Year 1 (from 1 May 2025): guarantee wage +4.38 SEK/hour → guarantee wage ~133.29 SEK/hour
- Year 2 (from 1 May 2026): +4.00 SEK/hour → guarantee wage ~137.29 SEK/hour

**Guarantee wage structure:**
- Drivers who are not assigned to a client company but remain available for deployment receive the **garantilön**
- After 18 months' employment: 90% of full pay (150 hours/month equivalent) when not booked
- Before 18 months' employment: 80% of full pay (133 hours/month equivalent)

**Equal treatment principle (likabehandlingsprincipen):** When a driver is deployed to a client where the going rate (e.g. under the Transportavtalet) is higher than the staffing agreement minimum, the driver's wage is governed by the **client's applicable collective agreement rates**. This ensures drivers are not underpaid relative to directly-employed counterparts at the same client.

> **Source:** [Almega: Nytt kollektivavtal 2025](https://www.almega.se/2025/04/nytt-kollektivavtal-for-bemanning-tecknat-mellan-kompetensforetagen-och-samtliga-lo-forbund/); [LO press release](https://www.lo.se/pressmeddelanden/lo-tecknar-nytt-kollektivavtal-for-bemanningsbranschen); [Transportarbetaren article](https://www.transportarbetaren.se/bemanning-har-nytt-avtal/)

---

### 3.3 The Equal Treatment Principle (Likabehandlingsprincipen)

Under **uthyrningslagen §6**, the staffing company must ensure the deployed driver receives **at least the same basic working and employment conditions** that would apply if the driver had been employed directly by the client company for the same work.

"Basic working and employment conditions" covers:
- Working hours, overtime rules, rest periods
- Night work entitlements
- Breaks and rest
- Vacation and holidays
- **Wages** (this is the most commercially significant element)
- Anti-discrimination protections

**The staffing company is responsible** for ensuring equal treatment compliance. In practice, this means the staffing company must ask each client what working conditions apply to equivalent directly-employed workers and calibrate the driver's pay accordingly.

> **Source:** Uthyrningslagen §§5–8; [Kompetensföretagen on directive](https://www.kompetensforetagen.se/2021/12/bemanningsdirektivet-och-uthyrningslagen/); [Riksdagen committee on equal treatment](https://www.riksdagen.se/sv/dokument-och-lagar/dokument/betankande/lag-om-uthyrning-av-arbetstagare-m-m_h001au5/)

**Exception:** The equal treatment obligation for wages does not apply to workers who are **permanently employed** and continue to receive pay (garantilön) between assignments (§8 uthyrningslagen). This is the most important structural incentive for staffing companies to use permanent contracts rather than SÄVA.

## 4. The Agency Work Act: 24-Month Rule

### 4.1 The Rule

Under **uthyrningslagen §12a** (added by amendment effective 1 October 2022), when a driver has been deployed to the **same client company at the same operating unit** for more than **24 months within a rolling 36-month period**, the **client company** must, within 1 month of the threshold being exceeded:

**Option A:** Offer the driver **permanent employment** at the client company, OR  
**Option B:** Pay the driver **compensation equivalent to 2 months' salary**

If a collective agreement between Kompetensföretagen and an LO union applies: compensation is **3 months' salary** (the bemanningsavtalet may set a higher floor).

> **Source:** Uthyrningslagen §12a; [L&E Global analysis 2024](https://leglobal.law/2024/09/25/sweden-the-24-month-rule-in-the-agency-work-act-will-now-become-effective-in-practice/); [Kompetensföretagen 24-month guide](https://www.kompetensforetagen.se/24-manadersregeln/)

---

### 4.2 How Time Is Calculated

- The 36-month window is **rolling** — at any moment, look back 36 months
- **Continuous deployment:** Count calendar months; absences of up to 35 consecutive calendar days count as if present
- **Intermittent assignments:** Only actual working days count toward the 24-month total
- The rule is per **client × operating unit** combination. Moving to a different site of the same client resets the clock (though not confirmed in all court interpretations — verify)

---

### 4.3 What Happens When the Threshold Is Reached

**If the client offers permanent employment and the driver accepts:**
- Driver's employment with the staffing company ends without notice
- Driver becomes a direct employee of the client company
- Client pays the staffing company no termination fee (unless contracted)

**If the driver declines the offer:**
- Client company has fulfilled its obligation under §12a
- Driver cannot claim this right again
- Staffing arrangement may continue indefinitely — the clock does not reset (the driver simply can no longer exercise the right for that client relationship)

**If the client pays 2/3 months' compensation instead:**
- The staffing arrangement continues
- The driver remains employed by the staffing company

**If the client does neither:**
- The client faces **financial and general damages** claims from the driver
- The driver has **4 months** from when the obligation arose to file a claim in court
- After 4 months, the claim is time-barred

> **Source:** Uthyrningslagen §§12a–12d, §§13–14; [Kompetensföretagen 24-month guide](https://www.kompetensforetagen.se/24-manadersregeln/); [Fill.work analysis 2024](https://www.fill.work/post/navigating-the-new-swedish-law-on-temporary-agency-workers-what-it-means-for-your-business-in-2024)

---

### 4.4 Practical Implication for DriverNord

The 24-month rule is a **client-side obligation**, not a staffing company obligation. However, the rule has direct commercial implications for DriverNord:

- A client transport company that has used the same DriverNord driver for 24 of the past 36 months faces a forced decision point
- If the client offers permanent employment, DriverNord loses the driver and the recurring revenue from that client-driver pair
- If the client pays 3 months' compensation (under the LO bemanningsavtalet), the cost is the **client's** — not DriverNord's
- **For DriverNord:** The practical risk is **client churn** and loss of a placed driver, not direct financial liability — but drivers who have been with one client for nearly 2 years are likely the most settled and productive ones

**Mitigation strategies (for evaluation):**
- Rotate drivers across multiple client companies to prevent any single client reaching 24 months
- Track time per client per driver actively from day one — build this into operations from launch
- Advise clients explicitly about the 24-month trigger when signing framework agreements

> ⚠️ **Uncertain:** Whether the rule can be modified or waived by the collective agreement is disputed in legal commentary. Verify with a labour lawyer before structuring long-duration placements.

## 5. Employer Obligations

### 5.1 Employer Contributions (Arbetsgivaravgifter)

The staffing company pays **full employer contributions of 31.42%** on all gross wages, including:
- Social security (ålderspension, sjukförsäkring, föräldraförsäkring, arbetslöshetsförsäkring, etc.)
- Arbetsgivaravgiften is declared and paid monthly via Skatteverket

**Reduced rates:** Employer contributions are reduced for workers over 66 years (10.21%) and those under 18 (10.21% on wages up to a threshold). These reduced rates are generally not applicable in standard driver staffing.

> **Source:** Skatteverket, [Employer Contributions (English)](https://www.skatteverket.se/servicelankar/otherlanguages/englishengelska/businessesandemployers/startingandrunningaswedishbusiness/declaringtaxesbusinesses/filingapayereturn/employercontributions.4.2fb39afe18dabf1e4d24a3d.html); [PwC blog on arbetsgivaravgifter](https://blogg.pwc.se/foretagarbloggen/arbetsgivaravgifter)

---

### 5.2 Vacation Pay (Semesterersättning)

Under **Semesterlagen (1977:480)**, employees are entitled to **25 days' paid vacation per year** (5 weeks). The employer must accrue and pay vacation pay at a minimum of **12% of qualifying wages** (may be higher under collective agreement).

For intermittently or variably-employed drivers, vacation pay is typically paid as an ongoing percentage addition to each pay period rather than as a block payment in summer.

---

### 5.3 Sick Pay (Sjuklön)

Under **Lag (1991:1047) om sjuklön (sjuklönelagen):**

- **Days 1–14:** The staffing company (as employer) pays sick pay at **80% of the driver's regular wage**, minus a **karensavdrag** (waiting-day deduction) equivalent to 20% of the average weekly sick pay
- **From day 15 onwards:** Försäkringskassan pays sjukpenning (state sickness benefit)
- The staffing company **cannot pass the sick-pay cost to the client** — this is the employer's risk

**Implication for DriverNord:** Sick-leave risk for deployed drivers sits entirely with DriverNord. If a driver is sick during an assignment at a client, DriverNord:
- Pays sick pay (days 1–14)
- Has no fee income from the client for those days (unless the commercial contract specifies otherwise)
- Must find a replacement driver if the client needs coverage

**If the driver has been sick 10+ times in 12 months:** The employer may apply for Försäkringskassan's high-risk protection (högriskskydd) to have the state reimburse some sick pay costs.

> **Source:** Lag (1991:1047) om sjuklön; [Försäkringskassan employer sick pay page](https://www.forsakringskassan.se/arbetsgivare/sjukdom-och-skada/sjuklon); [Fortnox sick pay guide](https://www.fortnox.se/fortnox-foretagsguide/driva-foretag/lon/sjuklon-och-sjukfranvaro-sa-raknar-du-ut-sjuklon)

---

### 5.4 Collective Agreement Insurance and Pension (if Bemanningsavtalet applies)

If DriverNord is bound by the Bemanningsavtalet (Kompetensföretagen–LO), the following Fora-administered products apply for all blue-collar (LO-category) drivers:

| Product | What it covers | Administered by |
|---|---|---|
| **Avtalspension SAF-LO** | Occupational pension (~4.5% of gross pay) | Fora |
| **AGS** (Avtalsgruppsjukförsäkring) | Supplementary sickness income from day 15 | Fora / AFA |
| **TFA** (Trygghetsförsäkring vid arbetsskada) | Work injury compensation | AFA Försäkring |
| **TGL** (Tjänstegrupplivförsäkring) | Death benefit to survivors | AFA Försäkring |
| **FPT** (Föräldrapenningtillägg) | Supplement to parental leave | Fora |
| **Premiebefrielse** | Continues pension contributions during sick/parental leave | Fora |
| **AGB** (Avgångsbidrag) | Severance for redundancy (lack of work) | AFA Försäkring |
| **Trygghetsfonden TSL** | Job transition support | TSL |

These are paid via the collective agreement fee structure when the staffing company registers with Fora. **If DriverNord has no collective agreement, none of these supplementary protections apply**, and drivers have only statutory protections.

> **Source:** [Fora — insurance and pension package](https://www.fora.se/foretag/forsakringsavtalet-med-fora/tjanstepension-och-forsakringar-och-ingar/); [Fora — SAF-LO pension](https://www.fora.se/privat/forsta-din-pension/sa-fungerar-avtalspension-saf-lo/)

### 5.5 Work Environment Responsibility (Arbetsmiljö)

The work environment responsibility is **divided and shared**, not exclusively one party's:

**Staffing company (DriverNord as employer) — primary obligations under AML 3:2:**
- Ensure drivers are equipped, trained, and fit for the tasks
- Conduct (or verify) risk assessments for each assignment type before deployment
- Provide introduction information about the client's workplace hazards
- Remain available for drivers to contact about health and safety concerns
- Report serious work injuries (allvarliga olyckor) to Arbetsmiljöverket
- Maintain systematic work environment management (SAM) per AFS 2001:1

**Client company — obligations under AML 3:12 (worksite control principle):**
- Provide a safe worksite and safe vehicles/equipment
- Give workplace-specific introduction to the driver on the first day
- Include the driver in all on-site safety procedures and emergency plans
- Assign a named contact person for the driver
- Conduct risk assessments for the actual work tasks
- Ensure the driver has the same access to safety equipment and facilities as permanent employees

**The responsibility cannot be contracted away.** The staffing company and client company cannot agree that only one of them bears AML responsibility — the split follows the law regardless of what the service contract says.

> **Source:** Arbetsmiljölagen (AML) 3:2 (employer's basic duty) and 3:12 (worksite owner's duty); [Prevent.se — in- och uthyrd personal](https://www.prevent.se/bransch/tjanster/in--och-uthyrd-personal/); [Kompetensföretagen — Arbetsmiljö för konsulter](https://www.kompetensforetagen.se/arbetsmiljo/); LU student paper: [Arbetsmiljöansvar i bemanningsbranschen](https://lup.lub.lu.se/student-papers/search/publication/9224303)

### 5.6 Summary: Cost of Employing One Driver

The following table illustrates the **minimum statutory cost floor** for the staffing company per 100 SEK of gross wage:

| Cost component | Rate | Per 100 SEK gross wage |
|---|---|---|
| Gross wage | — | 100.00 |
| Employer contributions (arbetsgivaravgift) | 31.42% | 31.42 |
| Vacation pay accrual | 12% | 12.00 |
| Sick pay risk (statistical) | ~2–3% est. | 2–3 |
| Fora/AFA insurance (if collective agreement) | ~2–4% est. | 2–4 |
| **Minimum total cost to employer** | | **~145–150 SEK** |

This means the invoiced hourly rate to the client must be at minimum ~1.45–1.50× the driver's gross hourly rate, **before any margin**. A typical staffing company charges 1.6–2.0× the driver's cost to the client, but this is a commercial decision not regulated by law.

> **Note:** The Fora/AFA rate is approximate — the exact rate depends on the agreement version and is set in the collective agreement. No public per-percentage figure has been independently verified for 2025. Verify with Fora or Almega.

## 6. Practical Model: How the Branch Works

### 6.1 Recruitment

1. DriverNord recruits drivers with valid licenses (C, CE, D) and YKB (yrkeskompetensbevis) from the open market
2. License and YKB validity is verified against Transportstyrelsen register
3. A background check may be conducted (note: GDPR and credit check rules apply)
4. Employment contract is signed (usually tillsvidareanställning or SÄVA)
5. Written employment information is provided within 7 days (LAS §6c requirement)
6. Driver is registered with Skatteverket (for employer contributions), Fora (if applicable), and internal payroll system

### 6.2 Keeping Drivers Available

- Permanently employed drivers are on the staffing company's roster
- When not assigned, they receive **garantilön** (133.29 SEK/hour from May 2025) for being available
- The driver is expected to accept reasonable assignments within their skill set and geography
- Intermittently-employed drivers (SÄVA/on-call) are typically only paid when working

> **Risk for DriverNord:** Guaranteeing pay between assignments is the main cost of the permanent model. The commercial pressure is to keep utilisation high (typically >80% billable hours to break even on salary commitments).

### 6.3 Assignment and Deployment

1. Client contacts DriverNord with a staffing need (often same-day / next-morning)
2. DriverNord matches from available drivers (license category, vehicle type, geography, availability)
3. Assignment brief is shared with the driver: client name, vehicle, depot, start time, expected duration
4. Driver reports to the client's site at the agreed time
5. Client's dispatcher or transport manager gives operational instructions for the day

> **Note for DriverNord:** The matching and deployment speed is the core operational challenge. Clients expecting replacement coverage often need confirmation within hours.

### 6.4 Timesheets and Approval

1. Driver records hours worked (most operators use a digital app or paper timesheet countersigned by the client's contact)
2. Client approves the timesheet (weekly is standard)
3. Approved timesheets flow to DriverNord payroll (for salary calculation) and invoicing (for client billing)
4. Disputed hours are resolved between DriverNord and the client — the driver is not party to this commercial dispute but is paid the recorded hours

### 6.5 Payroll

- Drivers are paid monthly (the norm) or bi-weekly
- Pay includes: base wage for worked hours + guarantilön for available-but-unassigned hours + any overtime + vacation pay accrual
- Tax withholding (preliminärskatt) and employer contributions are remitted to Skatteverket monthly via arbetsgivardeklaration

### 6.6 Invoicing the Client

- DriverNord invoices the client based on approved hours × agreed rate
- Invoice terms: typically 30 days net (branschstandard)
- If overtime or unsocial hours were worked, the invoice rate may be higher (depends on commercial contract)
- VAT is added at 25% standard rate

### 6.7 Managing Sick Leave and No-Shows

The **sick leave risk profile** is the most commercially sensitive element of driver staffing:

- **If a driver calls in sick:** DriverNord pays sick pay (days 1–14), has no billable output, and must find a replacement from roster
- **Replacement SLA:** Professional transport staffing companies offer a guarantee to find a replacement within a defined window (e.g. 2 hours' notice for same-day). This is a competitive differentiator
- **No-show without notice:** DriverNord carries the reputational and contractual risk to the client
- **Sick pay cost modeling:** At 4–5% annual sick absence (Swedish average for manual workers), a staffing company of 50 drivers should budget ~100 sick days/year. At 133 SEK/hour × 8 hours × 100 days = ~106,400 SEK/year in sick pay with no revenue offset

> **Source on Swedish sick absence rates:** Försäkringskassan publishes annual sick absence statistics; Swedish construction/transport average sick absence ~4–6% (industry estimate, not independently cited here).

## 8. Implications for DriverNord

DriverNord is evaluating whether to operate as a staffing company (recruiting, employing, and renting out drivers). The following summary is based on the research above:

### 8.1 What the Model Requires That DriverNord Does Not Yet Have

1. **Employer infrastructure:** Payroll system, tax registration as employer (Skatteverket F-tax + employer registration), sick pay management, vacation pay accrual
2. **Employment contracts:** Standard written contracts for each employment form (tillsvidareanställning template, SÄVA template, provanställning clause)
3. **Collective agreement or equivalent:** Either Kompetensföretagen membership + bemanningsavtalet, or a standalone agreement with a union, or a documented decision to operate without (with legal risk assessment)
4. **Fora / AFA registration** (if collective agreement): To activate driver insurance and pension
5. **Work environment procedures:** SAM (systematic work environment management) documentation; client onboarding checklist per AML 3:12
6. **24-month tracking system:** Per-driver, per-client, per-site time tracking from first assignment
7. **Client framework agreement:** Standard terms covering rate, approval, liability, work environment responsibilities (cannot simply pass all AML responsibility to client, but should document client's worksite obligations)

### 8.2 Structural Risk Factors

- **Sick leave cash-flow risk** is the highest operational risk in the model. DriverNord pays sick pay with no billable offset for 14 days per incident. Volume smooths this but early stage is vulnerable.
- **24-month rule** creates forced churn at 2 years for any stable long-term placement. Clients must be briefed, and DriverNord should offer to manage the tracking on their behalf.
- **Equal-treatment wage requirement** adds administrative complexity — DriverNord must understand each client's internal rates for equivalent drivers, which requires ongoing client dialogue.
- **Garantilön cost** is the cost of driver availability. If demand is lumpy (seasonal transport), guaranteeing pay during quiet periods creates a loss. Early operations should calibrate permanent headcount carefully.

### 8.3 What Is Well-Established and Manageable

- The legal framework (uthyrningslagen, LAS, AML) is mature and well-interpreted — there is extensive guidance from courts, employer bodies, and unions
- The tri-party model is understood by Swedish transport companies as standard practice — no need to educate clients on what bemanning means
- The bemanningsavtalet is the correct starting point for employment terms — it exists precisely for this model
- Kompetensföretagen auktorisation, while requiring 12 months and a collective agreement, provides significant commercial credibility with clients who require auktoriserade suppliers
